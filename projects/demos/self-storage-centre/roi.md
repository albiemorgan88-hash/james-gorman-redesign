# ROI Summary — AI Assistant for The Self Storage Centre

## The Problem
Most people search for storage **evenings and weekends** — exactly when nobody's answering the phone. Every unanswered enquiry is a potential customer lost to a competitor who replied first.

## The Numbers
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average storage customer value | ~£100/month |
| Average customer lifetime | 6-12 months |
| Lifetime value per customer | £600–£1,200 |
| AI management cost | £250/month |
| **Extra conversions needed to break even** | **Less than 1 per month** |

## What Changes
- **24/7 instant responses** — no more missed enquiries at 8pm or Sunday morning
- **Handles repetitive questions** — hours, locations, unit sizes, pricing framework, box shop
- **Frees up staff time** — less time on phone/email answering the same 10 questions
- **Captures leads** — collects contact details and enquiry specifics before office hours

## Conservative Estimate
If the AI converts just **2 extra customers per month** at £100/month average:
- Monthly revenue gain: **£200/month recurring**
- Annual revenue gain: **£2,400+ per year** (compounding as customers stay)
- AI cost: **£250/month**
- **Net ROI positive within the first month**

The real upside: customers who would have gone to a competitor at 9pm on a Tuesday now get an instant, helpful reply — and book with you instead.
