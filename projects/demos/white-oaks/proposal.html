White Oaks Connect — Aftercare App
Technical Overview & Cost Estimate
1. Overview

A mobile application to support residents after their 30-day programme. Available on iPhone and Android platforms. The app provides a private space for check-ins, peer support, and connection with counsellors. Data remains confidential and is stored securely within European Union jurisdiction.

2. What the App Does
  • Daily mood check-in
  • Peer support forum (moderated)
  • Gratitude journaling
  • Local meeting finder (AA/NA/GA)
  • Emergency contact buttons
  • Milestone tracking
  • Coping resources
  • Counsellor dashboard with alerts
3. Technical Matters
  • Data storage: All data stored in the EU (Supabase, EU region). GDPR compliant.
  • Privacy: Resident data is encrypted at rest and in transit. No data shared with third parties. No advertising. No tracking.
  • Access control: Residents only see their own data plus shared forum. Counsellors see anonymised trends and can view individual check-ins for residents in their care.
  • Authentication: Secure login with email/password. Optional two-factor authentication.
  • Hosting: Cloud-hosted on European servers. 99.9% uptime target.
  • App stores: Submitted to Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Updates managed centrally.
  • Data retention: Configurable. Suggest 12 months after last activity, then anonymised for aggregate reporting.
  • Backup: Daily automated backups with 30-day retention.
