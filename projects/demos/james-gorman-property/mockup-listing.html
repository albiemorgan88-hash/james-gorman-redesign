Property Description

A beautifully presented three-bedroom semi-detached family home situated in the highly sought-after Culmore area of Derry. This property has been lovingly maintained by its current owners and offers bright, spacious accommodation throughout.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming entrance hall, generous living room with feature fireplace, a separate dining room perfect for family meals, and a modern fitted kitchen with integrated appliances and access to the rear garden.

Upstairs, three well-proportioned bedrooms are served by a contemporary family bathroom with white suite and tiled walls. The master bedroom enjoys pleasant views over the surrounding area.

Externally, the property benefits from a low-maintenance front garden, driveway providing off-street parking, and an enclosed rear garden with patio area — ideal for summer entertaining. The property is conveniently located within walking distance of local schools, shops, and public transport links, with easy access to the Culmore Road and city centre.
