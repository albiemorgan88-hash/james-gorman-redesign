🏙 About Derry

Derry-Londonderry is Northern Ireland's second-largest city and one of the most exciting places to live on the island of Ireland. With a rich cultural heritage, stunning natural scenery along the River Foyle, and a rapidly growing economy, it's no wonder more people are choosing to call Derry home.

The city has undergone a remarkable transformation in recent years, with new developments like Merchants View on the Strand Road bringing contemporary living to the heart of the city. House prices remain significantly more affordable than Belfast or Dublin, making it an excellent investment opportunity.

🏆 UK City of Culture 2013

Derry's cultural renaissance continues with world-class festivals, a thriving food scene, and a creative arts community that punches well above its weight.
