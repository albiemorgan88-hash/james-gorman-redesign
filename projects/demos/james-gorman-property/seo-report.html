What We Found

Critical issues holding your site back

  • 🚨
    Terrible URL Structure (Wix Default)
    Your pages are named /blank, /blank-3, /blank-5, /blank-10. Google uses URLs to understand content. These tell Google absolutely nothing about what's on each page.
  • 🚨
    6 H1 Tags on Homepage
    Your homepage has 6 H1 headings including "MENU", "CONTACT US" and "SOCIAL MEDIA". Google expects ONE clear H1 per page. This confuses search engines about your main topic.
  • 🚨
    Weak Page Title
    Current title: "James Gorman Property | property | Derry, Londonderry, UK" — doesn't include "estate agent" or "letting agent". This is the single most important ranking signal.
  • 🚨
    Default Wix Social Links
    Your header links point to instagram.com/wix and facebook.com/WixStudio instead of your actual social profiles. Looks unprofessional and wastes link equity.
  • ⚠️
    Mobile Speed Score: 55/100
    Over 60% of property searches happen on mobile. Google penalises slow mobile sites. Your competitors load faster.
  • ⚠️
    No Blog or Content
    Zero blog posts = zero chances to rank for long-tail keywords like "best areas to live in derry" or "derry property market 2026". Your competitors publish regularly.
  • ⚠️
    No Google Business Profile Optimisation
    Your GBP may exist but isn't working hard enough. With 75 Trustpilot reviews, you should be dominating the local map pack — you're not appearing.
  • ⚠️
    No Area-Specific Pages
    Your competitors have pages for "property for sale in [area]" for every BT postcode. You serve BT47, BT48, BT49, BT51, BT55, BT56, BT82 — but have zero location pages.
