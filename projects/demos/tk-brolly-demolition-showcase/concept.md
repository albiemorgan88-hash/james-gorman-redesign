# TK Brolly Demolition — Mini Showcase Concept

## Purpose
A small high-end showcase piece for TK Brolly, similar to the Gavan mockup lane. This is not a full website build. It is a premium direction piece that shows how the brand could feel with stronger authority, safety, project proof, and visual structure.

## Positioning
**Complex demolition. Controlled delivery.**

TK Brolly should feel like a serious demolition and enabling-works partner across the UK and Ireland, not a dated brochure site.

## Core idea
BSG-style clarity, but more demolition-specific:
- more authority
- more safety and process
- more project proof
- less generic service-list copy

## 4-screen mini showcase flow
1. Hero / authority opener
2. Specialist capabilities
3. Featured project structure
4. Safety process + CTA

## Verified proof we can safely use
- Founded 1968 / 50+ years experience
- UK & Ireland coverage
- Structural alterations
- Façade retention
- Hydro demolition / high pressure water cleaning
- Recycling / waste management
- Safe-T-Cert mention
- Modern fleet / haulage / experienced personnel

## What the mockup is trying to sell
- stronger first impression
- premium contractor feel
- better trust and credibility
- clearer path from homepage to enquiry
- more serious positioning for larger jobs

## Next recommended step after this mockup
Turn the direction into:
- homepage
- projects / case studies
- service pages
- safety + accreditations page
- stronger tender / enquiry CTA
