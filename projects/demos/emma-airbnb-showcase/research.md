# Emma's Airbnb Business - Research Summary

## Business Model Analysis
**Emma** - Serviced accommodation property investor in Derry/Londonderry, Northern Ireland
- **Scale:** Multiple 1/2 bed properties, potentially 5+ units
- **Business Model:** Guaranteed rent to property owners (1-3 year contracts)
- **Current AI:** Minimal (basic hosting app functionality)

## Derry/Londonderry Market Analysis

### Current Market Rates (Airbnb Data)
- **Price Range:** £15-20+ per night base rates
- **Top Properties:** 4.89-4.99 star ratings with 174-502 reviews
- **Popular Areas:** Waterside, City Centre, River Foyle frontage
- **Key Features:** Modern refurbished, high standard finish, prime locations

### Major Tourism Events (Seasonal Pricing Opportunities)
1. **Foyle Maritime Festival:** June 25-28, 2026 (bi-annual nautical celebration)
2. **Derry Halloween:** October 28-31, 2026 (Europe's largest Halloween festival)
3. **Regular Events:** City tours, cultural events, business tourism

### Competition Analysis
**Established Properties:**
- Ebrington Mews Apartment (4.96★, 394 reviews) - Ground floor, Peace Bridge access
- Number 28 Apartment 2 (4.99★, 174 reviews) - Prime city centre location
- Penthouse on the Foyle (4.89★, 190 reviews) - River views, balcony access

### Regulatory Environment (NI)
- **Planning Permission:** May be required for change of use
- **Building Control:** Compliance needed even without structural changes
- **Private Tenancies Act 2022:** New regulations phasing in through 2024
- **Tourism NI Guidelines:** Accommodation specific requirements

## Property Investment Market Context
- **Guaranteed Rent Schemes:** Common in Ireland/NI property investment
- **Typical Terms:** 3+ year contracts with fixed monthly payments
- **Value Add:** Comprehensive maintenance, refurbishment, tenant management
- **ROI Focus:** £2k-5k annual increase per property, 85%+ occupancy targets

## AI Automation Opportunities
1. **Guest Communication:** 24/7 chatbot, check-in/out instructions
2. **Dynamic Pricing:** Seasonal adjustments, competitor analysis
3. **Property Management:** Maintenance, cleaning schedules, inventory
4. **Business Scaling:** Lead generation, owner outreach automation
5. **Portfolio Management:** Multi-property dashboard and control

## Key Pain Points Identified
- Manual guest communication (time consuming for multiple properties)
- Static pricing missing seasonal peaks
- Property acquisition pipeline needs automation
- Maintenance request handling interrupts Emma's time
- No centralized portfolio oversight
