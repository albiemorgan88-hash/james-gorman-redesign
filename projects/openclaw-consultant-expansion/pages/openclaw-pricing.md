### How does this compare?

**vs DIY setup:** You'll spend 20–40 hours learning, troubleshooting, and probably getting the security wrong. At any reasonable hourly rate, that's more than £1,500 — and you won't have the same result.

**vs other consultants:** Most AI consultants charge by the hour (£150–£300/hr) or require expensive discovery calls before quoting. A typical implementation runs £3,000–£10,000. We've standardised the process so we can offer a flat rate.

**vs hiring a developer:** A freelance developer might charge less per hour, but they won't know OpenClaw's architecture, security model, or optimisation patterns. You're paying for expertise, not just time.

**vs doing nothing:** The real cost is the hours your team spends on repetitive work that an [AI employee](/ai-employee) could handle. For most businesses, the ROI payback period is under 2 months.

### Frequently asked questions about pricing

**Do I need to buy hardware?**  
If you don't already have a Mac Mini or VPS, we'll advise on the best option. A Mac Mini M4 costs around £500 and is the ideal platform for OpenClaw. VPS hosting starts from £20/month.

**Are there ongoing costs?**  
OpenClaw is open source — no licensing fees. Your only ongoing costs are hosting (if using VPS), API usage for the AI models (typically £20–£100/month depending on usage), and optional managed support.

**What if I need more than 5 integrations?**  
Additional integrations are £200 each. Most businesses need 3–5 to start.

**Can I upgrade to managed support later?**  
Yes. Many clients start with the standard package and add managed support after a few months as they scale their AI usage.

**What's your refund policy?**  
If we can't deliver what we've agreed, you don't pay. Simple as that.
