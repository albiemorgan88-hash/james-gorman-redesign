Your next hire doesn't need a desk

An AI employee isn't a chatbot that answers FAQs. It's a digital team member that works across your business — handling emails, managing schedules, researching competitors, responding to customers on WhatsApp, drafting documents, and running workflows. All day, every day, without breaks.

Built on OpenClaw, an open-source AI assistant platform, your AI employee connects to the tools you already use and gets work done autonomously. It reasons through problems, remembers context from previous conversations, and improves over time.

What can an AI employee actually do?

💬 Customer communication

Your AI employee responds to enquiries on Telegram, WhatsApp, email, and Slack within seconds. Not canned responses — genuine, contextual replies that understand your business, your tone, and your customers' needs.

🔍 Research and analysis

Need competitor analysis? Market research? A summary of 50 documents? Your AI employee handles it in minutes, not hours. It searches the web, analyses data, and delivers structured reports.

📋 Admin and operations

Scheduling, data entry, CRM updates, invoice processing, document drafting — the repetitive work that drains your team's time and energy. Your AI employee handles it all without complaint.

⚡ Workflow automation

When a new lead comes in, your AI employee can qualify them, add them to your CRM, send a personalised follow-up, and book a meeting in your calendar. Automatically. Every time.

✍️ Content and communications

Draft blog posts, social media updates, client reports, internal memos. Your AI employee writes in your voice, following your guidelines, ready for a quick review and publish.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a tool you go to. An AI employee comes to you. It's always on, connected to your business systems, and proactively handles work without being asked. It has persistent memory — it remembers your clients, your preferences, your processes. It operates across multiple channels simultaneously and executes real actions, not just generates text.

Think of it this way: ChatGPT is a search engine with a chat interface. An AI employee is a team member with genuine intelligence.
