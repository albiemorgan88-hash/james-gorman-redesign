### Their Positioning
- Personal brand-led ("I'm Jess Mason, you know me from YouTube")
- US-based (no UK locality signals)
- No pricing on page — uses discovery call model
- Targets: agencies, SaaS, real estate, property managers, service businesses
- Offers: discovery → roadmap/prompts → full setup → training/support

### Their Weaknesses
- No UK focus — openclawconsultant.co.uk wins for UK searches
- No transparent pricing — PJ's £1,500 flat rate is a differentiator
- No blog/content — same problem we have
- Single page — whoever builds content first wins SEO
- YouTube-dependent for traffic, not organic search
- No schema markup visible
- US-oriented language and context

### Differentiation Opportunities for .co.uk
1. **UK-based, UK-focused** — local SEO advantage
2. **Transparent pricing** — £1,500 flat rate vs "book a call"
3. **Blue Canvas AI brand** — established consultancy behind it
4. **Content-first approach** — blog + service pages for organic traffic
5. **Enterprise angle** — Mac Mini fleets, multi-agent setups
6. **Industry-specific pages** — solicitors, accountants, agencies, property
7. **"Albie as a Service"** — managed AI employee (unique offering)
