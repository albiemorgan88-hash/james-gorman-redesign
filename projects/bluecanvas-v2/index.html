Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we see results from AI implementation?
Most clients see measurable ROI within 4-8 weeks. Quick wins like process automation can show immediate impact, while comprehensive AI strategies typically deliver full benefits within 3 months.
Do we need technical expertise to work with you?
Not at all. We handle all technical implementation and provide training so your team can confidently use and maintain AI solutions. Our approach is designed for business leaders, not just technical teams.
What size businesses do you work with?
We work with businesses of all sizes, from ambitious startups to established enterprises. Our solutions are tailored to your specific needs, budget, and growth stage.
How much does AI implementation typically cost?
Investment varies based on scope and complexity. We start with a £750 audit to identify opportunities, then provide custom quotes for implementation. Most projects range from £2,000 to £15,000.
What ongoing support do you provide?
We offer Academy training (£250) to upskill your team and monthly mentoring (£50/mo) for ongoing guidance. We believe in building your internal AI capability, not creating dependency.
Can you integrate with our existing systems?
Yes, we specialise in seamless integration with existing tools and workflows. Our implementations enhance rather than disrupt your current operations.
