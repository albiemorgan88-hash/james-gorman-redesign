Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI audit and do I need one? +
An AI audit is a deep-dive assessment of your business operations, identifying where artificial intelligence can save you time, reduce costs, or increase revenue. If you're curious about AI but unsure where to start, an audit gives you a clear, jargon-free roadmap. It's the smartest first step before committing to any AI investment.
How long does it take to see results from AI implementation? +
It depends on scope, but most clients see measurable improvements within 4-8 weeks. Quick wins like automated lead generation can deliver ROI in days. Larger projects like predictive analytics take longer but produce compounding returns.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with Blue Canvas? +
Not at all. We handle the technical side and translate everything into plain language. Our Academy programmes build your confidence with AI tools regardless of your starting point.
What industries do you work with? +
We work across retail, professional services, healthcare, hospitality, construction, legal, and more. AI is industry-agnostic. If your business has data, processes, or customer interactions, there are AI opportunities we can unlock.
What is the Blue Canvas Academy? +
The Academy is our training arm. For individuals: a structured programme teaching you to use AI tools effectively, priced at £250. For businesses: team upskilling at the same price. Graduates continue learning through our mentoring membership at £50/month.
How is Blue Canvas different from other AI consultancies? +
We're practitioner-led, not theory-led. Our founder Phil Patterson has hands-on experience in venture capital, blockchain, and AI implementation. We don't just hand you a strategy deck and leave — we build, implement, and train your team so AI capability stays with your business.
