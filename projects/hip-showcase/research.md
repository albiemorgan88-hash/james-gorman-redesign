# HIP Psychology School Showcase Research
*Researched: March 26, 2026*

## 1. UK Teacher Absence Statistics

### Key Stats:
- **67.5%** of teachers took sickness absence in 2021/22 (vs 45.3% in 2019/20 pre-pandemic)
- **9.3 days** average sickness absence per teacher who took time off (vs 8.6 days in 2020/21)
- **Supply teacher costs to schools:**
  - Primary schools: **£218 per day** average cost
  - Secondary schools: **£291 per day** average cost  
  - Special schools: **£270 per day** average cost
- **Supply teacher pay vs cost:** Schools pay almost **100% mark-up** on supply teachers
  - Teachers receive: £136 (primary), £150 (secondary), £144 (special)
  - Schools pay: £218 (primary), £291 (secondary), £270 (special)

### Supply Teacher Market Impact:
- **30% more** supply cover days needed in Autumn 2022 vs same period 2021 (Zen Educate data)
- **Three-fifths** of schools report increased demand for supply teachers since COVID
- Key drivers: sickness absence (physical/mental health), long-term absences, recruitment difficulties

*Sources: DfE School Workforce Statistics 2024, Schools Week DfE Study (Sept 2024)*

## 6. School Pain Points - What Headteachers Actually Complain About

### Ofsted/Inspection Stress:
- **92% of headteachers** report increased stress from Ofsted inspections
- **94% of primary schools** report increased workload due to Ofsted preparation
- Only **3% of schools** do nothing special to prepare for inspection
- **"Driving the joy out of education"** - frequent complaint
- **Performance anxiety** and fear of "the call"

### Top Workload Drivers (in order):
1. **Poor work-life balance (95%)**
2. **Inspection pressure (92%)**
3. **Insufficient staff levels (90%)**
4. **Appraisal/internal accountability (90%)**

### Administrative Burden:
- **Performance-related pay (PRP)** being scrapped Sept 2024 due to "significant workload burden"
- **23 barred administrative tasks** introduced to reduce teacher workload
- Target: **5 hours per week** workload reduction
- **"Mountain of evidence"** on excessive workload driving teachers to breaking point

### Specific Complaints:
- **Vexatious parent complaints** increasing
- **Funding pressures** creating additional admin
- **Data collection and reporting** overwhelming
- **Supply teacher management** time-consuming and expensive
- **Long working hours** and **inadequate funding** combination

### Mental Health Impact:
- High-stakes inspection system causing teacher exodus
- **"Breaking point"** frequently mentioned in research
- Many leaving for overseas positions due to workload
- Headship becoming "impossible job" due to inspection stress

*Sources: NASUWT Survey, NEU State of Education, TeacherToolkit Ofsted Research, Workload Reduction Taskforce 2024*
