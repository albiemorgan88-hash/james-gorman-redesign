## What They Do
Luxury/UHNW recruitment agency. Four divisions:
1. **Quay Crew** — superyacht crew recruitment (original business). Entry-level through Captains.
2. **Quay Maritime** — land-based maritime roles: shipyards, yacht management companies, marine corporate offices.
3. **Quay Estates** — private estate staff: butlers, estate managers, household staff for UHNW clients.
4. **Quay Family Office** — PAs, EAs, CFOs for UHNW family offices.

## Industry Pain Points (recruitment/superyacht specific)
1. **Candidate sourcing** — global, multilingual candidate pool; crew from 50+ nationalities
2. **CV screening volume** — high volume of applications, many unqualified
3. **Certification tracking** — STCW, ENG1 medicals, flag state requirements, all expire at different times
4. **Seasonal demand spikes** — Med season vs Caribbean season creates surges
5. **Candidate-market difficulty** — Tim himself noted "very difficult candidate market"
6. **Rotation management** — junior crew rotation rising, complex scheduling
7. **Salary benchmarking** — they already do annual surveys manually
8. **Reference checking** — CrewPass partnership suggests this is time-consuming
9. **Multi-division coordination** — now 4 divisions sharing candidates/clients
10. **UHNW client expectations** — extremely high service levels, confidentiality, speed
