2 AI in Luxury & Maritime Recruitment

The recruitment industry is being transformed by AI, but luxury/UHNW and maritime recruitment lags behind mainstream adoption. This creates both a risk and an opportunity for Quay Group.

Where the industry is heading

AI-powered candidate matching is replacing keyword-based CV search. Platforms like HireVue, Eightfold, and Fetcher use semantic understanding to match candidates to roles based on skills, experience patterns, and cultural fit — not just keywords.

Automated sourcing and outreach tools can scan global candidate pools, social platforms, and maritime databases to surface passive candidates that manual search would miss.

Compliance automation is critical in maritime — STCW certifications, ENG1 medicals, flag state requirements, and visa status all have expiry dates. AI can track, alert, and verify these automatically.

Conversational AI is handling initial candidate screening, scheduling, and FAQ responses — freeing recruiters for high-value relationship work with UHNW clients.

Quay Group's Advantage

With 12 years of placement data, industry surveys, and deep client relationships, Quay Group has the data foundation that most competitors lack. The question isn't whether to adopt AI — it's which applications deliver the fastest ROI while preserving the personal, high-touch service that UHNW clients expect.
