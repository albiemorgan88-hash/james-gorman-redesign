Half-Day AI Consultancy Session

Agenda & Session Plan
Client: Tim Clarke, Quay Group
Date: TBC
Consultant: Phil Patterson
Duration: ~4 hours
09:00
60 min

Discovery & Current State

Understand how the business operates today — workflows, tools, team structure, and pain points.

  • Walk through a typical recruitment lifecycle end-to-end
  • Map current tech stack (ATS, CRM, spreadsheets, comms tools)
  • Identify top 3 time drains for consultants
  • Understand cross-division workflows and data sharing
  • Discuss client expectations and service level commitments
  • Note any previous tech initiatives — what worked, what didn't
💡 Tip for consultant: Let the client talk 70% of the time here. Listen for the real pain, not the assumed pain.
10:00
15 min

☕ Break

10:15
60 min

AI Opportunity Mapping

Present tailored AI opportunities based on pre-session research, adjusted for what was learned in Discovery.

  • Walk through the pre-session brief opportunities
  • Show real-world examples and case studies for each
  • Discuss which resonate most with the client
  • Identify any new opportunities surfaced during Discovery
  • Remove or deprioritise anything that doesn't fit
💡 Use screen sharing to show live examples. Tangible > theoretical.
11:15
45 min

Prioritisation Workshop

Collaboratively rank opportunities using a simple Impact vs Effort framework.

  • Score each opportunity: Impact (1-5) × Ease (1-5)
  • Plot on a 2×2 matrix (Quick Wins / Strategic Bets / Nice-to-Haves / Avoid)
  • Select top 3 priorities for the next 90 days
  • Discuss budget appetite and internal capacity
  • Agree on "do now / do next / do later" buckets
12:00
15 min

☕ Break

12:15
45 min

Quick Wins — Live Demo

Hands-on demonstration of 2–3 tools the client could implement within weeks.

  • Demo 1: AI CV parsing & candidate matching (e.g., with sample CVs)
  • Demo 2: AI-assisted content/report generation
  • Demo 3: Automated outreach or CRM intelligence (if relevant)
  • Let the client interact — hands on keyboard where possible
  • Discuss costs, setup time, and who would manage each tool
💡 Prepare demos in advance with client-relevant data. Generic demos don't land.
13:00
30 min

Roadmap & Next Steps

Wrap up with a concrete action plan and clear next steps.

  • Summarise agreed priorities (top 3)
  • Draft 30-60-90 day implementation timeline
  • Outline estimated costs for each initiative
  • Define what Blue Canvas will deliver vs. client-side actions
  • Agree on follow-up cadence and communication
  • Discuss ongoing support / retainer options
