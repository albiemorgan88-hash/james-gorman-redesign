## Budget & Settings

### Daily Budget
- **Total: £15/day (£450/month)**
- Ad Group 1 (Local + High-Intent): £10/day
- Ad Group 2 (Industry-Specific): £5/day

*This is within the £10-20/day range. Start at £15, scale to £20 if converting well.*

### Bid Strategy
- **Week 1-2:** Maximise Clicks (gather data, cap at £5 max CPC)
- **Week 3+:** Switch to Maximise Conversions
- **After 30 conversions:** Switch to Target CPA (aim for £50-75)

### Geographic Targeting

| Ad Group | Primary Geo | Radius/Region |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| 1 (Local keywords 1-12) | Derry | 30mi radius |
| 1 (Local keywords 1-12) | Belfast | 20mi radius |
| 1 (UK keywords 13-17) | United Kingdom | National |
| 2 (Industry) | United Kingdom + ROI | National |

**Important:** Set to "People IN or regularly in" your target locations (not "interested in" — wastes budget).

### Ad Scheduling
- **Mon-Fri: 7am - 8pm** (business decision-makers searching during work hours)
- **Sat: 9am - 2pm** (some weekend research)
- **Sun: Off** (save budget)

*Adjust after 2 weeks based on conversion data.*

### Device Targeting
- Start with all devices
- After 2 weeks, check device performance — consider reducing mobile bid if desktop converts better (B2B usually does)

## Conversion Tracking (Essential — Do Before Launching)

Track these actions:
1. **Calendly booking** — Primary conversion (value: £100)
2. **Contact form submission** — Secondary (value: £50)
3. **Phone call >60 seconds** — Secondary (value: £50)

Without conversion tracking, Google can't optimise. This is non-negotiable.
