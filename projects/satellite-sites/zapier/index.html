Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our Zapier consulting service.

What is Zapier?
Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects over 6,000 apps. It lets you create automated workflows (called Zaps) that move data between your tools without any manual effort.
How many Zaps can you build?
Our £1,500 flat rate covers a full automation project — typically 5–15 Zaps depending on complexity. We'll scope everything during the discovery call so there are no surprises.
Do I need a Zapier plan?
Yes, you'll need an active Zapier account. We'll advise on the best plan for your needs during the audit — most businesses do well on the Professional plan.
How long does setup take?
Most projects are completed within 1–2 weeks from kick-off. Complex enterprise setups may take 3–4 weeks.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer ongoing maintenance retainers starting from £300/month.
