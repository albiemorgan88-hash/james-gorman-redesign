### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is an AI agent?**
An AI agent is software that uses large language models (like GPT, Claude or Gemini) to perform tasks autonomously. Unlike a basic chatbot, an AI agent can use tools, access data, make decisions and take actions — like a digital employee that works 24/7.

**How long does setup take?**
Most AI agent setups are completed within 5–7 working days from the discovery call. Complex multi-agent systems or heavy integration work might take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline upfront.

**Which AI platform is right for me?**
Depends on your use case. We'll recommend the best fit during the discovery call. Simple personal assistant? OpenClaw. Visual workflow automation? n8n. Complex multi-agent systems? CrewAI or LangChain. We're platform-agnostic — we pick what works.

**What about ongoing AI costs?**
Most AI agents require API access to language models (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), which have usage-based pricing. We'll estimate your monthly costs upfront and optimise your setup to keep them sensible.

**Is my data safe?**
Security is built into every engagement. We deploy on your infrastructure (not ours), harden access controls, audit permissions and encrypt communications. Your data never leaves your control.
