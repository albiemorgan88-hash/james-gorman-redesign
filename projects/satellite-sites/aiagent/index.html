Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our AI agent setup service.

What is an AI agent?
An AI agent is software that uses large language models (like GPT or Claude) to autonomously perform tasks — answering customer queries, processing data, managing workflows, or handling sales enquiries. Unlike a simple chatbot, agents can use tools and take real actions.
Which platforms do you work with?
We work with OpenAI (GPT-4), Anthropic (Claude), open-source models, and platforms like LangChain, n8n, and custom Python/Node.js builds. We choose the best fit for your use case and budget.
How long does deployment take?
Most AI agent projects take 2–4 weeks from kick-off to production deployment. Simpler setups (e.g. a customer service agent) can be ready in under 2 weeks.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We implement strict data access controls, use secure API connections, and can deploy agents on your own infrastructure if required. We'll discuss your specific compliance needs during the discovery call.
Do you offer ongoing management?
Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer ongoing management and optimisation retainers starting from £500/month.
