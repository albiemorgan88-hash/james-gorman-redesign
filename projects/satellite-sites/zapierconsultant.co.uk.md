### Why You Need a Zapier Consultant

Zapier is deceptively simple. Point, click, automate. But the gap between "it works" and "it works properly" is where businesses lose money.

🔓

#### Security Risks

Bad Zaps leak data. Shared connections with overly broad permissions, unencrypted data passing between apps, orphaned automations running on old credentials — one misconfigured Zap can expose your entire stack. Most businesses don't find out until it's too late.

⏰

#### Wasted Time

You built 30 Zaps over two years. Half of them are broken. A quarter are redundant. The rest run but nobody knows what they actually do. You're paying for a Zapier plan that's mostly running ghosts. A Zapier expert cleans the mess and gets you back to actual ROI.

🔧

#### Missed Potential

Most businesses use Zapier for basic triggers — new form submission, send an email. That's 5% of what it can do. Multi-step workflows, conditional logic, filters, formatters, webhooks, API integrations — there's a whole level most teams never reach.

🔗

#### Integration Complexity

Connecting two apps is easy. Connecting twelve apps with conditional routing, error handling and data transformation? That's where it gets complicated. One wrong path and your data ends up in the wrong place — or nowhere at all.
