## Methodology

1. Reviewed existing bluecanvas.ai sitemap — identified ~90+ published blog posts and landing pages
2. Cross-referenced existing keyword data from analytics-seo skill (Feb 2026 baseline)
3. Identified industry verticals and topics NOT yet covered by existing content
4. Validated keyword demand via SERP analysis, Google autocomplete, and competitor content
5. Selected 5 keywords with: estimated UK volume 150+, low-to-moderate KD (<30), commercial/informational intent aligned with Blue Canvas services

## Existing Coverage Gaps Identified

The site has strong coverage of: AI consultancy (generic), AI for accountants, AI for recruitment, AI in construction, AI in dentistry, AI in education, AI grants NI, AI tools lists, AI strategy, AI automation, agentic AI.

**Not covered:** Industry-specific AI guides for estate agents, hospitality, HR; practical chatbot implementation; step-by-step AI implementation planning.
