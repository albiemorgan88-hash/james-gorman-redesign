## Read on the site right now
OCC already has heavy coverage around:
- OpenClaw buyer intent (`openclaw-consultant-uk`, setup, pricing, managed service, proof of concept, migration)
- broad AI agent education (`what-is-an-ai-agent`, costs, ROI, comparisons, industry pages)
- OpenClaw feature/setup content

What is still notably thin is the exact-match commercial cluster identified in the Ahrefs-led strategy docs:
- `ai employees` / `ai employee`
- `ai agents for business`
- `ai automation consultant`
- `agentic ai for business`
- `ai training for employees`

These are higher-value because they either:
1. map to the clearest non-brand search demand in `keyword-research.md`, or
2. bridge OCC from niche OpenClaw intent into broader business AI buying journeys without drifting off-brand.

## Why I did not prioritise some other gaps
- `openclaw agency` / `openclaw expert`: valid, but more likely to cannibalise existing `openclaw-consultant-uk` coverage.
- `ai managed services`: commercially useful, but it overlaps more directly with existing `openclaw-managed-service-uk`.
- more industry pages: OCC already has a large industry footprint, the bigger win is strengthening the central category pages those industry pages should link back to.

## Positioning notes to avoid cannibalisation
- **`ai-employees-for-business`** should be the commercial framing page, not a duplicate of `openclaw-for-business`.
- **`ai-agents-for-business`** should be the category explainer and buyer guide, not a setup tutorial.
- **`ai-automation-consultant-uk`** should stay tightly about hiring/evaluating help, with internal links to implementation and OpenClaw-specific pages.
- **`agentic-ai-for-business`** should translate jargon into operating decisions, not just define the term.
- **`ai-training-for-employees`** should focus on rollout, adoption, policy, and practical training outcomes.
