## Intro draft
The phrase “AI agents for business” sounds broad because it is. It can mean anything from a tightly-scoped inbox assistant to a multi-agent workflow coordinating sales, ops, and reporting.

That breadth is exactly why buyers need a grounded guide. The problem is not finding another article saying AI agents are the future. The problem is deciding where they fit, what they should do first, and how to avoid overbuilding before the business is ready.

The strongest first deployments are usually boring in the best way. One workflow. One owner. Clear approvals. A result you can measure.

## Suggested structure

### H2: What counts as an AI agent in a business setting
- More than a prompt, less than a science-fiction employee.
- An agent can read context, choose actions, use tools, and pass work on.
- Explain the difference between chatbot, automation, and agent workflow.

### H2: The business use cases that justify the spend
- lead qualification and CRM updates
- customer service triage
- recurring reporting and data checks
- onboarding and internal admin handoffs
- document-heavy workflows in legal, finance, and operations

### H2: Three rollout paths buyers usually choose
1. **Assistive agent**: drafts, summaries, triage, research.  
2. **Semi-autonomous workflow**: takes actions with review points.  
3. **Agent team**: multiple specialised agents with orchestration and escalation logic.

### H2: What the first AI agent should not do
- anything with unclear rules
- high-risk financial or legal judgement without approval
- customer-facing work with no fallback path
- a giant multi-department rollout before one workflow has proven itself

### H2: What it costs to get started
- low-cost experiments with one workflow
- implementation projects with consultant oversight
- ongoing managed support when the workflow matters commercially
- steer readers into cost and consultant pages rather than forcing exact pricing here

## FAQ ideas
1. What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?  
   A chatbot mainly answers prompts. An AI agent can use context, choose actions, work across tools, and support a real workflow.
2. What is the best AI agent for business?  
   The best one is the one that fits the workflow, approval needs, and existing systems. There is no single best answer for every business.
3. How should a business start with AI agents?  
   Start with one repetitive workflow where time saved or response quality can be measured clearly.
4. Are AI agents only for enterprise teams?  
   No. SMEs often benefit fastest because repetitive admin and follow-up create a bigger drag on a smaller team.
5. Do AI agents need human oversight?  
   Yes, especially at the start and especially on sensitive work.
