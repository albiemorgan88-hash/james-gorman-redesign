# LinkedIn Post — Claude Cowork | 10 March 2026

## Post Copy

I haven't "used AI" in months.

That sounds mad for someone who runs an AI consultancy. But here's the thing — I stopped using AI as a tool and started working *with* it as a colleague.

Anthropic's Claude Cowork dropped in January and it quietly changed everything. It's not a chatbot you paste prompts into. It's a digital coworker that accesses your files, connects to your apps, runs multi-step tasks, and delivers finished work — not drafts.

Yesterday Microsoft announced they're building it into Copilot. That should tell you where this is heading.

Most businesses are still using AI like a fancy Google search. Meanwhile I've got an AI agent that runs operations at Blue Canvas day-to-day. SEO audits, lead research, content, client deliverables — it handles the lot while I focus on strategy and client relationships.

The gap between "companies experimenting with AI" and "companies actually working alongside it" is about to become a canyon.

This isn't about replacing people. It's about giving every knowledge worker a tireless, brilliant teammate who never calls in sick and remembers everything.

What's stopping your business from working this way? Genuine question — I'd love to hear what the blockers are.
