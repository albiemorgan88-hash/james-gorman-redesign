A cricket club asked us to automate their weekly lottery.

Not a Fortune 500 company.
Not a Silicon Valley startup.
A cricket club. In Ireland.

They were running the whole thing manually — selling tickets, tracking numbers, picking winners, chasing payments, updating members.

Hours of volunteer time. Every single week.

Blue Canvas built a system that handles the lot.

Automated draws. Payment processing. Member notifications. Results posted without anyone lifting a finger.

Total cost to run? Pennies.

Time saved per week? About 6 hours of volunteer admin that nobody wanted to do anyway.

Here's what gets lost in the AI conversation:

Most small organisations don't need GPT solving quantum physics.

They need the boring stuff handled.

The admin. The repetitive tasks. The things that eat time and drain energy from the work that actually matters.

That's where AI delivers real ROI — not in flashy demos, but in the unsexy automation that gives people their evenings back.

What's the one repetitive task in your business that you wish would just... disappear?
