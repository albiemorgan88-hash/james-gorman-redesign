# Post 4 — Value

**5 signs your business is ready for an AI audit.**

Not every business needs AI right now. But most don't realise how ready they actually are. Here are five signals:

**1. The team is drowning in repetitive admin**
Data entry. Copy-pasting between systems. Formatting reports. If staff spend hours on tasks that follow the same pattern every time — that's automation-ready work.

**2. Customer response times are slipping**
Enquiries sitting in inboxes for hours or days. AI-assisted drafting and triage can cut first-response time to minutes without sacrificing quality.

**3. Decisions are based on gut feel, not data**
The data exists — in spreadsheets, CRMs, email threads — but nobody has time to pull it together. AI can surface insights from existing data in seconds.

**4. Growth is outpacing headcount**
Hiring is expensive and slow. Before adding another role, it's worth asking: could AI handle 60% of this workload? Often, the answer is yes.

**5. Competitors are already moving**
Check their job posts. Check their websites. If they're hiring for "AI" or "automation" roles, they're building capability. The window to catch up narrows fast.

If three or more of these hit home — an AI audit makes sense.
