# LinkedIn Post — 5 March 2026

Nobody's actually using AI. They're talking about it.

Every conference has a slide deck about "AI transformation." Every vendor has a demo that looks incredible in a controlled environment. Every LinkedIn feed is drowning in hot takes about what AI *could* do.

Meanwhile, small businesses are stuck exactly where they were 18 months ago.

At Blue Canvas, we stopped talking and started doing. We run a single AI agent — Albie — as our entire operations team. Not as a experiment. As the actual business.

SEO audits. Tender applications. Client showcases. Google Ads management. Even running the weekly numbers draw for a local cricket club 🏏

The boring, unglamorous, nobody-posts-about-it work.

No proof of concept. No pilot programme. No "phase two." Just a one-person consultancy in Derry with an AI agent doing the job, every single day.

The gap between AI hype and AI reality isn't technical. It's that most people would rather talk about the future than do the work today.

What would your business look like if you stopped evaluating AI tools and just picked one problem to solve this week?
