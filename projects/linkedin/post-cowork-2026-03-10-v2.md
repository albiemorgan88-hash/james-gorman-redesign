**Date:** 10 Mar 2026

Nobody at Blue Canvas has "used AI" in months.

Sounds odd for an AI consultancy. But there's a difference between using AI and working with it.

Anthropic's Claude Cowork launched in January. It's not a chatbot. It accesses files, connects to apps, runs multi-step tasks, and delivers finished work — not drafts.

Yesterday Microsoft announced they're building the same thing into Copilot. That tells you where this is heading.

Most businesses are still treating AI like a fancy Google search.

Meanwhile the companies actually working alongside AI? They're pulling away. Fast.

The gap between "experimenting" and "adopting" is becoming a canyon.

This isn't about replacing people. It's about giving every knowledge worker a tireless teammate who never calls in sick.

What's actually stopping your business from working this way?
