Privacy

Privacy notice for website enquiries

This notice explains the information requested by the workshop and resource forms on AntiBullyingNI.co.uk.

Information requested

The forms ask for professional contact information such as your name, work email, school or organisation, role and details needed to understand a workshop or resource request.

How enquiries are used

Information submitted through these forms is used to respond to the request and arrange relevant follow-up. The forms are not intended for confidential pupil information or incident reports.

Data minimisation

Please do not include pupil names, health information, safeguarding records or other confidential case details. Use your school’s approved safeguarding and reporting routes for individual concerns.

Retention, sharing and rights

Enquiry information should be kept only for as long as it is needed to respond and meet relevant record-keeping obligations. To ask about access, correction or deletion of your information, use the HIP Psychology contact page.

Support

Need help with bullying now?

If you are a pupil or parent, contact your school’s safeguarding or pastoral team or speak to a trusted adult. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112.
