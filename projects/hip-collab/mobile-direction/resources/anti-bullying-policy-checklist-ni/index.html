NI Anti-Bullying Policy Checklist

Practical review prompts for Northern Ireland schools, designed to be used alongside the Addressing Bullying in Schools Act and current Education Authority guidance.

What is included

  • Definition and scope prompts
  • Recording and review schedule
  • Parent and pupil consultation prompts
  • Education Authority resource links

This resource is being checked against current regional guidance before release.

How NI schools can use the checklist

Northern Ireland school leaders can use the checklist before Anti-Bullying Week, during policy review or after a significant pastoral concern. It prompts the team to check whether definitions, recording expectations, pupil consultation and parent-facing language are current and easy for staff to apply.

Where it fits in the NI hub

Use it alongside the Addressing Bullying in Schools Act summary, the NI policy template guide and the Education Authority resource page.

Use it alongside official guidance

These practical prompts support school planning. They do not replace current statutory, Department, Education Authority or safeguarding guidance for your setting.

Need help with bullying now?
