Support and safeguarding information

This website provides school planning information. It is not an incident-reporting service, emergency service or replacement for your school’s safeguarding procedures.

For pupils and parents

Contact the school’s safeguarding or pastoral team, or speak to a trusted adult. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112.

For school staff

Follow your school’s current safeguarding, child-protection and incident-reporting procedures. Use the official regional sources linked throughout this site when a concern may require escalation.
