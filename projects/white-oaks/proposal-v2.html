1. Services

Blue Canvas AI will design, build and deploy White Oaks Connect — a dedicated aftercare app for your residents to stay connected and supported after completing their treatment programme.

The app gives graduates a digital support network that fits in their pocket, helping prevent relapse and keeping people connected to their recovery journey. Your team gets a simple dashboard showing who's checking in, who might need extra support, and trends across your aftercare community.

Core features included in the build:

  • Community Support — Peer-to-peer encouragement and shared experiences
  • Progress Tracking — Visual charts showing recovery milestones and achievements
  • Emergency Support — Direct link to crisis helplines and White Oaks support
  • Appointment Reminders — Follow-up session scheduling and notifications
  • Educational Content — Recovery resources and coping strategies
  • Staff Dashboard — Overview of residents' wellbeing without compromising privacy

Technical specification: Progressive Web App (works on iOS, Android and desktop — no app store download required). Secure cloud database, end-to-end encryption, GDPR compliant, 99.9% uptime.
