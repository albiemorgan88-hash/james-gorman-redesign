1. The Project — White Oaks Connect

White Oaks Connect is a dedicated aftercare app for your residents to stay connected and supported after completing their treatment programme. Think of it as a digital support network that fits in their pocket.

After leaving White Oaks, residents often feel isolated and vulnerable — this app gives them a lifeline back to their recovery community and tools to maintain their progress. Recovery doesn't end at discharge. This extends your care and support beyond the programme, helping prevent relapse and keeping people connected to their recovery journey.

Your team will have access to a simple web dashboard showing who's checking in regularly, who might need extra support, and trends across your aftercare community — like a gentle pulse on how your graduates are doing, without being intrusive.

2. What We'll Build

Blue Canvas AI will design, build and deploy the White Oaks Connect app, including:

  • Daily Check-ins — Simple wellness tracking with mood, sleep, and stress levels
  • Community Support — Peer-to-peer encouragement and shared experiences
  • Progress Tracking — Visual charts showing recovery milestones and achievements
  • Emergency Support — Direct link to crisis helplines and White Oaks support
  • Appointment Reminders — Follow-up session scheduling and notifications
  • Educational Content — Recovery resources and coping strategies
  • Staff Dashboard — Overview of residents' wellbeing without compromising privacy

3. Technical Details

Platform: Progressive Web App (iOS, Android & desktop)
Backend: Secure cloud database with real-time updates
Security: End-to-end encryption, GDPR compliant
Hosting: Cloud infrastructure, 99.9% uptime

The app works through any web browser — residents simply add it to their home screen. No app store download required.
