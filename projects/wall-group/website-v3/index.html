What began as a 468 sq ft corner shop on the Crumlin Road has grown into one of North Belfast’s most trusted retail groups. Founded by Gavan and Helen Wall, The Wall Group now operates six stores across North Belfast, employing over 300 local people.

From our flagship Landscape Filling Station to our newest Flax EUROSPAR, every store reflects our commitment to quality, value, and community.
