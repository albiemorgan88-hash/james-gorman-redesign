Our Story

From a corner shop to a community

ABOUT THE WALL GROUP

Built on hard work,
driven by community

What began as a single 468 sq ft corner shop on the Crumlin Road has grown into one of North Belfast’s most trusted retail groups. Founded by Gavan and Helen Wall, The Wall Group now operates six stores, employing over 300 local people and serving thousands of customers every day.

OUR JOURNEY

From 2008 to today

2008

Gavan and Helen Wall open their first store — a 468 sq ft SPAR on the Crumlin Road. The beginning of a family business built on hard work, long hours, and a commitment to the local community.

2011

The Landscape Filling Station opens at 135 Crumlin Road, transforming a derelict site into North Belfast’s premier convenience and fuel destination. Open 24/7, it quickly becomes a community hub.

2014

SPAR Whitewell joins the portfolio, bringing Wall Group quality and service to the Whitewell Road area. The team grows past 100 employees.

2017

Rathcoole Filling Station opens at 42 Doagh Road with SPAR, Subway, and Texaco fuel. Subway Fortwilliam follows on the Antrim Road, bringing the group to five locations.

2023

The flagship Flax EUROSPAR opens in the Flax Centre on Ardoyne Avenue — the group’s largest and most ambitious store, featuring Wall’s Foodhall & Butchery, a full-service Daily Deli, and premium off sales.

2026

Today, The Wall Group operates six stores across North Belfast, employing over 300 local people. The focus remains the same as day one: family, community, and excellence.

“Every decision we make comes back to one question — is this good for the community? If the answer is yes, we do it. That’s how we’ve always operated, and it always will be.”

Gavan Wall, Founder
