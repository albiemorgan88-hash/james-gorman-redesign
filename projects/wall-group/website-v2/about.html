Our Story

From a single phone call to a barrister, to Northern Ireland's most trusted community retail network.

Journey of Growth

2008
The Beginning
First store — Landscape Filling Station opens on Crumlin Road with just 468 sq ft of space. A leap of faith that would change everything.
2010
Community Expansion
SPAR Ardoyne opens, bringing essential services and employment to the heart of North Belfast.
2016
Innovation in Food
YoggieBerrie launches on Antrim Road, showcasing our commitment to fresh, innovative food concepts.
2020
Building Momentum
8 stores operational with 140+ team members, establishing ourselves as a cornerstone of Belfast's retail landscape.
2024
Major Investment
Flax EUROSPAR Ardoyne opens — our largest project yet with £4M investment and 7,800 sq ft of community-focused retail space.
2026
Today & Tomorrow
12+ stores, 300+ team members, serving 5M+ customers annually across Belfast and Northern Ireland.

Building Bridges, Creating Opportunities

At The Wall Group, we believe business has the power to unite communities. Our cross-community employment approach has created hundreds of opportunities across traditional divides, proving that shared purpose transcends all boundaries.

Every store we open becomes a gathering place where neighbors meet, families shop, and communities thrive. We're not just retailers — we're community builders, creating spaces where everyone belongs.

Partnership with Henderson Group

Our strategic partnership with Henderson Group and SPAR Northern Ireland combines our local community focus with world-class retail expertise.

This collaboration enables us to offer premium products, competitive prices, and exceptional service while maintaining our commitment to community-centered retail.

"We're not afraid of failure. We will achieve it."
— Gavan Wall, Founder

This philosophy drives everything we do. We embrace challenges, learn from setbacks, and never stop pushing forward. Our success isn't measured only in profits, but in the jobs we create, the communities we serve, and the connections we foster across Northern Ireland.
