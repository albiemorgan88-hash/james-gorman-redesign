Years Growing
Building Communities Since 2008

It started with a phone call to a barrister and a 468 square foot filling station on the Crumlin Road. What began as a leap of faith has grown into Northern Ireland's most trusted community retail network.

From that first store, we've built something bigger than retail — we've created gathering places, employment opportunities, and genuine connections across Belfast and beyond. Every Wall Group location serves as a cornerstone of its community.
