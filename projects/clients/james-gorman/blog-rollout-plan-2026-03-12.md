## 6) URL structure
Best-case Wix URL approach:
- blog hub: `/property-blog-derry`
- post structure: `/post/post-slug`

If Wix allows custom blog prefixes, use a clean local prefix. If not, standard Wix post URLs are acceptable.

### Suggested slug style for posts
Use short, descriptive, keyword-led slugs. Examples:
- `/post/choosing-an-estate-agent-in-derry`
- `/post/how-to-prepare-your-home-for-sale-in-derry`
- `/post/property-market-update-derry-and-north-west`
- `/post/selling-your-home-in-limavady`
- `/post/buying-a-home-in-eglinton`
- `/post/selling-a-home-in-dungiven`

### Slug rules
- lowercase only
- hyphens, no underscores
- avoid dates unless the article is time-sensitive
- avoid filler words where possible
- keep one main intent per article

## 7) Internal linking rules
This is the bit that makes the blog commercially useful.

## Core rule
Every blog post should push authority and clicks into a **money page**.
No orphan articles. No dead-end content.

## Each post should include links to:
- one relevant **service page**
- one relevant **location page** where appropriate
- one lead-gen page, ideally **valuation** or **contact**

## Priority internal link targets
Use/finalise these core destinations:
- `/sales-derry`
- `/lettings-derry`
- `/free-property-valuation-derry`
- `/property-search-derry`
- `/contact-james-gorman-property`
- `/estate-agent-derry`
- `/estate-agent-limavady`
- `/estate-agent-eglinton`
- `/estate-agent-dungiven`

## Anchor text rules
Use natural anchor text variations such as:
- estate agent in Derry
- free property valuation in Derry
- lettings services in Derry
- selling your home in Limavady
- estate agent in Eglinton
- estate agent in Dungiven

Avoid repeating the exact same anchor text every time.

## Link placement rules
Per article:
- 1 contextual link in the intro or first half
- 1–2 contextual links in the body
- 1 CTA link near the end

### Do not
- stuff 8–10 links into short posts
- link every mention of Derry
- link to pages that are not ready yet
