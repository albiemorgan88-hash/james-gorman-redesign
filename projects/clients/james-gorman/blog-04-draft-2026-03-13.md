# Selling a Home in Dungiven: 7 Mistakes That Slow Down a Sale

If you are thinking of selling in Dungiven, getting the launch right from the start matters more than most people think.

A lot of sellers do the hard work of deciding to move, then accidentally weaken the sale before the property even has a proper chance.

Here are seven common mistakes worth avoiding.

## 1. Pricing too high because it feels safer
An inflated asking price often reduces early enquiry levels and makes the property look stale later. A realistic valuation is usually the better move.

## 2. Going live before the house is properly ready
Quick fixes, tidy presentation and strong photography can materially affect first impressions.

## 3. Using weak photos
Online presentation is the first viewing for most buyers. If the listing looks flat, cluttered or dark, fewer serious buyers will click through.

## 4. Not thinking about local buyer behaviour
Buyers in Dungiven often compare value against Derry, Limavady and nearby village markets. That means pricing needs to make sense in context.

## 5. Poor communication during viewings and offers
Momentum matters once the property is live. Slow follow-up or unclear feedback can lose serious buyers.

## 6. Ignoring outside space and practical details in the marketing
Parking, gardens, sheds, home office space and storage often matter a lot in this market. They should be easy to spot in the listing.

## 7. Choosing an agent without a proper launch strategy
You do not just need someone to upload the property. You need a clear plan for pricing, marketing, viewings and negotiation.

## Final thought
If you are selling in Dungiven, the best next step is to get a proper valuation and build the launch plan around realistic buyer demand instead of guesswork.
