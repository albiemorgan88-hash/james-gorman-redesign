## What buyers tend to compare

Waterside buyers often compare homes by:

- proximity to Derry city and key routes
- parking and access
- bedroom size and storage
- garden practicality
- condition and likely upgrade cost
- value compared with Cityside, Newbuildings, Drumahoe or Eglinton

The right choice is not always the newest or largest property. It is the home that fits the buyer's budget, routine and tolerance for work after completion.

## How to read a listing properly

Photos and floorplans are helpful, but they do not tell the full story. Before viewing, look at the property description carefully and note what is missing as well as what is included.

Useful questions include:

- Has the property been priced against recent local demand?
- Is the layout practical, or just photographed well?
- Does the garden suit the buyer's actual lifestyle?
- Are there signs of upgrades being needed soon?
- How does it compare with nearby alternatives at the same budget?

## Viewing advice for Waterside buyers

A viewing should be practical, not just emotional. Check natural light, storage, heating, windows, parking, noise, maintenance and how the home feels at different points in the day if possible.

For family buyers, the surrounding routine matters too. Think about school routes, work routes, local services, play space and whether the area feels convenient after the novelty of the move has worn off.
