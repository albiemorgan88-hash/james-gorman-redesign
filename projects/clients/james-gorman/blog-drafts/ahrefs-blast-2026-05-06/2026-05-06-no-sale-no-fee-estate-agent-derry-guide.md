# No Sale No Fee Estate Agent Derry: What Sellers Should Check

Searching for a no sale no fee estate agent in Derry usually means you want confidence before committing. Sellers want to know what they will pay, when they will pay it and what happens if the property does not sell.

That is a sensible question. But fee structure is only one part of choosing an estate agent.

## What no sale no fee usually means

In simple terms, no sale no fee means the agent's selling fee is normally payable only if the property sells. The exact terms can vary, so sellers should always read the agreement carefully before signing.

Check what is included, what is extra and whether there are any withdrawal, marketing or upfront costs.

## Questions sellers should ask

Before choosing an agent, ask:

- What fee is payable if the property sells?
- Are there any upfront costs?
- What marketing is included?
- Is photography included?
- What happens if I withdraw the property?
- How long is the agreement?
- How will viewings and feedback be handled?

A clear agent should be comfortable answering these questions plainly.

## Do not judge on fee alone

A cheaper fee can become expensive if the valuation is weak, the marketing is poor or the negotiation is passive. The real question is not only what the agent charges. It is what the agent helps you achieve.

Good presentation, realistic pricing, consistent follow-up and strong negotiation can matter far more than a small fee difference.

## Why transparency builds trust

Sellers should understand the process before launch. That includes price strategy, marketing plan, viewing feedback, offer handling and when the agent would recommend a change.

If the agreement is unclear, ask for clarification before proceeding.

There should not be surprises. Always check the agency agreement for marketing, withdrawal or extra charges.

Not automatically. Poor marketing or weak negotiation can cost more than the fee saving.
