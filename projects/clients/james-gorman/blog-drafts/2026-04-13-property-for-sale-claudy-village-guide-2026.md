## Why Claudy keeps attracting attention

Claudy is close enough to Derry to stay practical but far enough away to feel properly separate from the city. That balance is a huge part of its appeal.

A drive into Derry is manageable for work, shopping and older pupils travelling for school or activities. At the same time, everyday life in Claudy feels quieter and more grounded. The village has a Main Street, familiar local faces, active sport and easy access to countryside and river valley scenery. Buyers often describe it as somewhere that still feels settled.

That sense of identity matters in property. Homes tend to hold interest better in places where buyers feel they are moving into a community rather than just buying a house number. Claudy benefits from that.

## What your budget may buy in Claudy

Claudy remains one of the more sensible-value village markets in the wider Derry area.

As a broad market guide, **three-bedroom homes commonly sit around £110,000 to £150,000** depending on position, finish and whether the property has been modernised. **Four-bedroom detached homes and larger family houses often come in from around £150,000 to £220,000**, while stronger rural homes, homes with land or standout detached properties can move above that.

For many buyers, that comparison against Derry is what makes Claudy attractive. At the same spend, Claudy can often offer:

- more internal space
- better parking
- larger gardens or plots
- a quieter setting
- a more settled family feel

The market is still price-sensitive, though. Buyers in Claudy care about value and tend to compare carefully. The strongest opportunities are usually the properties where condition, layout and price line up properly.

## Schools and daily practicality

Schools are a major part of why family buyers take Claudy seriously.

The village is served by **Cumber Claudy Primary School**, **St Colmcille's Primary School and Nursery Unit**, and **St Patrick's and St Brigid's College**. That range helps buyers picture staying in the area for the long term rather than treating Claudy as a short stop before another move.

Beyond education, everyday convenience matters. Claudy has the basics needed for ordinary life, including local shops and services around the village core, healthcare access, takeaways, cafés and the **Diamond Centre**, which adds useful community and leisure value.

That combination helps the village feel genuinely liveable rather than dependent on Derry for absolutely everything.
