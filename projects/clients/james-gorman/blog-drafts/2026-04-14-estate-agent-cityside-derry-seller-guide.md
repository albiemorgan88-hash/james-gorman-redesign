Because Cityside stock varies so much, sellers should think carefully about the strengths a buyer will notice first.

## Pricing Cityside property properly

This is often where sellers go wrong. Some overprice because the home is central or has period details. Others underplay the value of a good location because the property is not the biggest in its bracket.

The right answer usually sits in the middle. Cityside prices should reflect:

- the exact street or immediate pocket
- whether the property is period, modern or converted stock
- the standard of kitchen, bathroom and windows
- storage, parking and outside space
- whether the layout works for contemporary living

Two Cityside homes can look similar online but attract very different reactions once buyers consider all of that. A realistic price gives the property the best chance of getting attention early, which is especially important in a market where buyers can be decisive.
