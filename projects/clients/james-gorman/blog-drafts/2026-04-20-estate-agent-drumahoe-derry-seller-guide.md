## Pricing a home in Drumahoe properly

One of the biggest seller mistakes is pricing from personal attachment rather than market behaviour. Owners understandably think about what they paid, what they improved and what they need from the move. Buyers think about choice.

They are comparing your house against other homes with similar layouts, newer finish, better gardens or stronger value. That is why pricing needs to be disciplined.

A proper valuation should consider:

- exact position in Drumahoe
- standard of kitchen, bathroom and flooring
- heating, windows and general maintenance
- parking, outdoor space and privacy
- what competing local stock looks like right now

The goal is not to go cheap. The goal is to launch at a level that creates believable value and serious interest.

## What sellers should do before the property goes live

The best pre-launch work is usually not dramatic. It is targeted.

Before listing, sellers should usually focus on:

- brightening tired rooms with simple repainting
- removing visual clutter so spaces read more clearly
- fixing obvious snags such as damaged trim, marked walls or worn sealant
- tidying the front approach and garden areas
- making sure key rooms photograph cleanly and honestly

In Drumahoe, buyers often respond well to homes that feel calm, practical and easy. You do not need a showroom finish. You do need a home that looks cared for.

## Why launch quality matters more than sellers think

The first days of a listing do a lot of heavy lifting. That is when the property feels fresh, portal response is strongest and buyers decide whether to take it seriously.

A weak launch can waste that window. Common problems include:

- pricing too high to generate clicks

- vague copy that does not explain what kind of buyer the home suits
- listing before the house looks ready

A better launch usually includes realistic pricing, sharper visuals and copy that explains the practical strengths of the home clearly.
