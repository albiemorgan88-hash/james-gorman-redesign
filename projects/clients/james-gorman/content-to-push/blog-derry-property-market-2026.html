Market Overview - Where We Stand

Derry's property market in 2026 shows resilience and steady growth, supported by local economic development and continued demand from both local buyers and those relocating to the area. Current average house prices vary significantly by area and property type, ranging from £85,000 for starter apartments to £350,000+ for premium family homes. The market demonstrates healthy diversity, with options available across different budget levels and lifestyle preferences.

Price Trends by Property Type

Terraced Houses (Most Popular)

Terraced houses remain Derry's most popular property type, offering excellent value for families and first-time buyers. Prices typically range from £95,000-£180,000 depending on size, condition, and location, with three-bedroom properties in good condition achieving the strongest demand.

Modern terraced houses in developments like Culmore and Drumahoe command premium prices due to contemporary features, energy efficiency, and family-friendly locations. These properties often achieve 10-15% higher values than equivalent older properties requiring updating.

Period terraced houses in areas like Cityside and Waterside attract buyers seeking character and charm. While requiring more maintenance, these properties offer unique features and potential for value enhancement through sympathetic improvement.

Semi-Detached Properties

Semi-detached homes provide the balance of space and value that appeals to growing families and professionals seeking more accommodation than terraced options provide. Price ranges typically span £120,000-£220,000, with location and condition being primary value determinants.

Properties featuring modern kitchens, additional bathrooms, and garden improvements achieve the strongest prices within this category. Buyers increasingly prioritize properties requiring minimal immediate investment, supporting values for well-maintained homes.

Detached Family Homes

Detached properties represent the premium end of Derry's family market, with prices ranging from £160,000-£350,000+ depending on size, location, and features. Areas like Eglinton and established Waterside locations command highest values in this category.

Luxury features like extensive gardens, multiple bathrooms, garage space, and period character can add substantial premiums. Properties offering privacy, space, and prestigious locations continue to attract strong buyer interest despite higher price points.

Apartments & Flats

Apartment markets serve diverse buyer groups, from first-time buyers to professionals seeking low-maintenance living. Price ranges typically span £65,000-£150,000, with location and property condition determining values within this range.

City center apartments offer convenience and lifestyle benefits that appeal particularly to young professionals and those preferring urban living. Properties with parking spaces and modern amenities achieve premium values within the apartment sector.

New Build vs Period Properties

New build properties command premiums for energy efficiency, modern layouts, and minimal maintenance requirements. Buyers increasingly value the predictability and lower running costs associated with contemporary construction standards.

Period properties attract buyers seeking character, unique features, and potential for value enhancement. While often requiring more investment, these properties offer charm and individuality that appeals to specific buyer segments willing to pay for distinctive homes.
