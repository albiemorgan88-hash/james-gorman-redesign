## Streets, developments and the local detail that shapes value

One of the mistakes non-local agents sometimes make in Eglinton is treating the whole village as though it has one value level. It does not.

Buyer perception changes based on the street and development. Homes on or around **Church Road**, **Hill Street**, **School Lane**, **Main Street**, **Mill Road** and **Queen Street** all present slightly different appeals, whether that is convenience, character, plot size or ease of access. Modern development names such as **River View**, **Oakwood Drive** and **Castle Gardens** also carry their own buyer expectations around finish, layout and family suitability.

That is why pricing cannot be based on a lazy average alone. A well-presented home on a strong road in Eglinton can justify more attention and stronger competition than a weaker comparable elsewhere in the village.
