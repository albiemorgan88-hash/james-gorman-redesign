Meet James Gorman

Originally from Essex with 20+ years in property across East London and surrounding areas, James brings a wealth of experience from traditional high street agencies to online hybrid leaders and the new build sector.

After working in Belfast, James settled in Derry 9 years ago, bringing his diverse background and unique blend of knowledge and dedication to Northern Ireland's property market.

Premium Services

Four pillars of exceptional property service, tailored to your needs

📷

Marketing Package

Upfront fee service including walk-through video, professional photography, room measurements, JGP board, and comprehensive listing across PropertyPal, our website, and all social media channels (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).

🏠

Lettings

Complete tenant management including meeting prospective tenants, obtaining references and photo ID, preparing tenancy agreements, and collecting first month's rent and deposit credited directly to the landlord.

💼

Residential Sales

Full-service sales including handling all enquiries, accompanied viewings (evenings and weekends), direct communication beyond 9-5, instant feedback after viewings, offer negotiations, and solicitor updates until completion. No fees if property doesn't sell.

🔑

Property Management

Premium service combining all marketing and lettings services, plus periodic inspection reports, rental payment handling, TDS deposit registration, meter readings, and key handover on moving day.
