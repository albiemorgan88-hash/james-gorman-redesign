## 8 Essential Questions to Ask Any Estate Agent

### 1. What's Your Average Time on Market for Properties Like Mine?

A quality agent should be able to tell you their average time to sale for properties in your area, price range, and property type. In Derry, depending on location and price point, this typically ranges from 6-16 weeks for reasonably priced properties.

**Red flag:** Vague answers or claims that all properties sell within a few weeks regardless of location or condition.

### 2. How Will You Market My Property?

Modern property marketing goes far beyond sticking a board outside and hoping for the best. Your agent should offer:

- Professional photography (not smartphone snaps)
- Virtual tours or detailed video walkthroughs
- Listings on major portals (Property Pal, Rightmove, etc.)
- Social media marketing across multiple platforms
- Targeted online advertising
- Local print advertising where appropriate
- Open houses and private viewings

**What to look for:** A comprehensive marketing package that reaches buyers where they actually search for properties.

### 3. What Are Your Fees and When Do I Pay Them?

Estate agent fees in Derry typically range from 1.5% to 3% of the sale price, plus VAT. However, fee structure varies significantly:

- **Traditional model:** Commission paid only on completion
- **Upfront fee model:** Fixed fee paid at instruction, regardless of sale
- **Hybrid models:** Combination of upfront and success fees

**Key point:** The cheapest isn't always the best value. A skilled agent who achieves £10,000 more for your property easily justifies higher fees.

### 4. How Many Properties Are You Currently Marketing?

An overloaded agent can't give your property the attention it deserves. While there's no magic number, be wary of agents juggling 50+ active listings unless they have substantial support staff.

**Sweet spot:** 15-30 active listings allows for personal attention while maintaining market presence.

### 5. Can You Provide References from Recent Clients?

Any confident, professional agent should be happy to provide references. Don't just ask for names – ask for specific examples of how they handled challenges or exceeded expectations.

**Bonus tip:** Check online reviews, but remember that property transactions are emotional – even excellent agents may have occasional negative reviews.

### 6. What's Your Knowledge of My Local Area?

Local expertise is absolutely crucial in Derry's diverse property market. Different areas command different prices, attract different buyer types, and have unique selling points:

- **Waterside vs Cityside dynamics**
- **School catchment areas that add value**
- **Transport links and commuter appeal**
- **Future development plans affecting property values**
- **Neighbourhood character and buyer preferences**

Your agent should demonstrate intimate knowledge of your specific area, not just general Derry market knowledge.

### 7. How Will You Handle Viewings?

Some agents are happy to hand over keys for DIY viewings, while others insist on accompanied viewings. Both approaches have merits:

**Accompanied viewings advantages:**
- Agent can highlight property benefits
- Better security for your possessions
- Immediate feedback from potential buyers
- Professional handling of questions and objections

**DIY viewings advantages:**
- More flexible for buyers
- Buyers may feel less pressured
- Allows for longer, more relaxed viewing

**Best practice:** A hybrid approach where initial viewings are accompanied, with DIY options for seriously interested buyers.

### 8. What Happens If My Property Doesn't Sell?

No agent can guarantee a sale, but they should have clear strategies for properties that aren't attracting offers:

- Price review and adjustment recommendations
- Enhanced marketing efforts
- Staging advice to improve presentation
- Honest assessment of property condition or presentation issues
- Clear communication about market conditions

## Red Flags: Warning Signs to Avoid

### The Over-Promiser
Be extremely wary of agents who:
- Guarantee unrealistic sale prices
- Promise sale within unrealistic timeframes
- Claim they "never" reduce asking prices
- Suggest your property is worth significantly more than others have valued it

### The Absent Agent
Warning signs include:
- Taking days to return calls or emails
- Missing or frequently rescheduling appointments
- Poor quality or delayed marketing materials
- Lack of feedback after viewings

### The Cookie-Cutter Approach
Avoid agents who:
- Use identical marketing for all properties
- Can't explain why your property is unique
- Offer identical strategies regardless of property type or price point
- Show little interest in understanding your specific needs

## Making Your Final Decision

### Compare Like-for-Like

When evaluating multiple agents, ensure you're making fair comparisons:

- Get all proposals in writing
- Compare total fees, not just percentages
- Evaluate marketing packages comprehensively
- Consider communication style and responsiveness
- Assess local knowledge and experience

### Trust Your Instincts

The numbers matter, but so does your comfort level. You'll be working closely with this person during what can be a stressful time. Choose someone who:

- Communicates clearly and honestly
- Responds promptly to your concerns
- Demonstrates professionalism in all interactions
- Shows genuine enthusiasm for your property
- Provides realistic expectations without crushing optimism

### The Value of Local Expertise

While national chains offer brand recognition, local independent agents often provide:
