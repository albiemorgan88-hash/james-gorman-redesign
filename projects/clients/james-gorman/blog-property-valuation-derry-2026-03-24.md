## Preparing Your Property for Valuation

### Presentation Matters

While valuers are professionals who can see past clutter and minor issues, good presentation helps:

- **Declutter:** Clear, spacious rooms appear larger
- **Clean thoroughly:** Clean properties suggest good maintenance
- **Make minor repairs:** Fix leaky taps, squeaky doors, broken handles
- **Maximize light:** Open curtains, clean windows, turn on lights
- **Create good first impressions:** Tidy garden, clean pathway, fresh paint on front door

### Documentation to Have Ready

Gather relevant documents:
- **Recent utility bills** (for running costs information)
- **Council tax band** (affects buyer calculations)
- **Any improvement receipts** (new boiler, extension, rewiring)
- **Planning permissions** (for any alterations or extensions)
- **Warranty information** (for newer components like windows, roof)
- **Energy Performance Certificate** (EPC)

**Q: How long does a property valuation take?**

**Q: How accurate are property valuations?**
A: Professional valuations from experienced local agents are typically accurate within 5-10% of eventual sale price when market conditions are stable.

**Q: What's the difference between a valuation and a survey?**
A: A valuation assesses market value for selling purposes. A survey examines structural condition for buyers and is more detailed and expensive.

A: If you're not selling, every 2-3 years is sufficient. If you're considering selling, annual valuations help you time the market.
