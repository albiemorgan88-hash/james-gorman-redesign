## What an estate agent needs to understand in Claudy

An estate agent in Claudy cannot treat every instruction the same way. The buyer profile changes depending on the type of property and exactly where it sits.

A modern three-bedroom semi in or close to the village appeals to first-time buyers, young families and buyers moving out from Derry for more space. A detached family home with a garden is likely to attract upsizers who want room for children, home working or extended family life. An older farmhouse, cottage or countryside property around the Claudy area may attract a different buyer entirely, someone who values character, views, outbuildings or development potential.

That changes how the property should be priced, photographed and marketed.

It also changes the questions buyers ask. In Claudy, buyers tend to care about:

- whether the home feels village-based or more rural

- how much value they are getting versus Derry, Eglinton or Waterside alternatives

An agent who knows the local market can answer those questions properly and position the property accordingly.

## Selling in Claudy, what actually makes a difference

In a village market, presentation matters because buyers notice small details. They look closely at kerb appeal, parking, gardens, layout flow and whether the home feels bright, warm and well kept.

The biggest improvements sellers can make before listing are often simple:

- fresh paint in high-traffic rooms
- tidier entrances and front gardens
- clear, uncluttered kitchens and family rooms
- finishing small repair jobs that suggest neglect if left undone
- making sure outdoor space is photographed properly

Village buyers do not just buy square footage. They buy how the property feels. A well-presented home in Claudy can create an immediate emotional pull, especially if it offers a garden, views or a layout that suits family life.

Pricing strategy is just as important. If a property sits too high above the natural Claudy market level, buyers tend to step back quickly because they always have comparison points in Derry, Dungiven and the surrounding villages. A sharper guide price usually creates stronger first-round interest and better-quality enquiries.
