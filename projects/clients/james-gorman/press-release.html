Derry Man Launches Independent Estate Agency With Fresh Approach to Property

Local entrepreneur James Gorman combines digital innovation with personal service to shake up Northern Ireland's property market

DERRY CITY, Northern Ireland – Local property professional James Gorman has launched his own independent estate agency, bringing a fresh, digital-first approach to property sales and lettings across Derry City and Northern Ireland. Based in the heart of the city, James Gorman Property represents a new generation of estate agents combining innovative marketing strategies with the personal touch that clients expect.

Having established himself as a trusted name in the local property market, James made the decision to launch his own agency to better serve clients with a more personalised, modern approach. Operating from 12 Clearwater in Derry, the agency specialises in sales and lettings throughout the city and wider Northern Ireland region.

The launch coincides with the unveiling of the agency's new website, jamesgormanproperty.com, which serves as the digital hub for the business. The site showcases James's commitment to using technology to enhance the property buying and selling experience, while maintaining the personal relationships that are crucial in the industry.

"I've always believed in Derry and its potential. This city has so much to offer, and I wanted to create an agency that reflects that confidence and ambition. We're not just selling houses – we're helping people find their place in a city that's going places,"

says James Gorman, founder of James Gorman Property.

What sets James Gorman Property apart is its blend of cutting-edge digital marketing with genuine personal service. The agency leverages modern technology and innovative marketing strategies while ensuring every client receives individual attention and expertise. This approach has already earned the agency excellent reviews on Trustpilot, with clients praising the professionalism, correspondence quality, and attention to detail.

"At the end of the day, buying or selling a home is one of the biggest decisions people make. You need someone who understands that, someone who's going to be straight with you and work their hardest for you. That's what we're about – proper service, honest advice, and getting results,"

James explains.

The agency currently manages premium properties across sought-after locations including Merchant's View and Coney Road, with listings featured on PropertyPal and Propertynews. James has also built a strong investor network, positioning the agency to serve both residential clients and property investors looking for opportunities across Northern Ireland.

Looking ahead, James Gorman Property has ambitious plans to expand its reach across the North West, building on the foundation of excellence established in Derry. The agency represents part of a growing trend of local entrepreneurs choosing to back themselves and their communities, contributing to the broader economic development of the region.

For James, the launch of his independent agency is about more than business – it's about investing in Derry's future and proving that local expertise, combined with modern approaches, can compete with anyone.
