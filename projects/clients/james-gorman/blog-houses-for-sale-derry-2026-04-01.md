## What to Look for When Viewing Houses

### Essential Property Checks

**Structural and Condition Factors:**
• **Roof condition**: check for missing tiles, leaks, or wear  
• **Damp or moisture issues**: particularly in older properties  
• **Heating system efficiency**: oil, gas, or electric systems  
• **Windows and doors**: double glazing and security features  
• **Electrical systems**: safety and adequacy for modern needs  
• **Plumbing condition**: water pressure and system age

**Layout and Living Considerations:**
• **Room sizes and proportions**: furniture placement possibilities  
• **Natural light levels**: window orientation and garden aspect  
• **Storage provision**: built-in wardrobes, loft access, utility areas  
• **Parking arrangements**: off-street parking increasingly valuable  
• **Garden orientation**: south-facing gardens most desirable  
• **Noise levels**: traffic, neighbours, and local environment

### Future Potential Assessment

**Value-Adding Opportunities:**
• **Extension possibilities**: rear, side, or loft conversion potential  
• **Modernisation scope**: kitchen, bathroom, or bedroom updates  
• **Energy efficiency improvements**: insulation, heating, solar panels  
• **Garden development**: landscaping, outbuildings, or parking  
• **Planning considerations**: local development plans affecting area

## Understanding the Buying Process

### Getting Mortgage Ready

**Financial Preparation:**
• **Credit score checking**: ensuring optimal borrowing terms  
• **Deposit saving**: typically 10-20% of purchase price required  
• **Income verification**: salary, self-employed, or other income sources  
• **Affordability assessment**: realistic budget including all costs  
• **Mortgage pre-approval**: strengthening your buying position

**Additional Costs Planning:**
• **Stamp duty**: payable on properties over certain thresholds  
• **Legal fees**: solicitor costs for conveyancing process  
• **Survey costs**: homebuyer's report or full structural survey  
• **Moving expenses**: removals, utilities, and immediate improvements  
• **Insurance**: buildings and contents insurance arrangement
