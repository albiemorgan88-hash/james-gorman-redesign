## Types of Property Valuations Available

### Estate Agent Valuation

The most common type of valuation for homeowners looking to sell. Local estate agents in Derry provide free market appraisals based on current market conditions, recent sales data, and their extensive knowledge of the local area. These valuations give you a realistic market price that will attract serious buyers.

### RICS Surveyor Valuation

A formal valuation carried out by a qualified RICS (Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors) surveyor. This type of valuation is typically required for mortgage applications, legal proceedings, or insurance purposes. RICS valuations are more detailed and carry professional indemnity insurance.

### Bank Valuation

Required when applying for a mortgage, this valuation is primarily to protect the lender's interests. It may be more conservative than a marketing valuation and focuses on ensuring the property provides adequate security for the loan.

## How to Prepare for a Property Valuation

### Improve Your Property's Presentation

**Before the valuation visit:**

• **Deep clean throughout**: carpets, windows, bathrooms, and kitchen appliances  
• **Declutter every room**: remove personal items to help valuers visualise space  
• **Complete minor repairs**: fix leaky taps, squeaky doors, and scuffed paintwork  
• **Maximise natural light**: clean windows, open curtains, and ensure all lights work  
• **Garden maintenance**: mow lawns, trim hedges, and clear any debris  
• **Fresh presentation**: consider fresh flowers and ensure good ventilation

### Gather Important Documentation

**Essential paperwork:**

• **Energy Performance Certificate** (EPC) - must be valid and up-to-date  
• **Building regulations certificates** for any extensions or conversions  
• **Planning permission documents** if applicable  
• **Guarantees and warranties** for recent work (boiler, windows, roof)  
• **Service charges and ground rent** details (if leasehold)  
• **Council tax band** information and recent bills

### Research Your Local Market

**Prepare market evidence:**

• Recent sales of similar properties in your area  
• Current asking prices for comparable homes  
• Local market trends and price movements  
• Any unique features that add value to your property

## Common Property Valuation Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these common pitfalls that can affect your property valuation:

### Overestimating Improvements

• **Personal taste improvements**: not all renovations add equivalent value  
• **Over-personalisation**: unique features may not appeal to general buyers  
• **DIY quality concerns**: professional vs amateur work quality differences  
• **Market timing**: improvements may not reflect current market preferences

### Market Misunderstanding

• **Ignoring comparable sales**: focusing on asking prices instead of achieved sales  
• **Location overconfidence**: assuming all areas are equal in buyer appeal  
• **Timing misconceptions**: not understanding seasonal market variations  
• **Emotional attachment**: personal connection affecting realistic price expectations

### Preparation Oversights

• **Poor presentation**: clutter, maintenance issues, or poor lighting  
• **Documentation gaps**: missing certificates or warranty information  
• **Access problems**: restricted viewing times or difficult property access  
• **Disclosure failures**: not mentioning known issues or planned developments
