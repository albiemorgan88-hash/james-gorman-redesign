## Full article copy
Selling your home is a big decision, and choosing the right estate agent in Derry can have a real impact on the result you get.

The right agent should do more than get your property listed online. They should understand the local market, give you honest advice, market your home properly, and keep the whole process moving from valuation to completion.

If you are thinking of selling, here are a few things worth checking before you decide who to trust with your home.

## 1. Choose someone who knows the Derry market properly
Not all agents approach the market in the same way.

A good estate agent in Derry should be able to explain:
- what similar homes are actually achieving
- what buyers are looking for right now
- how location affects demand
- what makes one property stand out over another

That local knowledge matters. A home in the city can attract a different kind of buyer than one in a nearby village or commuter area, and your pricing and marketing need to reflect that.

If an agent cannot clearly explain how they would position your property in the Derry market, that is usually a warning sign.

## 2. Ask how they will market your property
One of the biggest differences between agents is how seriously they take marketing.

Good marketing is not just a few photos and a listing going live.

You want to know:
- will professional photography be used?
- will there be a video walk-through?
- will the property be promoted properly on social media?
- will it appear on the main property portals?
- how will they make the listing stand out from competing homes?

Strong presentation can directly affect both enquiry levels and the quality of viewings.

That is especially important in a competitive market where buyers are scrolling quickly and comparing multiple homes at once.

## 3. Be careful of unrealistic valuations
It is always tempting to choose the agent who gives the highest valuation.

But the highest number is not always the best advice.

An inflated asking price can lead to:
- fewer early enquiries
- reduced momentum after launch
- longer time on the market
- more pressure to cut the price later

A better agent will explain their valuation clearly and back it up with evidence, buyer demand, and current local conditions.

If you want a realistic starting point, it is worth requesting a **free property valuation in Derry** before making any decision.

## 4. Look at communication, not just branding
A polished brand is great, but good communication is what sellers actually remember.

Before you instruct an agent, ask yourself:
- are they easy to get hold of?
- do they explain things clearly?
- do they sound honest?
- do they actually listen?
- will you be dealing with one person directly or being passed around?

Selling a home can move quickly, and missed calls, vague feedback, or poor follow-up can cost time and confidence.

A strong agent should keep you informed, give clear feedback after viewings, and stay proactive when offers come in.

## 5. Check reviews and proof of results
Reviews will not tell you everything, but they do help you spot patterns.

Look for comments about:
- communication
- speed of sale
- professionalism
- trust
- support during the full process

The best agents are usually the ones people mention by name when talking about how well they were looked after.

It is also worth checking whether the agent has a visible track record of selling and marketing homes in the local area, not just a logo and a sales pitch.

## 6. Make sure they understand your type of move
Every sale is a bit different.

You may be:
- upsizing
- downsizing
- relocating
- selling an investment property
- selling and buying at the same time

A good estate agent should adapt their advice around your timeline, your next move, and the level of support you need.

That matters even more if you want a strategy for launching the property, managing viewings, and negotiating seriously when interest comes in.

## 7. Look for a modern approach, not an outdated one
The property market has changed. Buyers now expect better presentation, quicker responses, and stronger digital marketing.

A modern estate agent in Derry should be thinking about:
- strong visuals
- mobile-first listings
- social media visibility
- fast response times
- clean, clear buyer communication

That does not mean style over substance.
It means using modern marketing properly to get more eyes on the property and better-quality enquiries.

## Why the right choice matters
The right agent can help you:
- launch at the right price
- attract stronger interest early
- avoid unnecessary delays
- negotiate with confidence
- move with less stress

The wrong one can leave your home sitting on the market with weak marketing, poor communication, and no real strategy behind the listing.

## Final thought
If you are comparing estate agents in Derry, do not just ask who can list your home.
Ask who can market it properly, price it realistically, communicate well, and guide the sale from start to finish.

That is what makes the difference.

If you are planning a move and want a realistic view of your home’s value, the best next step is to book a valuation and talk through your options properly.
