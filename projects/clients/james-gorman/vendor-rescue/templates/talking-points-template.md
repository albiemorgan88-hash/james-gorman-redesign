# Door-knock talking points — {{ADDRESS}}

## Opener

Hi, I’m James Gorman. I’m local and I work with homeowners around Derry. I noticed your property has been on the market for a while, so I put together a private example of how I’d relaunch it if you ever wanted a fresh approach.

I’m not here to pressure you — I just wanted to leave this with you. The QR code opens the example.

## If they ask “what would you change?”

The main thing I’d change is the story and presentation. Buyers need to quickly understand why this home is worth viewing, not just scroll through rooms. I’d sharpen the launch around:

- stronger first impression
- better buyer positioning
- clearer local selling points
- refreshed portal/social/database push
- a realistic plan for the next 14–30 days

## If they are interested

Would it be useful if I called back and walked you through the example properly? It only takes 10 minutes and there’s no obligation.

## Do not say

- Do not criticise the current agent by name.
- Do not promise a sale or price uplift.
- Do not imply private data access.
- Do not pressure the homeowner at the door.
