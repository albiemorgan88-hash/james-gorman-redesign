Executive Summary

James Gorman Property has a real SEO opportunity in Derry, Limavady, Eglinton and Dungiven, but the website is currently held back by weak URL structure, limited location-specific landing pages, and incomplete on-page optimisation.

This week focused on building the proper foundations for local search growth. We reviewed the live site, checked live UK search results, benchmarked the strongest local competitors, drafted the next content rollout, and prepared the technical SEO fixes needed to give Google clearer signals about what James Gorman Property offers and where it operates.

The biggest issue is structural rather than cosmetic. Right now, the website still exposes multiple blank-* URLs, several priority pages have weak titles, and there are no dedicated live landing pages targeting the four commercial location terms we are going after.

To fix that, we have now prepared a full first content pack: four location pages and four supporting blog articles, alongside a clear technical implementation list for metadata, slug cleanup, internal links, schema and homepage copy improvement.

What we found

  • James Gorman Property was not visible in the sampled top 10 organic results for the four target terms.
  • Derry is competitive; Eglinton looks the quickest short-term opportunity.
  • Blank slugs and weak page naming are still hurting index quality.
  • Homepage and service pages need stronger Derry-led SEO signals.

What we did

  • Completed competitor research and ranking snapshot.
  • Drafted technical SEO fixes for core pages.
  • Prepared 4 location pages and 4 support articles.
  • Tested Wix API access and documented live blockers.

    Once the pages are live, schema and internal links should be added quickly to strengthen the rollout.
