1. Executive Summary

Technical foundations are moving in the right direction

James Gorman Property is an estate agent in Derry with a clear local SEO goal: build visibility for estate agent derry and then expand reach into Limavady, Eglinton, and Dungiven.

Right now, the business is already performing strongly for branded search and holds position 1 for the core branded terms reviewed. The main gap is non-branded local discovery: James Gorman Property was not visible in the top 10 organic results sampled for any of the four target non-branded phrases.

This week focused on technical SEO foundations and content structure: improving page titles and meta descriptions, cleaning URL slugs, preparing redirects, replacing weak or placeholder content, and mapping the local page architecture needed to compete properly.

Key takeaway: branded demand is healthy, traffic is already coming through, and AI visibility is accelerating. The next growth step is turning that early momentum into dedicated local landing pages and stronger internal linking so James Gorman Property can compete for non-branded searches.
