3. Staff wellbeing / operations — burnout, resilience, Copilot / process improvement angle

## The 3 email campaigns to run first

### 1) Principal / VP campaign
**Audience**
- Principals
- Vice Principals
- School leaders with broad responsibility

**Angle**
- Whole-school outcomes
- Attendance, behaviour, staff pressure, inspection readiness
- Practical support rather than therapy jargon

**Subject-line style**
- Quick thought for school wellbeing support
- One idea for pupil and staff wellbeing this term
- A practical support offer for post-primary schools

**CTA**
- 15-minute call
- reply for workshop options

### 2) SENCO / Pastoral campaign
**Audience**
- SENCOs
- Pastoral leads
- Heads of Year
- Wellbeing / safeguarding contacts

**Angle**
- EBSA
- anxiety support
- resilience
- behaviour / emotional regulation
- parent and pupil workshops

**Subject-line style**
- Support for emotionally based school avoidance
- Workshop support for pupil anxiety and attendance
- A practical pastoral workshop idea

**CTA**
- send workshop overview
- book intro call

### 3) Staff wellbeing / Copilot operations campaign
**Audience**
- Staff wellbeing leads
- senior leadership
- operations-minded contacts

**Angle**
- teacher burnout
- staff wellbeing sessions
- Microsoft Copilot / school workflow improvement
- admin time reduction

**Subject-line style**
- Reducing admin pressure in schools
- A staff wellbeing support idea for this term
- Could Microsoft Copilot help with school admin pressure?

**CTA**
- demo / conversation
- send examples / case study
