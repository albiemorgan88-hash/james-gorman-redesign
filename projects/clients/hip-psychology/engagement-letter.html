1. Services

Blue Canvas AI will provide the following Business Development services for HIP Psychology:

  • Build targeted lead lists by scraping and enriching prospect data
  • Design, draft and run email outreach sequences from the client's email address
  • A/B test subject lines, messaging and send times to maximise open rates
  • Continuously optimise campaigns based on real engagement data
  • Manage outreach lists and pipeline directly inside the client's CRM (Zoho)

2. Reporting

The Provider will deliver a weekly report to the Client including:

  • Weekly performance report: list size, open rate, click rate, replies, bookings
  • Baseline CRM snapshot on day one so improvements are measurable
  • Summary of all work completed that week
  • Recommendations and next steps

4. Term & Termination

This agreement has no minimum term.

Either party may terminate by giving 1 calendar month's written notice (email is sufficient).

The agreement continues on a rolling monthly basis until terminated.

Start date: 1 April 2026

5. Intellectual Property

All work product, content, and materials created by Blue Canvas AI under this agreement are the property of HIP Psychology upon payment. This includes lead lists, outreach copy, campaign data, strategy documents, and any other deliverables.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any business information, strategies, data, or proprietary information shared during this engagement. This includes analytics data, outreach strategies, lead lists, pricing, and business plans. This obligation survives termination of the agreement.

7. Limitation of Liability

Blue Canvas AI will perform all services with reasonable skill and care. However:

  • Business Development results depend on many factors outside the Provider's control (recipient behaviour, market conditions, email deliverability, client's offering, etc.)
  • The Provider does not guarantee specific numbers of leads, meetings, or conversions
  • The Provider's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client in the 3 months preceding any claim

8. General

  • Communication: Day-to-day communication via phone, email, or WhatsApp as suits both parties.
  • Changes to scope: Any significant changes to the scope of services will be discussed and agreed in writing before implementation.
  • Governing law: This agreement is governed by the laws of Northern Ireland.
  • What we need from the client: Admin access to Zoho CRM, details of what they're selling, ideal buyer profile, permission to send outreach from their email.
