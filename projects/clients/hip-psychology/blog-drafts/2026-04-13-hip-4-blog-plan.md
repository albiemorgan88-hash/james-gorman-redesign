# HIP Psychology, 4-blog content plan

Date: 2026-04-13
Site: hippsychology.com
Audience: school leaders, pastoral leads, SENCOs, principals, vice-principals, wellbeing leads, and staff development decision-makers in Northern Ireland and ROI

## Chosen keywords and why

| Keyword | Ahrefs GB data | Why it was chosen | Overlap check |
|---|---:|---|---|
| emotionally based school avoidance | Volume 500, KD 3, TP 3700 | Best search opportunity in the set, very strong school intent, highly relevant to pastoral and attendance support, and a practical topic schools actively seek help with | Distinct from the existing student anxiety post because it focuses on attendance, patterns, and school response |
| mental health in schools | Volume 300, KD 24, TP 250 | Broad strategic keyword with strong relevance for school leaders planning whole-school support, policy, early identification, and referral pathways | Broader than the existing student anxiety and wellbeing workshop posts, so it can target a leadership and systems angle rather than a single issue |
| staff wellbeing in schools | Volume 100, KD 8, TP 300 | Slightly lower volume than teacher wellbeing, but more commercially aligned with HIP’s staff development offer and whole-school purchasing decisions | Distinct from pupil wellbeing content and closely aligned with HIP’s live NI staff workshop page |
| educational psychologist northern ireland | Volume 40, KD 1, TP 70 | Lower volume, but highly commercial and locally relevant. It gives HIP a strong Northern Ireland intent page for schools researching specialist support and complementary wellbeing provision | Distinct from existing posts. Article needs careful positioning so it informs schools without implying services that are not explicitly stated on the site |

## Keywords not selected

| Keyword | Reason not prioritised |
|---|---|
| student wellbeing | Strong keyword, but likely to overlap with both the existing wellbeing workshops post and the broader mental health in schools topic |
| teacher wellbeing | Good keyword, but staff wellbeing in schools is a better fit for school-level buyers and whole-staff workshop positioning |
| school refusal anxiety | Strong traffic potential, but closely overlaps with emotionally based school avoidance and risks cannibalisation |
| wellbeing in schools | Useful, but broader and more generic than mental health in schools, with greater overlap risk against existing wellbeing content |
| transition from primary to secondary school | Too close to the existing Year 8 transition support post |

## Planned articles

1. **Emotionally Based School Avoidance: A Practical Guide for Schools**
   - Target keyword: `emotionally based school avoidance`
   - Slug: `emotionally-based-school-avoidance`
   - Angle: practical school response, early signs, attendance planning, parent communication, when to involve outside support

2. **Mental Health in Schools: What Good Support Looks Like**
   - Target keyword: `mental health in schools`
   - Slug: `mental-health-in-schools`
   - Angle: whole-school approach, early identification, staff confidence, referral pathways, realistic leadership actions

3. **Staff Wellbeing in Schools: How to Build a Sustainable Plan**
   - Target keyword: `staff wellbeing in schools`
   - Slug: `staff-wellbeing-in-schools`
   - Angle: leadership strategy, culture, workload, support systems, CPD and resilience workshops

4. **Educational Psychologist Northern Ireland: What Schools Should Look For**
   - Target keyword: `educational psychologist northern ireland`
   - Slug: `educational-psychologist-northern-ireland`
   - Angle: informational guide for schools, when specialist psychological support is needed, what to ask, and how workshop-based support can complement wider provision
