# HIP Psychology, second 4-blog content plan

Date: 2026-04-15
Site: hippsychology.com
Audience: school leaders, pastoral leads, SENCOs, vice-principals, principals, wellbeing leads, and staff development buyers in Northern Ireland and ROI

## Chosen keywords and why

| Keyword | Ahrefs GB data | Why it was chosen | Overlap check |
|---|---:|---|---|
| anti bullying week | Volume 2500, KD 14, TP 3100 | Biggest reachable school keyword in the set, highly seasonal, and a strong fit for HIP's anti-bullying workshop offer | Distinct from the live anti-bullying workshops post because this piece focuses on planning, timing and how schools can use the week well |
| teacher burnout | Volume 400, KD 11, TP 250 | Strong search volume with direct relevance to staff development and leadership support | Distinct from the live staff wellbeing in schools post because this piece focuses specifically on identifying and responding to burnout |
| mental health workshops for schools | Volume 150, KD n/a, CPC 140 | Clear service-intent keyword that maps closely to HIP's school workshop offer | Distinct from the live mental health in schools post because this piece focuses on choosing and structuring workshops rather than whole-school mental health strategy |
| resilience workshop | Volume 200, KD 0, TP 70 | Commercially useful workshop keyword that matches HIP's resilience-based delivery across pupils and staff | Distinct from the live wellbeing and anxiety posts because it focuses specifically on what resilience workshops should include and when schools should use them |

## Keywords not selected

| Keyword | Reason not prioritised |
|---|---|
| teacher wellbeing | Lower volume than teacher burnout and too close to the existing staff wellbeing post |
| student wellbeing | Useful, but likely to overlap with wellbeing workshops for schools |
| wellbeing in schools | Good keyword, but already partially covered by the live wellbeing and mental health posts |
| transition to secondary school | Too close to the live Year 8 transition support article |
| anti bullying activities | Useful supporting term, but weaker commercial fit than anti bullying week |

## Planned articles

1. **Anti-Bullying Week: How Schools Can Make It Count**
   - Target keyword: `anti bullying week`
   - Slug: `anti-bullying-week-schools`
   - Angle: practical planning guide for schools, how to make Anti-Bullying Week more than a poster campaign, and where workshops fit

2. **Teacher Burnout in Schools: Early Signs and Practical Support**
   - Target keyword: `teacher burnout`
   - Slug: `teacher-burnout-in-schools`
   - Angle: leadership guide to spotting burnout, reducing avoidable pressure, and supporting staff before people hit empty

3. **Mental Health Workshops for Schools: What to Look For and What Works**
   - Target keyword: `mental health workshops for schools`
   - Slug: `mental-health-workshops-for-schools`
   - Angle: service-intent guide for school buyers comparing workshop options and trying to build a joined-up pastoral offer

4. **Resilience Workshops for Schools: What Good Delivery Looks Like**
   - Target keyword: `resilience workshop`
   - Slug: `resilience-workshops-for-schools`
   - Angle: practical guide on resilience workshops for pupils and staff, including what strong sessions include and how schools can use them well
