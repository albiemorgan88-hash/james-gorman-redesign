# Contact Research & Enrichment Strategy

## Contact Finding Methodology

### Priority Contact Types (in order):
1. **Club Secretary** - Handles all admin, likely decision maker
2. **Treasurer** - Handles finances, will love recurring revenue  
3. **Chairman/President** - Final approval authority
4. **Committee Members** - Can influence decision
5. **Coaches/Managers** - Close to membership, good for referrals

### Research Sources & Order of Approach

#### 1. Club Websites (Start here - highest accuracy)
- Look for "Committee" or "Contact" pages
- Annual reports often list full committee
- Download and store any contact PDFs
- Note: many sites outdated, cross-reference with other sources

**Search Terms for Club Websites:**
- "[Club Name] committee members"
- "[Club Name] secretary contact"
- "[Club Name] officials"
- "site:[clubwebsite.com] secretary OR treasurer OR committee"

#### 2. Facebook Pages & Groups (Most current info)
- Official club pages - check "About" section and pinned posts
- Look for AGM announcements (lists new committee)
- Committee election posts
- Event posts often tagged with organizers
- Private club groups (if accessible)

**Facebook Search Strategy:**
- Search club name + "GAA" / "Cricket Club" / "RFC"
- Look at page admins (usually committee members)
- Check tagged photos from AGMs or events
- Search within page for "secretary" "treasurer" "committee"

#### 3. LinkedIn (Professional contacts)
- Search "[Club Name] secretary" or "[Club Name] treasurer"
- Look for people with club affiliations in bio
- Sports industry connections
- Local business owners often involved in clubs

**LinkedIn Search Terms:**
- "Secretary at [Club Name]"
- "Treasurer [Club Name]"
- "[Club Name] committee"
- "GAA club committee Northern Ireland"
- "Cricket club official Ulster"

#### 4. Local Newspapers & Sports Websites
- Match reports often mention club officials
- AGM reports and election results
- Fundraising event coverage
- Award ceremonies and presentations

**Sites to Check:**
- BBC Sport NI
- Belfast Telegraph sport section
- Ulster Herald
- Derry Journal
- Local council websites (often sponsor clubs)
- Club championship websites

#### 5. Google/Directory Searches
- Google "[Club Name] secretary email"
- Companies House (if club is incorporated)
- Charity Commission (if registered charity)
- Local council grant recipient lists

#### 6. Sports Association Directories
- **Cricket:** NCU and NWCU contact lists (password protected)
- **GAA:** County board websites sometimes list club contacts
- **Rugby:** Ulster Rugby club finder (limited info)
- **Football:** IFA club directories

#### 7. Social Media Deep Dive
- Instagram: club accounts, hashtags, tagged locations
- Twitter/X: local sports journalists, club accounts
- WhatsApp: if you know any members, ask for contacts
- TikTok: younger committee members might be here

### Contact Verification Process

Before reaching out, verify contacts:

1. **Email Verification:**
   - Use Hunter.io or similar to verify email exists
   - Check for bounces on first send
   - Cross-reference with LinkedIn if possible

2. **Phone Number Validation:**
   - Quick Google search to confirm it's not a business line
   - Check if it matches other club communications

3. **Role Confirmation:**
   - Check if still current (committees change annually)
   - Look for recent posts/activities confirming role

### Contact Approach Strategy

#### Warm Introductions (Best conversion):
- Phil's connections in NW Cricket League
- Mutual club contacts
- Local business connections
- Sports association contacts

#### Cold Email Approach:
- Personalize first line with recent club achievement/news
- Reference specific club details (ground name, recent results)
- Keep initial email under 100 words
- Always include Ardmore success story

#### Social Media Approach:
- Connect on LinkedIn first with personalized message
- Comment positively on club posts before reaching out
- Share relevant content to build rapport

### Legal & GDPR Compliance

#### Data Handling:
- Only collect publicly available contact information
- Store securely with access controls
- Delete after 12 months if no engagement
- Provide easy opt-out in all communications

#### Email Compliance:
- Include unsubscribe link in all emails
- Honor opt-out requests immediately
- Use legitimate interest basis for initial contact
- Keep records of consent and opt-outs

### Success Metrics

#### Data Quality Metrics:
- **Contact accuracy rate:** >80% of emails should be valid
- **Role accuracy:** >70% should be current position holders
- **Response rate:** Target 15%+ from cold outreach
- **Bounce rate:** Keep under 5%

#### Efficiency Metrics:
- **Research time per club:** Target <30 minutes
- **Contacts found per club:** Minimum 2, target 3+
- **Source effectiveness:** Track which sources yield best contacts
- **Conversion rate by source:** Email vs social vs phone

### Seasonal Considerations

#### Best Times for Contact:
- **Cricket:** March-April (pre-season planning)
- **GAA:** February-March (AGM season)
- **Rugby:** August-September (season start)
- **All sports:** Avoid December-January (holidays)

#### Avoid These Periods:
- During championship finals
- School holiday periods
- Major tournament weeks
- Christmas/New Year period

### Quality Assurance Checklist

Before marking a club as "Research Complete":

- [ ] At least 2 verified contacts found
- [ ] Primary contact (Secretary/Treasurer) confirmed
- [ ] Email address format verified
- [ ] Phone number (if available) cross-checked
- [ ] Recent club activity/news found for personalization
- [ ] Club website and social media links saved
- [ ] Ground/venue name confirmed
- [ ] Estimated membership size noted
- [ ] Any warm intro possibilities identified
- [ ] Best approach method determined (email/social/phone)

This systematic approach should yield 80+ qualified club contacts within the first month, with strong personalization data for effective outreach.
