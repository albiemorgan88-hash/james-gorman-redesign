## Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

### Potential Obstacles & Solutions

**Low email response rates (<10%):**
- Increase personalization depth
- Add warm intro approaches via LinkedIn
- Shift to phone-first strategy for high-value targets

**Demo sites not driving conversions:**
- A/B test different revenue projections
- Add more interactive elements
- Include video testimonials from Ardmore

**Clubs hesitant about legal/compliance:**
- Create detailed compliance guide
- Offer to handle council registration process
- Get legal opinion letter for credibility

**Competition or market saturation:**
- Focus on relationship-based selling
- Emphasize local success story (Ardmore)
- Create exclusive launch incentives

### Success Amplification Strategies

**If early conversions exceed targets:**
- Accelerate research into new sports/regions
- Develop referral incentive program
- Create video case studies for wider reach
- Expand into Republic of Ireland market

**Leverage network effects:**
- Encourage club-to-club referrals
- Target clubs in same leagues as successful clients
- Use match fixtures as networking opportunities
- Build relationships with sports association officials
