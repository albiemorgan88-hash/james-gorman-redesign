## Quick Customization Guide

1. **Replace bracketed items:**
   - [FIRST_NAME] → Paul, Margaret, Christine, etc.
   - [COMPANY_NAME] → Full company name as it appears officially
   - [PAIN_POINT] → Choose from list in main template
   - [SHOWCASE_URL] → bluecanvas.ai/showcase/[company-slug]

2. **Company slug format:**
   - EF McCambridge & Co → ef-mccambridge
   - MJ Kane Chartered Accountants → mj-kane
   - Generally: lowercase, hyphens, drop formal endings

3. **Subject line selection:**
   - A/B test the different options
   - Track open rates
   - Adjust based on performance
