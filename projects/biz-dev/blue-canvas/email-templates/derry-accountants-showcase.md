## Usage Notes

### Northern Ireland Context
- Keep it local and relatable
- Reference "across Northern Ireland" to show local expertise
- Use "no bother" instead of "no problem" (local phrase)
- Phone number with local format

### Tone Guidelines
- Professional but not stiff
- Direct and honest
- Value-first approach
- No aggressive sales language
- Respectful of their time

### Personalization Points
1. Always use their first name
2. Reference their actual company name
3. Mention a specific, real gap you've identified
4. Custom showcase URL just for them

### Follow-up Sequence (Optional)
If no response after 5 days:

**Follow-up Subject:** `{{first_name}}, did you get a chance to check that showcase for {{company_name}}?`

**Follow-up Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Just following up on the showcase I sent over for {{company_name}} - did you get a chance to have a look?

I know you're busy, so no worries if it's not a priority right now.

If you did check it out and have any questions, I'm happy to have a quick chat.

Best,
Phil
```
