### For Timeline Pressures:
"I understand you need this completed quickly. I can prioritize your project and deliver in [timeframe] with a 20% rush fee. The quality remains the same - I just allocate additional resources to meet your deadline."

### For Scope Clarification:
"Great question! Based on my experience with similar projects, I'd recommend [specific approach] because [reason based on results]. This ensures you get [specific benefit]. Happy to jump on a quick call to clarify the best approach for your specific situation."

### For Competition/Price Shopping:
"I focus on delivering measurable business results rather than competing on price. My recent client [specific example] would tell you the ROI was worth every penny. I'm confident I can deliver similar results for your business. Shall we discuss your specific goals?"
