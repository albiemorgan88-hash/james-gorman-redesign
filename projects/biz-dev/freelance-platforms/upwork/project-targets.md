## Proposal Customization Framework

### For AI/Automation Projects
**Opening hook:** "I've automated [similar process] for [similar business type] resulting in [specific time/cost savings]"
**Credibility:** Blue Canvas track record with £4M+ client expansion
**Solution:** Specific automation approach for their process
**Timeline:** Clear weekly milestones
**Guarantee:** 30-day optimization period included

### For Website/SEO Projects
**Opening hook:** "Just delivered 300% traffic increase for [similar business] through strategic website + SEO"
**Credibility:** UK Trade Jobs case study + James Gorman results
**Solution:** Technical approach + SEO strategy
**Portfolio:** Direct links to live examples
**Bonus:** 3 months SEO monitoring included

### For Google Ads Projects
**Opening hook:** "Improved ROAS by 300% for recent [industry] client while reducing cost-per-lead"
**Credibility:** Google Ads certification + real results
**Solution:** Audit → strategy → optimization approach
**Guarantee:** 90-day improvement guarantee or fee refund
**Structure:** Clear monthly reporting and strategy calls

## Red Flag Projects to Avoid

### Scope Creep Risks
- "Build a website like Amazon/Facebook"
- Vague requirements with low budgets
- "Need it done yesterday" with complex scope
- Multiple decision makers without clear authority

### Low-Value Indicators
- Budgets under £300 for complex projects
- "Looking for cheapest option"
- Poor English/communication
- No clear business case or urgency

### Platform Risks
- Clients with poor review history
- Payment verification issues
- Projects requiring upfront payments
- Clients asking to communicate off-platform immediately

## Client Communication Strategy

### Response Times
- **Within 30 minutes:** During UK business hours (9am-6pm GMT)
- **Within 2 hours:** Evenings and early mornings
- **Within 4 hours:** Weekends and holidays

### Question Framework for Client Calls
1. **Current situation:** What's the biggest challenge you're facing?
2. **Attempted solutions:** What have you tried before?
3. **Success metrics:** How will we measure success?
4. **Timeline:** What's driving the urgency?
5. **Budget:** What investment level makes sense for this ROI?

### Closing Techniques
- **Assumption close:** "When would you like to start?"
- **Options close:** "Would you prefer the basic or comprehensive package?"
- **Urgency close:** "I have availability starting Monday - shall we secure that slot?"
