## Week 2-4: Review Building Phase

### Application Strategy
**Daily target:** 3-5 quality applications
**Response time:** Within 2 hours during business hours
**Pricing strategy:** Start 20% below target rate to win first projects

### Proposal Quality Framework
1. **Hook:** Address their specific pain point in first line
2. **Credibility:** Mention relevant recent result
3. **Solution:** Specific approach for their project
4. **Social proof:** Similar project success story
5. **CTA:** Clear next step (call/discussion)

### Project Delivery Excellence
- **Communication:** Daily updates on progress
- **Quality:** Exceed expectations on first deliverable
- **Speed:** Deliver ahead of agreed timeline
- **Value-add:** Include bonus elements not in scope
- **Follow-up:** Check satisfaction 1 week after completion

## Month 1: Establish Credibility

### Target Metrics
- **PPH:** 3-5 completed projects, all 5-star reviews
- **Upwork:** 5-7 completed projects, 100% job success score
- **Revenue:** £2,000-4,000 total across both platforms
- **Reviews:** 8-12 detailed reviews highlighting AI/automation expertise

### Rate Increase Strategy
**Week 4:** Increase rates by 25% after 3x 5-star reviews
**Week 6:** Another 25% increase after 6x 5-star reviews
**Week 8:** Move to target rates (£50-75/hour, £1,500+ project minimums)

## Risk Mitigation

### Platform Risk
- **Diversified presence:** Don't rely on one platform
- **Direct relationships:** Move good clients to direct contracts
- **Portfolio building:** Use platforms to build case studies
- **Skills documentation:** Document all capabilities publicly

### Quality Control
- **Project screening:** Only take projects you can excel at
- **Scope management:** Clear boundaries and deliverables
- **Communication:** Over-communicate progress and expectations
- **Backup plans:** Have contingencies for every project
