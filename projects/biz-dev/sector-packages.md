**"We get enough work through word of mouth"**
> "That's brilliant — means you're doing great work. But your competitors are now showing up on Google too. When someone new to the area searches, they won't know about your reputation. This just makes sure they find you."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "Depends on what you need, but most local businesses are looking at £200-350/month. And if you're NI-based, the DTFF grant covers up to 70% of the setup costs. Want me to run through the numbers?"

**"I tried SEO before and it didn't work"**
> "Honestly, a lot of SEO agencies are rubbish. I'm happy to show you exactly what I'd do differently — and I'll build you a free showcase first so you can see it before you spend a penny."
