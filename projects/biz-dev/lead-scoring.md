# Blue Canvas AI — Lead Scoring System

Score each lead 1-10 based on the criteria below. Higher = contact sooner.

---

## Scoring Matrix

### Critical Issues (3 points each)

| Signal | Points | How to check |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Website down / 404 / DNS error | +3 | Visit the URL |
| No website at all | +3 | Google the business name |
| No Google Business Profile (GBP) | +3 | Search "[business] [location]" on Google Maps |

### High Impact (2 points each)

| Signal | Points | How to check |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| PageSpeed score under 50 | +2 | pagespeed.web.dev |
| Not ranking for primary keyword | +2 | Search "[service] [location]" — are they on page 1? |
| GBP listing unclaimed/incomplete | +2 | Check Google Maps listing |
| No reviews or <5 reviews on Google | +2 | Google Maps |
| Mobile site broken/unusable | +2 | Google Mobile-Friendly Test |

### Medium Impact (1 point each)

| Signal | Points | How to check |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Competitors visibly outranking them | +1 | Search main keywords |
| No SSL certificate (http not https) | +1 | Visit the URL |
| Outdated website design (pre-2018 look) | +1 | Visual check |
| No social media presence | +1 | Search Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn |
| In a sector that can't use Google Ads | +1 | Healthcare, beauty, legal (restricted) |

### Bonus Multipliers

| Signal | Multiplier | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| DTFF grant eligible (NI-based SME) | ×1.2 | Makes pricing conversation easier |
| Decision maker found on LinkedIn | ×1.1 | Can do multi-channel outreach |
| Competitor is a Blue Canvas client | ×1.3 | Social proof angle |
| Recently left a negative review about their current provider | ×1.5 | They're actively unhappy |

---

## Priority Tiers

| Score | Tier | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| 9-10 | 🔴 **Hot** | Build showcase + email + LinkedIn TODAY |
| 7-8 | 🟠 **Warm** | Contact this week |
| 5-6 | 🟡 **Lukewarm** | Add to pipeline, contact when capacity allows |
| 3-4 | 🟢 **Cool** | Note for later, nurture via LinkedIn content |
| 1-2 | ⚪ **Cold** | Don't bother unless they come inbound |

---

## Sector Weighting

Some sectors are better targets than others right now:

| Sector | Base bonus | Reason |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Estate agents | +2 | Proven case study (James Gorman), high competition |
| Solicitors | +1 | High-value clients, DTFF angle, professional image matters |
| Healthcare/Beauty | +2 | Can't use Google Ads — SEO is their ONLY channel |
| Trades (plumbers, electricians) | +1 | Local SEO + Google Maps = immediate ROI |
| Hospitality | +0 | Good but seasonal, lower budgets |
| Retail | -1 | Competing with Amazon, harder sell |

---

## When to Re-Score

- After initial outreach with no response: -2 points
- After follow-up with no response: -3 points (move to Cool)
- After 30 days cold: reset to original score and try again
- If they respond positively to anything: automatic 🔴 Hot
