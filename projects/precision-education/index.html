Overview

Precision Education offers educational strategy solutions for partner organisations, and private individuals.

Our Work

Precision Education works with a diverse portfolio of clients, including national governments, academy trusts, and private individuals. We work to devise educational strategy for our clients, and we accompany this with practical implementation support. Examples of our work include:

  • Educational reform at a national level by drawing upon and implementing successful models for system assurance, via qualification and assessment reform, curriculum review and school inspection
  • School budget management support via Curriculum Led Financial Management
  • Development and Implementation of knowledge-rich curricula, via media of explicit teacher instruction
  • Parental choice support, via group and individual sessions which support parents around the question of school choice for their children
  • Coaching of engaged individuals, including Headteachers and Senior Leaders, and other individuals with an interest in career development

Our solutions draw upon the models established by the top performing trusts and schools in the UK system, with a focus on practical implementation solutions.
