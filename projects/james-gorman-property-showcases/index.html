The seller problem

Most homes get uploaded. Better homes need to be wanted.

A premium family home should not rely on buyers spotting it by chance. It needs the right first impression, a clear reason to view, and follow-up that keeps serious buyers warm instead of letting interest fade.

1
Make buyers remember the home

Lead with the reasons people move: privacy, family space, village life, garden quality, double garage and flexible reception rooms.

2
Make the property feel premium

Use the strongest shots in the right order, with richer crops and cleaner contrast, so the home feels worth the price before the viewing.

3
Turn interest into conversations

Every QR scan, social click and enquiry should give a motivated buyer an easy next step toward a viewing or direct conversation with James.

First week plan

Here is what happens when your home is properly launched.

For sellers receiving the letter, the point is simple: James can make the home look stronger, reach more relevant buyers, and keep the best prospects engaged beyond the first click.

Day 1
Find the reason buyers will remember it

Shape the lead angle around the emotional reasons people move: privacy, scale, family flexibility and Eglinton convenience.

Day 2
Create the premium first impression

Build the hero gallery, mobile page, social cuts and direct enquiry links so every buyer lands on a stronger version of the home.

Day 3
Put the home in front of local buyers

Launch short video, Facebook posts and Instagram stories before the weekend window, with each post giving people a reason to view.

Day 4-5
Follow up while interest is warm

Contact active buyers, previous valuation leads and people who interacted with the campaign before they move on to another home.

Day 6-7
Show the seller what is working

Report views, clicks, viewing requests, comments and buyer feedback so the seller can see the launch is being actively managed.
