# Page brief

## Working page concept
A Blue Canvas landing page and embedded self-check that helps businesses quickly assess whether they may need to act on the EU AI Act, then converts that attention into a consultation enquiry.

## Target audience
Primary audience:
- founders and managing directors of SMEs and mid-sized firms
- heads of operations, digital, product, innovation, or compliance
- teams already using AI tools internally or in customer-facing workflows
- software, SaaS, agencies, consultancies, professional services, e-commerce, recruiters, and B2B service firms

Secondary audience:
- UK businesses that sell into the EU or support EU clients
- businesses buying AI from vendors and unsure what they need to evidence
- teams preparing for procurement, due diligence, or customer compliance questionnaires

Best-fit visitor mindset:
- knows the EU AI Act is a thing but is fuzzy on what it means in practice
- wants clarity quickly
- does not want a dense legal explainer
- is open to outside help if the gap looks real

## Messaging hierarchy
1. **You do not need to guess.**
   The page should reduce confusion fast.
2. **The Act may still matter even if you are not an EU company.**
   If you sell into the EU, deploy systems there, or your outputs affect people in the EU, you may have exposure.
3. **This is about readiness, not scare tactics.**
   The tool gives a practical view of likely pressure points.
4. **The August 2026 timing matters.**
   As at April 14, 2026, most remaining obligations are due from 2 August 2026.
5. **Blue Canvas can help turn the result into an action plan.**
   The consultation is the commercial next step.

## Brand stance
How Blue Canvas should come across:
- commercial and credible
- plain English, not legalese
- calm, useful, and pragmatic
- direct about risk, without being alarmist
- focused on action, documentation, governance, and implementation support

What the page should not feel like:
- a law firm article
- a fear-based compliance trap
- a generic lead magnet with no substance
- a definitive legal compliance checker

## Positioning line
Blue Canvas helps businesses turn AI compliance confusion into a practical readiness plan.

## Main conversion action
Primary conversion:
- submit details to receive the detailed result and recommended next steps

Secondary conversion:
- book a consultation call

## Recommended page sections in order
1. Hero
2. Short credibility and timing strip
3. The self-check tool
4. Results preview + gated detailed result
5. Who this is for
6. What the check covers
7. EU AI Act timing section
8. Why Blue Canvas / consultation CTA
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

## Section intent
### 1) Hero
Make the value obvious in under five seconds.

### 2) Credibility and timing strip
Frame urgency carefully using the verified dates and caveat that timing may evolve.

### 3) Self-check tool
Get the user into motion quickly. This is the page's centre of gravity.

### 4) Results preview + gated detailed result
Show enough value to feel real, then ask for details to unlock the deeper recommendation.

### 5) Who this is for
Help visitors self-qualify.

### 6) What the check covers
Show breadth: exposure, use cases, governance, documentation, oversight, vendor controls.

### 7) EU AI Act timing section
Anchor urgency with exact dates, phrased as at April 14, 2026.

### 8) Why Blue Canvas / consultation CTA
Reassure visitors there is practical help after the self-check.

### 9) FAQ
Handle standard objections and uncertainty.

### 10) Final CTA
Give people one last low-friction route into the checker or a call.

## SEO / intent angle
This should work first as a conversion page, second as a search landing page.
Potential intent themes:
- EU AI Act compliance checker
- AI Act readiness assessment
- does the EU AI Act apply to my business
- AI governance consultation

## Success criteria
The page is working if it:
- gets users into the checker quickly
- explains the Act in plain English
- creates credible urgency without overclaiming
- produces qualified consultation leads, not junk submissions
- makes Blue Canvas look like a practical implementation partner
