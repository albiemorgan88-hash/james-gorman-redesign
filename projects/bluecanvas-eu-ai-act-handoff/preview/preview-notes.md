# Blue Canvas EU AI Act preview notes

## What this preview is
A polished static concept mockup for the Blue Canvas EU AI Act readiness checker / lead capture page.

## Design direction used
- calm, premium, modern Blue Canvas feel
- lead-capture and checker first, not article-first
- gradients, glass panels, and geometric UI shapes instead of legal-tech clichés
- British English and careful compliance wording throughout
- useful result preview before the gated form

## Included in the mockup
- hero with dual CTA
- key dates / timing strip
- interactive-style checker preview in the hero
- fuller checker section
- result preview block
- gated form block
- who it is for
- what the check covers
- timing section
- Blue Canvas consultation block
- FAQ and final CTA cues

## Notes for handoff
- The hero panel already demonstrates the intended one-question-at-a-time interaction pattern.
- The main checker section shows how the live product flow should feel after a few answers are in.
- Result language stays in "readiness" territory and avoids certification / compliance claims.
- Dates are anchored as at 14 April 2026 and include the 19 November 2025 simplification caveat.
