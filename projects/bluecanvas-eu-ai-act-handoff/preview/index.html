Key dates to know

As at 14 April 2026, the AI Act has already phased in key duties, with most remaining obligations due from 2 August 2026.

Exact dates, careful wording
1 Aug 2024
AI Act entered into force.
2 Feb 2025
Prohibited practices, definitions, and AI literacy provisions applied.
2 Aug 2025
Governance rules and GPAI obligations applied.
2 Aug 2026
Most remaining obligations are due.
2 Aug 2027
Some product safety and Annex I-linked obligations follow later.

The European Commission proposed simplification changes on 19 November 2025, so timing and scope messaging should stay practical and avoid false certainty.

FAQ cues

Handle the usual objections without bloating the page

Does the EU AI Act apply if we are based in the UK?+

It can. If your AI systems, services, or outputs are placed on the EU market or affect people in the EU, the Act may still matter.

Does this checker tell us whether we are compliant?+

No. It gives a practical readiness view based on your answers. It is designed to highlight likely areas for review, not provide formal legal advice.

Do we need this if we only use third-party AI tools?+

Possibly, yes. Even if you are not building models yourself, your use of AI, your governance, and your vendor controls can still matter.

What happens after I submit my details?+

You receive a fuller result summary and, where relevant, a recommendation to speak with Blue Canvas about the next practical steps.
