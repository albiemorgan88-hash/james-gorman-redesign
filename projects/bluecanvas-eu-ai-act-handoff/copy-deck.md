## Timing / credibility strip
### Strip heading
Key dates to know

### Strip copy
As at April 14, 2026:
- The AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024.
- Prohibited practices, definitions, and AI literacy provisions have applied since 2 February 2025.
- Governance rules and GPAI obligations have applied since 2 August 2025.
- Most remaining obligations are due from 2 August 2026.
- Some Annex I-linked and product safety obligations are due from 2 August 2027.

Small-print note:
The European Commission proposed simplification changes on 19 November 2025, so timing and scope copy should stay careful and avoid overclaiming certainty.

## FAQ
### Q1
Does the EU AI Act apply if we are based in the UK?

### A1
It can. If your AI systems, services, or outputs are placed on the EU market or affect people in the EU, the Act may still matter.

### Q2
Does this checker tell us whether we are compliant?

### A2
No. It gives a practical readiness view based on your answers. It is designed to highlight likely areas for review, not provide formal legal advice.

### Q3
How long does the check take?

### A3
Around three minutes for most users.

### Q4
Do we need this if we only use third-party AI tools?

### A4
Possibly, yes. Even if you are not building models yourself, your use of AI, your governance, and your vendor controls can still matter.
