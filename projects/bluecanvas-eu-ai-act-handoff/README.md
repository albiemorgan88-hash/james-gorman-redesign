# Blue Canvas EU AI Act handoff pack

## What this pack is
A practical handoff bundle for building a lead-generating EU AI Act readiness self-check on bluecanvas.ai.

It is designed so Niall can move from brief to build without needing loads of clarification. The page should feel commercial, useful, and credible, not like a law firm microsite.

## Goal of the page
Turn relevant visitors into qualified consultation leads by giving them a quick, plain-English view of where they may need attention under the EU AI Act.

This is not a legal determination tool. It is a readiness and triage experience that:
- helps visitors understand whether the Act is likely relevant to them
- surfaces obvious governance gaps
- creates urgency around the August 2026 deadline window
- moves strong-fit visitors into a consultation with Blue Canvas

## Main CTA / lead capture goal
Primary CTA:
- Start the self-check

Lead capture goal:
- collect qualified consultation enquiries after the checker returns a result
- show a headline result immediately
- gate the detailed summary and follow-up recommendations behind the form

Secondary CTA:
- Book a consultation

## Core page promise
In a few minutes, a visitor should be able to answer:
- Does the EU AI Act likely matter for us?
- Are we low, medium, or high priority for action?
- What should we do next?

## Recommended build order
1. Read `page-brief.md` for positioning and audience.
2. Use `wireframe.md` to map the page structure and component order.
3. Drop in `copy-deck.md` for on-page content and CTA language.
4. Build the checker using `checker-logic.md`.
5. Apply `implementation-notes.md` for Webflow/frontend behaviour.
6. Sanity-check all compliance/timing copy against `legal-notes.md`.
7. QA the full flow on mobile, then desktop.

## Suggested finished experience
- one landing page on bluecanvas.ai
- strong hero with a clear self-check CTA
- 10 to 12 question checker
- result bucket with practical next steps
- lead form to unlock detailed recommendations
- consultation CTA for higher-intent users
- careful legal wording throughout

- a results state that feeds the lead form
