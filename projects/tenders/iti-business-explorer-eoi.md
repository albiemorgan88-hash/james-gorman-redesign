## About Blue Canvas

Blue Canvas is an AI consultancy based in Derry, Northern Ireland, focused exclusively on helping SMEs adopt AI and digital technologies in practical, measurable ways. We bridge the gap between what AI can theoretically do and what it actually delivers for businesses with real constraints — limited budgets, small teams, and no in-house data science capability.

We are **Cyber Essentials certified** and currently progressing applications to Crown Commercial Service frameworks (RM6200 AI Dynamic Purchasing System and RM6173 Automation Marketplace), reflecting our commitment to operating at recognised public-sector standards.

## Why Blue Canvas for Business Explorer

**Practical, not theoretical.** We implement. Our clients get working systems, not research papers. Every engagement starts with commercial reality — what will actually move the needle for this business?

**SME-native.** We work exclusively with SMEs. We understand the constraints, the pace, and the decision-making. We don't need to "translate" enterprise approaches downward — we build for SMEs from the ground up.

**Cross-sector capability.** Our experience spans construction, healthcare, professional services, and manufacturing. AI and digitalisation challenges are often structurally similar across sectors; what differs is context. We're skilled at rapid domain familiarisation and pragmatic solution design.

**Delivery-ready.** For the Explore phase (5-day engagement), we bring a proven rapid-assessment methodology: current-state mapping, opportunity identification, feasibility scoring, and a prioritised action plan — all within the programme timeframe.

**For the Plan phase**, we deliver detailed implementation roadmaps including technology selection, integration architecture, cost-benefit analysis, and phased delivery plans that SMEs can actually execute.
