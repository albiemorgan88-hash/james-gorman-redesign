## 1. Executive Summary

Blue Canvas AI is pleased to submit this tender response for InterTradeIreland's research contract on AI Adoption, Opportunities, and Impact for SMEs across the island of Ireland.

We bring a distinctive perspective to this research: Blue Canvas AI is not an academic institution or a large consultancy theorising about AI adoption — we are a practitioner-led AI consultancy that works with SMEs on AI integration every single day. We understand the opportunities, barriers, and realities of SME AI adoption because we live them.

Founded in March 2025 by Phil Patterson and based in Derry — uniquely positioned on the border between both jurisdictions — Blue Canvas AI has deep connections across the Northern Ireland and Ireland business ecosystems. Our cross-border perspective, combined with hands-on AI implementation experience, makes us ideally placed to deliver research that is not only rigorous and evidence-based, but also grounded in the practical realities facing SMEs.

**Our approach:**
- A comprehensive all-island landscape analysis that synthesises existing research and identifies genuine gaps
- Primary research (200+ SME survey, 15–20 stakeholder interviews, 2 facilitated roundtables, 8–10 SME case studies) providing fresh evidence
- A clear three-tier framework distinguishing AI experimentation from transformational adoption
- International benchmarking against leading small economies
- Specific, costed, actionable recommendations aligned to InterTradeIreland's strategic objectives

**Our model:** Lean, agile, and efficient. Every pound goes into research delivery, not organisational overhead. Blue Canvas leads with deep subject-matter expertise, supported by specialist subcontractors for quantitative research and report production.

**Total cost:** £72,000 + VAT — delivering exceptional value against the £95,000 budget ceiling.
