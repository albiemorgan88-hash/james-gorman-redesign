# AC-4: Role of the Public Sector as a Driver of SME Innovation (10%)

## Overview

Blue Canvas AI will examine how public sector demand-side levers — procurement, open calls, and pilot programmes — can stimulate SME AI adoption across both jurisdictions, with practical recommendations drawn from international best practice.

## Methodology

### 4.1 Mapping Current Public Sector Demand-Side Mechanisms (Weeks 4–6)

We will conduct a comprehensive audit of existing mechanisms in both jurisdictions:

**Northern Ireland:**
- CPD (Construction & Procurement Delivery) innovation procurement provisions
- Invest NI challenge-led programmes
- DfE innovation policy instruments
- Local council digital transformation projects and SME involvement
- Health & Social Care innovation challenges

**Ireland:**
- Office of Government Procurement (OGP) innovation partnership procedures
- Enterprise Ireland SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research) programme
- Public service innovation challenges (e.g., GovTech initiatives)
- HSE innovation procurement
- Local authority digital projects

**Cross-border:**
- InterTradeIreland's existing innovation programmes and their AI component
- EU-funded cross-border programmes (PEACEPLUS innovation strands)
- North-South research collaborations with SME participation

For each mechanism we will assess: scale, accessibility for SMEs, AI relevance, and demonstrated impact.

### 4.2 Stakeholder Engagement — Public Sector Perspective

Targeted interviews with:
- Procurement policy leads in CPD and OGP
- Innovation programme managers in Invest NI and Enterprise Ireland
- InterTradeIreland programme delivery teams
- 3–5 SMEs that have participated in public sector innovation procurement (success stories and challenges)
- 3–5 SMEs that attempted to participate but faced barriers

This dual perspective (buyer and supplier) ensures recommendations address both sides of the market.

### 4.3 International Comparisons

We will analyse leading international models for public sector-driven SME innovation:

| Country | Programme | Key Feature | Transferability |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **UK (England)** | GovTech Catalyst | Challenge-led funding for SME tech solutions to public sector problems | High — similar governance structures |
| **Scotland** | CivTech | Accelerator model connecting public bodies with tech SMEs | Very high — devolved context, similar scale |
| **Denmark** | SME:Digital + public sector digital procurement | Coordinated demand-supply approach | High — small economy model |
| **Estonia** | GovStack / e-Estonia procurement | Digital-first public services creating market for SME AI solutions | Medium — different digital maturity baseline |
| **Netherlands** | StartupDelta / PIANOo | Innovation-friendly procurement guidance and SME set-asides | High — practical toolkit approach |
| **EU** | Pre-Commercial Procurement (PCP) / Innovation Partnerships | Structured R&D procurement for novel solutions | Relevant for cross-border context |

### 4.4 Barrier Analysis — Why Public Procurement Doesn't Work for SMEs

Based on our research and experience, we will address common procurement barriers:

- **Lot sizing:** Contracts too large for SME capacity
- **Track record requirements:** Catch-22 of needing experience to gain experience
- **Payment terms:** Cash flow challenges for SMEs in public contracts
- **Specification rigidity:** Outcome-based vs input-based specifications
- **Bureaucratic burden:** Disproportionate bid costs relative to contract value
- **Risk aversion:** Public sector reluctance to procure novel AI solutions from SMEs
- **Awareness:** SMEs unaware of procurement opportunities or how to access them

### 4.5 Practical Recommendations

We will develop specific, implementable recommendations for how procurement and public sector demand can stimulate SME AI adoption:

- **AI Innovation Challenges:** An all-island AI challenge fund (modelled on CivTech/GovTech Catalyst) where public bodies pose problems and SMEs propose AI solutions — InterTradeIreland as the cross-border coordinator
- **AI Procurement Readiness Toolkit:** Guidance for both public buyers and SME suppliers on AI procurement — addressing specification, evaluation, ethics, and contract management
- **Dynamic Purchasing Systems for AI Services:** Simplified frameworks allowing public bodies to access SME AI providers without full OJEU/eTenders processes
- **Sandbox/Pilot Programmes:** Structured pilot programmes where SMEs can test AI solutions in public sector environments with reduced risk
- **Cross-border procurement collaboration:** Mechanisms for public bodies in both jurisdictions to jointly commission AI solutions, creating larger markets for SME providers

## Key Differentiator

Blue Canvas understands both sides of the procurement equation. As an SME providing AI services, we experience the procurement barriers first-hand. We also understand what public sector bodies need from AI suppliers — reliability, compliance, and measurable outcomes. This dual perspective ensures our recommendations are grounded in practical reality, not theoretical procurement reform.

## Deliverable

A comprehensive Public Sector chapter forming Part 4 of the final report, including the current mechanisms audit, stakeholder perspectives from both buyers and suppliers, international comparison matrix, barrier analysis, and specific implementable recommendations for leveraging public sector demand to drive SME AI adoption.
