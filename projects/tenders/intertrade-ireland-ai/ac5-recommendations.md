# AC-5: Actionable Recommendations (20% — DOUBLE WEIGHTED)

## Overview

This section delivers the core output of the research: clear, specific, and actionable recommendations tailored to InterTradeIreland's strategic objectives and those of its government partners. Every recommendation is designed to be implementable, costed where appropriate, and aligned with the productivity and competitiveness priorities in both Programmes for Government, national AI strategies, and InterTradeIreland's Corporate Plan 2023–25.

## Approach to Developing Recommendations

### Guiding Principles

All recommendations will be:

1. **Specific and actionable** — not generic "more should be done" statements; each recommendation identifies the action, the actor, the mechanism, the timeframe, and the expected outcome
2. **Aligned to existing strategy** — mapped explicitly to InterTradeIreland's Corporate Plan, both Programmes for Government, and national AI strategies
3. **Additive, not duplicative** — each recommendation identifies where InterTradeIreland adds unique value that neither jurisdiction can deliver alone
4. **Evidence-based** — grounded in primary research findings, international benchmarking, and SME case studies from this project
5. **Prioritised** — categorised by impact, feasibility, and timeframe (quick wins, medium-term, strategic)

### Development Process

1. **Evidence synthesis** (Week 8): Consolidate findings from AC-1 through AC-4 into a structured evidence base
2. **Recommendation workshop** (Week 9): Facilitated session with InterTradeIreland steering group to test emerging recommendations against strategic priorities and operational reality
3. **Stakeholder validation** (Week 10): Share draft recommendations with 6–8 key stakeholders from enterprise agencies and SME representative bodies for feasibility testing
4. **Refinement and prioritisation** (Weeks 11–12): Final recommendations incorporating all feedback, with implementation roadmap

## Recommendation Framework

### PILLAR 1: ALL-ISLAND AI KNOWLEDGE AND COORDINATION

**Recommendation 1.1 — Establish an All-Island AI for SMEs Observatory**

InterTradeIreland should create a lightweight, ongoing research and monitoring function that tracks AI adoption across both jurisdictions, maintains the landscape map produced by this research, and publishes annual benchmarking data.

- **Rationale:** Current research is fragmented and jurisdiction-specific (as identified in AC-1). No single body monitors all-island SME AI adoption.
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Only body with the mandate and relationships to deliver all-island data collection and analysis.
- **Implementation:** Commission annual survey (building on this project's methodology); publish All-Island AI Adoption Index; share data with DETE, DfE, and enterprise agencies.
- **Alignment:** InterTradeIreland Corporate Plan — "robust evidence base to design initiatives"; Programmes for Government — evidence-based policy-making.
- **Estimated cost:** £25,000–35,000 per annum for ongoing data collection and reporting.
- **Timeframe:** Establish within 6 months of this report's publication.

**Recommendation 1.2 — Cross-Border AI Policy Coordination Forum**

Establish a biannual policy coordination forum bringing together AI policy leads from DfE, DETE, Invest NI, Enterprise Ireland, AICC, and other stakeholders to align interventions, share intelligence, and avoid duplication.

- **Rationale:** AC-1 findings will likely confirm fragmentation and duplication between jurisdictions.
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Convening power across both jurisdictions; neutral all-island platform.
- **Implementation:** Biannual half-day events (rotating Belfast/Dublin); published communiqué; action tracking.
- **Alignment:** InterTradeIreland's statutory remit for cross-border economic cooperation; both governments' AI strategies.
- **Estimated cost:** £8,000–12,000 per annum.
- **Timeframe:** First meeting within 3 months.

### PILLAR 2: SME AI ADOPTION SUPPORT

**Recommendation 2.1 — All-Island AI Readiness Programme for SMEs**

A structured, cross-border programme providing SMEs with:
- **Stage 1 — AI Readiness Assessment:** Free online self-assessment tool helping SMEs understand their current digital maturity and AI readiness (building on the three-tier adoption framework from AC-2)
- **Stage 2 — AI Discovery Workshops:** Half-day sector-specific workshops delivered across both jurisdictions, showing SMEs practical AI applications relevant to their sector
- **Stage 3 — AI Implementation Support:** Matched co-funding (e.g., £5,000–£10,000) for SMEs ready to implement their first substantive AI project, with access to approved AI service providers

- **Rationale:** Existing supports are either too generic or too advanced; SMEs need a structured pathway from awareness to implementation.
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Cross-border delivery; connecting SMEs in both jurisdictions to providers and peer networks.
- **Implementation:** Year 1 pilot with 100 SMEs (50 per jurisdiction); evaluate and scale.
- **Alignment:** InterTradeIreland's productivity and innovation strategic priorities; 10X Economy; Ireland's AI: Here for Good.
- **Estimated programme cost:** £200,000–£350,000 per annum (for policy advocacy; InterTradeIreland to co-design with sponsor departments).
- **Timeframe:** Design and pilot within 12 months.

**Recommendation 2.2 — AI Peer Learning Network**

Create a cross-border peer learning network connecting SMEs at similar stages of AI adoption for shared learning, problem-solving, and encouragement.

- **Rationale:** AC-3 research will likely confirm that peer influence is one of the strongest enablers — SMEs adopt when they see similar businesses succeeding.
- **Implementation:** Monthly online meetups; quarterly in-person events; curated case studies; facilitated by practitioners (not academics).
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Cross-border network building is core to InterTradeIreland's mission.
- **Estimated cost:** £15,000–25,000 per annum.
- **Timeframe:** Launch within 6 months.

**Recommendation 2.3 — All-Island AI Voucher Scheme**

A cross-border voucher scheme (modelled on Denmark's SME:Digital programme) providing SMEs with £2,000–£5,000 to purchase AI advisory services from approved providers.

- **Rationale:** Many SMEs need expert guidance but cannot justify consultant costs; vouchers de-risk the first engagement.
- **Implementation:** Panel of approved AI advisors across both jurisdictions; simple application process; post-project impact reporting.
- **Alignment:** Innovation Voucher models already proven in both jurisdictions; extends existing concept to AI-specific support.
- **Estimated cost:** £150,000–£300,000 per annum (50–100 vouchers).
- **Timeframe:** Design within 6 months; first round within 12 months.

### PILLAR 3: SKILLS AND TALENT

**Recommendation 3.1 — AI Leadership Programme for SME Owners/Managers**

A short, intensive programme (2–3 days) specifically designed for SME decision-makers — not technical training, but strategic AI literacy:
- Understanding what AI can and cannot do
- Identifying AI opportunities in their business
- Evaluating AI vendors and solutions
- Managing AI implementation risk
- Navigating regulatory requirements (UK and EU)

- **Rationale:** AC-3 evidence will show that the primary skills gap is at leadership level, not technical level. SME owners need enough understanding to make informed investment decisions.
- **Implementation:** Delivered cross-border; residential or blended format; cohorts of 15–20; delivered by practitioners with real SME implementation experience.
- **Alignment:** Both jurisdictions' skills strategies; InterTradeIreland's focus on leadership and management capability.
- **Estimated cost:** £3,000–5,000 per cohort; 6–8 cohorts per year = £25,000–40,000.
- **Timeframe:** Pilot first cohort within 6 months.

**Recommendation 3.2 — Cross-Border AI Talent Pipeline Initiative**

Work with universities and FE colleges across both jurisdictions to develop applied AI modules that include SME placement components:
- Students gain real-world experience implementing AI in SMEs
- SMEs get affordable access to AI talent
- Builds on InterTradeIreland's existing Fusion programme model

- **Rationale:** SMEs cannot compete with large firms for AI talent; placement models create pathways.
- **Implementation:** Partner with 4–6 institutions across both jurisdictions; 20–30 placements per year.
- **Alignment:** Fusion programme model; both jurisdictions' skills and higher education strategies.
- **Estimated cost:** £100,000–200,000 per annum (building on existing Fusion infrastructure).
- **Timeframe:** First cohort within academic year 2026/27.

### PILLAR 4: PUBLIC SECTOR AS DEMAND DRIVER

**Recommendation 4.1 — All-Island AI Innovation Challenge Programme**

Modelled on Scotland's CivTech and the UK GovTech Catalyst, establish an all-island programme where public bodies in both jurisdictions pose operational challenges and SMEs propose AI-powered solutions:

- Public bodies define challenges (not solutions)
- SMEs compete to develop prototypes
- Winners receive development funding and pilot opportunities
- Successful solutions can be procured through simplified mechanisms

- **Rationale:** AC-4 analysis demonstrates that demand-side innovation stimulation is the most effective way to accelerate SME AI adoption and create commercially viable AI businesses.
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Cross-border coordination; enabling public bodies from both jurisdictions to collaborate on shared challenges.
- **Implementation:** 2–3 challenge rounds per year; 5–10 challenges per round.
- **Alignment:** Both jurisdictions' innovation procurement policies; InterTradeIreland's innovation mandate.
- **Estimated cost:** £500,000–1,000,000 per annum (for policy advocacy; to be co-funded by sponsor departments and participating public bodies).
- **Timeframe:** First challenge round within 12 months.

**Recommendation 4.2 — AI Procurement Readiness Toolkit**

Develop and publish practical guidance for public sector procurement professionals and SME suppliers on AI procurement:

- How to specify AI requirements (outcome-based, not input-based)
- How to evaluate AI solutions
- Contract structures suitable for iterative AI development
- AI ethics and assurance requirements
- SME-friendly approaches (lot sizing, track record alternatives, payment terms)

- **Rationale:** Procurement teams lack confidence in buying AI solutions; SMEs lack confidence in selling to the public sector.
- **Implementation:** Co-developed with CPD, OGP, and SME advisors; published as free resource; supported by training workshops.
- **Estimated cost:** £30,000–50,000 (one-off development with annual updates).
- **Timeframe:** Published within 9 months.

### PILLAR 5: REGULATORY NAVIGATION

**Recommendation 5.1 — Cross-Border AI Regulatory Guidance Service**

Establish a dedicated advisory service (initially online, with access to expert advisors) helping SMEs navigate the dual UK/EU AI regulatory environment:

- Plain-language guides to EU AI Act and UK AI regulatory framework
- Self-assessment tool for AI risk classification
- Specific guidance for cross-border scenarios
- Sector-specific compliance checklists

- **Rationale:** AC-3 analysis will likely confirm that regulatory uncertainty is a significant barrier, particularly for cross-border SMEs facing two evolving regimes simultaneously.
- **InterTradeIreland's unique role:** Only body positioned to provide authoritative cross-border regulatory guidance; mirrors InterTradeIreland's existing role in Brexit/trade guidance.
- **Implementation:** Online resource hub with quarterly updates; access to 2–3 specialist regulatory advisors for complex queries.
- **Alignment:** InterTradeIreland's track record in cross-border business environment guidance.
- **Estimated cost:** £40,000–60,000 per annum.
- **Timeframe:** Basic online resource within 6 months; full service within 12 months.

## Strategic Alignment Summary

Each recommendation maps to:

- **InterTradeIreland Corporate Plan 2023–25:** Cross-border collaboration, innovation, SME competitiveness, evidence-based policy
- **NI Programme for Government / 10X Economy:** Productivity, innovation, skills, digital economy
- **Ireland's Programme for Government:** Enterprise competitiveness, digital transition, regional development
- **AI for NI Strategic Overview:** Skills, adoption, ecosystem development
- **Ireland's AI: Here for Good:** Responsible adoption, SME inclusion, skills development

## Key Differentiator

These recommendations are not theoretical — they are designed by a practitioner who works with SMEs on AI adoption daily. Each recommendation addresses a real gap that we observe in our work. They are specific enough to be budgeted, planned, and implemented, and they leverage InterTradeIreland's unique cross-border mandate rather than duplicating what either jurisdiction can do alone.

The prioritisation matrix enables InterTradeIreland to begin implementation immediately with quick wins while building toward more ambitious medium-term programmes — creating visible momentum and stakeholder buy-in.
