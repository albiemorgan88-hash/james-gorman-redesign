# Criterion C: Methodology (40%)

## Training Programme: Applied AI and Emerging Technologies for Tourism SMEs

### Overview

This programme is designed to take tourism SME owners and marketing staff from "AI-curious" to "AI-confident" in a practical, hands-on way. Every session focuses on tools participants can use the next day in their business — not theoretical concepts they'll never touch again.

The programme is structured as three half-day workshops delivered over six weeks, combining in-person sessions in Donegal with online follow-up support.

---

### Programme Structure

#### Session 1: AI Foundations & Generative AI for Marketing (In-Person, Donegal)
**Duration:** 3.5 hours (half-day workshop)
**Week of 2nd March 2026**

**Part A — What Is AI and Why Should Tourism SMEs Care? (60 mins)**
- Plain-English introduction to AI, machine learning, and generative AI
- Current AI trends specifically relevant to tourism: smart booking, dynamic content, automated guest communication
- Overview of free and low-cost AI tools available today (ChatGPT, Canva AI, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot)
- Discussion: What are the biggest time-drains in your business? (identifying where AI can help)

**Part B — Generative AI for Marketing Content Creation (90 mins, hands-on)**
- Writing compelling property descriptions, social media posts, and email campaigns using ChatGPT/Claude
- Creating and editing marketing images with Canva AI and free image generators
- Prompt engineering workshop: how to get consistently good results
- Live exercise: Each participant creates a social media post and email campaign for their own business
- Tips for maintaining authentic brand voice while using AI

**Part C — Action Planning (30 mins)**
- Each participant identifies 2-3 AI tools to trial before the next session
- Q&A and troubleshooting

---

#### Session 2: AI Chatbots & Customer Service Automation (In-Person, Donegal)
**Duration:** 3.5 hours (half-day workshop)
**Week of 16th March 2026**

**Part A — Review & Share (30 mins)**
- Participants share their experience using AI tools since Session 1
- Group troubleshooting and tips

**Part B — AI-Driven Customer Service: Chatbots for Tourism (90 mins, hands-on)**
- Why chatbots matter for tourism: 24/7 availability, handling common queries, booking support
- Overview of free/low-cost chatbot platforms (Tidio, ChatBot.com free tier, HubSpot chatbot)
- Live demonstration: Building a simple FAQ chatbot for a tourism business
- Hands-on exercise: Participants design chatbot conversation flows for their own business
- Integration options: connecting chatbots to websites, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp

**Part C — Personalisation & Recommendation Systems (60 mins)**
- How recommendation engines work (Netflix-style "you might also like" for tourism)
- Practical tools for personalisation: email segmentation, website personalisation, dynamic content
- Using customer data you already have (booking history, enquiry patterns) to personalise offers
- Case Study: How a small Irish activity provider increased repeat bookings by 35% using simple email personalisation

**Part D — Action Planning (30 mins)**
- Participants choose a chatbot platform to explore
- Planning personalisation quick-wins for their business

---

#### Session 3: Predictive Analytics, Case Studies & Putting It All Together (Online, via Zoom)
**Duration:** 3 hours (online workshop)
**Week of 6th April 2026**

**Part A — Review & Share (30 mins)**
- Progress update from participants
- Showcasing chatbots and personalisation efforts

**Part B — Predictive Analytics for Pricing & Inventory (60 mins)**
- Introduction to dynamic pricing concepts for accommodation, tours, and experiences
- Free/affordable tools for demand forecasting (Google Trends, basic spreadsheet models, Beyond Pricing concepts)
- How to use historical booking data to predict busy periods and adjust pricing
- Practical exercise: Creating a simple seasonal pricing model

**Part C — Real-World Case Studies (60 mins)**
- **Case Study 1:** Irish boutique hotel using AI chatbot to handle 60% of enquiries automatically
- **Case Study 2:** Adventure tourism operator using generative AI to produce multilingual marketing content at a fraction of previous cost
- **Case Study 3:** Rural B&B network using simple predictive analytics to optimise pricing across shoulder seasons
- **Case Study 4:** International example — how a cycling tourism operator in Scandinavia used AI personalisation to match riders with routes (relevant to SUB project's cycling tourism focus)

**Part D — AI Implementation Roadmap (30 mins)**
- Each participant creates a 90-day AI implementation plan for their business
- Prioritising tools by impact vs. effort
- Where to get ongoing support and stay up-to-date

---

### Delivery Approach

**Hybrid Model:**
- Sessions 1 & 2: In-person at a venue in Donegal (Letterkenny or location agreed with Donegal County Council)
- Session 3 & follow-up clinics: Online via Zoom

**Why this mix?**
- In-person sessions build confidence and allow hands-on support — critical for participants new to AI
- Online final session and clinics reduce travel burden and demonstrate that remote collaboration works
- Hybrid approach mirrors how participants will actually use AI: sometimes at their desk, sometimes on the go

**Participant Materials:**
- Pre-session briefing emails with setup instructions (creating free accounts for tools)
- Session handouts with step-by-step guides for each tool covered
- Post-session resource pack: links, templates, prompt libraries, and cheat sheets
- All materials provided digitally and designed to be kept as ongoing reference guides

**Group Size & Engagement:**
- Optimised for 10-20 participants
- Interactive, workshop-style delivery — minimal slides, maximum hands-on practice
- Participants work on their own businesses throughout, leaving with real assets they can use immediately

**Accessibility:**
- All materials available in digital format
- Online sessions recorded (with participant consent) for those who cannot attend live
- Plain language throughout — no jargon, no assumptions about technical background
