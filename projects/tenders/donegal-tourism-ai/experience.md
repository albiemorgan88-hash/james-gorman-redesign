## Relevant Experience (Last 2 Years)

### 1. AI Strategy & Implementation Consulting for SMEs (2025–present)

Blue Canvas AI has worked with a range of SME clients across Northern Ireland and the border region to integrate AI into their day-to-day operations. Typical engagements include:

- **AI readiness assessments** — evaluating where businesses can realistically benefit from AI tools, based on their current workflows, data, and budgets
- **Content creation automation** — implementing generative AI workflows for marketing teams, reducing content production time by 50-70% while maintaining brand consistency
- **Chatbot implementation** — deploying customer service chatbots for small businesses using free and low-cost platforms (Tidio, HubSpot), handling common enquiries and reducing response times
- **AI-assisted workflow design** — helping businesses automate repetitive tasks using AI-powered tools integrated with existing systems

These engagements directly mirror the training content required for this programme: we don't just teach AI theory, we implement these tools in real businesses every week.

### 2. AI Training & Workshops (2025–present)

We have designed and delivered AI training sessions for SME audiences, including:

- **"AI for Your Business" workshop series** — practical, hands-on sessions introducing business owners to generative AI tools, prompt engineering, and content creation automation
- **One-to-one AI coaching** — working with individual business owners to build confidence with AI tools and develop implementation plans tailored to their specific business needs
- **Group training sessions** — delivering to mixed-ability groups with participants ranging from complete beginners to those already experimenting with AI

Our training approach has been refined through direct feedback: we know that SME owners want practical skills they can use immediately, not academic presentations about the future of technology.
