## Structure of Each Landing Page

Every landing page follows the same structure:
1. **Hero** — Headline, subheadline, CTA button, price (where applicable)
2. **Problem/context** — Why this matters
3. **What you get** — Features and deliverables
4. **How it works** — Simple step-by-step
5. **Who it's for** — Target audience
6. **Social proof** — Testimonial placeholders (marked for replacement)
7. **FAQ** — 3-5 questions
8. **Final CTA** — Closing headline + button
