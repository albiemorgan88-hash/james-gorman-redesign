## Our Story

Blue Canvas was founded in March 2025 with a simple premise: most businesses know AI matters, but they don't know where to start.

The AI space is noisy. Full of hype, buzzwords, and solutions looking for problems. We saw businesses being sold tools they didn't need, or doing nothing because it all felt too complicated.

Blue Canvas exists to cut through that. We help businesses figure out where AI actually fits, build the systems that work, and train the people who use them.

We're based in Derry, Northern Ireland — but we work with clients wherever they are. We're sector-agnostic because the opportunities AI creates aren't limited to one industry.

---

## What We Believe

**Practical over theoretical.** We don't talk about AI in the abstract. We build things that work and save time.

**Honest over salesy.** If AI isn't the right fit for something, we'll say so. We'd rather give you good advice than sell you a service you don't need.

**Results over hype.** We measure success by what changes in your business — time saved, costs reduced, capacity gained.

**Plain English over jargon.** AI doesn't have to be complicated. We explain everything in terms anyone can understand.
