## FAQ Section

**Is it really free?**
Yes. No card details, no hidden fees. It's a genuine 30-minute conversation to see if we can help.

**What should I prepare?**
Nothing specific. Just come ready to talk about your business. If you have particular challenges or goals in mind, that's helpful but not required.

**Who will I speak to?**
A Blue Canvas consultant — someone who understands AI and knows how to apply it to businesses like yours.

**What happens after the call?**
That's up to you. If there's a clear opportunity, we'll suggest next steps (usually an AI audit). If not, you've still had a useful conversation. No follow-up pressure.

**Can I book on behalf of my team or company?**
Absolutely. We often speak with team leads or department heads who are exploring AI on behalf of their organisation.
