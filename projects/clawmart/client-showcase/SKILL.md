# Client Showcase Builder

> Build a stunning pitch deck for any prospect in minutes. Designed for WhatsApp sharing.

## What This Skill Does

Give your agent a prospect's website URL and it will:

1. **Crawl their site** — analyse design, content, SEO, mobile experience
2. **Identify problems** — what's broken, missing, or underperforming
3. **Generate a showcase** — 8-10 slide tap-through HTML presentation
4. **Brand it** — your colours, your logo, your CTA
5. **Deploy it** — push to GitHub Pages for a shareable link

The output is a mobile-first, tap-through HTML file that looks premium on any phone. Send it via WhatsApp, iMessage, or any messenger.

## Slides Structure

1. **Hook** — attention-grabbing question specific to their business
2. **The Problem** — what we found on their site (with visual mockups)
3. **The Fix** — what their site/business could look like
4. **Social Proof** — your results and credibility
5. **The Package** — exactly what they'd get
6. **The Numbers** — projected ROI / uplift
7. **Testimonials** — client quotes (if available)
8. **CTA** — book a call (links to your Calendly/booking page)
