## Step 5: Build and Integrate — Secure Implementation

Successful **AI implementation** requires careful attention to integration, security, and user experience.

### Data Security Considerations:

Before implementing any AI solution, ensure [AI data security](/blog/ai-data-security) protocols are established:

- **Data classification:** Identify sensitive vs. non-sensitive information
- **Access controls:** Limit AI access to necessary data only
- **Encryption:** Secure data in transit and at rest
- **Audit trails:** Log all AI interactions for compliance
- **Privacy compliance:** Ensure GDPR and other regulations are met

### Establishing AI Policies:

Every business needs an [AI policy template](/blog/ai-policy-template-business) covering:

- Approved AI tools and platforms
- Data handling procedures
- Quality control processes
- Escalation protocols for AI limitations
- Regular review and update schedules

### Integration Best Practices:

**Start with Pilot Groups:** Begin with enthusiastic early adopters who can provide feedback and become internal champions.

**Gradual Rollout:** Expand access systematically rather than organisation-wide deployment.

**Monitor Performance:** Track usage, accuracy, and user satisfaction metrics continuously.

**Iterate Based on Feedback:** Regularly refine configurations based on real-world usage.
