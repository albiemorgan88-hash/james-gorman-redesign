## Why Not Just Use ChatGPT?

You can. And you should — it's a great tool. But there's a massive difference between an individual using ChatGPT for ad hoc tasks and a business systematically implementing AI across its operations.

The difference is:
- **Strategy** — knowing which processes to automate first for maximum impact
- **Integration** — connecting AI tools to your existing systems (CRM, email, website, accounting)
- **Prompting** — getting consistent, high-quality outputs requires structured approaches (we use the CRISP framework for this)
- **Training** — making sure your whole team can use these tools, not just the one person who's into tech
- **Governance** — making sure you're not accidentally breaching GDPR or feeding sensitive data into public AI models

That's what a consultancy does. We don't replace ChatGPT — we make it (and dozens of other tools) work properly for your business.
