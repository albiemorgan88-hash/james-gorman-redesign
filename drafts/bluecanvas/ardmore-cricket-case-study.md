## Background
<h4>Background</h4><p>Ardmore Cricket Club needed more than a brochure site. The club wanted a digital home that looked credible, felt local, and could support a recurring weekly draw without relying on messy manual admin. The brief combined community presentation with real operational needs: member sign-up, recurring payments, results handling, club updates, and admin visibility.</p><h4>The Challenge</h4><p>Most small clubs end up juggling WhatsApp, spreadsheets, payment chasing, and fragmented website tools. That creates friction for members and extra work for volunteers. The challenge was to build something simple enough for club members to trust, while giving the organisers a cleaner system behind the scenes.</p>

## Solution & approach
<h4>Solution &amp; Approach</h4><p>Blue Canvas built Ardmore Cricket Club as a live Next.js platform tailored to a local sports club and its weekly numbers draw.</p><ul><li>Club-branded public website with fixtures, news, archive, sponsors, and membership pages</li><li>Weekly draw journey with number selection, recurring payment flow, results pages, and member account management</li><li>Admin routes for club operators to manage draw activity, payouts, and subscriber information</li><li>Production deployment, live fixes, and ongoing improvements to keep the club site usable for real members</li><li>Practical UX work focused on trust, clarity, and ease of use for a community audience</li></ul><p>The goal was not flashy software for its own sake. It was a dependable club system that people could actually use.</p>

## Results & impact
<h4>Results &amp; Impact</h4><p>The result is a live club platform that combines fundraising, member experience, and club content in one place.</p><ul><li>Ardmore now has a proper digital home rather than a disconnected set of tools</li><li>The weekly draw has a branded front end, member flow, and admin layer designed around real club operations</li><li>Blue Canvas has continued to improve the platform through live fixes, admin-flow improvements, and archive/content restoration as new needs surfaced</li></ul><p>For Blue Canvas, Ardmore is strong proof that we can take a local organisation with practical operational needs and ship a real working platform, not just a mock-up.</p>
