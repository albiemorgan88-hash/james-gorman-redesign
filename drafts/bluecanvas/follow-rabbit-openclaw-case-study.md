## Website-ready Blue Canvas case study draft

# Follow Rabbit, Early OpenClaw Implementation for Internal Operations

Follow Rabbit helps engineering and data teams reduce waste across Google Cloud. Publicly, the company positions itself around BigQuery optimisation, automated optimisation PRs, cost-focused code reviews, and broader GCP efficiency.

Blue Canvas is now working with Follow Rabbit on a separate operational challenge: implementing OpenClaw as an internal operating layer to support cleaner onboarding, stronger knowledge capture, and more repeatable internal workflows.

This matters because it is exactly the kind of buyer you want for serious AI operations work. Follow Rabbit is not a hype-driven prospect looking for a chatbot demo. It is a technical company with a real product, enterprise-facing credibility, and a clear understanding of how automation creates leverage. That makes the fit with OpenClaw commercially strong and strategically believable.

## The brief

The engagement is early stage and focused on setup, onboarding, and workflow design.

The aim is not to replace the team or force in process overhead. The aim is to give Follow Rabbit a better internal operating layer, so recurring work can be captured, reused, delegated, and improved over time.

The first areas of focus are likely to include:
- onboarding support and handoff structure
- internal summaries and action tracking
- reusable business context and memory
- recurring operational tasks that currently live across chats, docs, and individual heads
- selected monitoring, drafting, or follow-up workflows where consistency matters

## Why this is a strong Blue Canvas case study

Most case studies in AI consulting are built around inflated outcomes or vague pilot projects. This one is stronger because the signal is clear even before the rollout is mature.

Follow Rabbit is already in the business of helping teams run cloud infrastructure more efficiently. The fact that a company like that has engaged Blue Canvas to implement OpenClaw internally is meaningful on its own.

It shows that:
- Blue Canvas can sell OpenClaw to technically literate buyers
- the offer is credible beyond non-technical SMEs
- there is demand for AI operating systems behind real delivery businesses
- a phased, practical engagement model can win paid work without overcomplicating the sale

## Commercial structure

This engagement began with a £1,000 setup fee and a £500 monthly retainer.

That is useful proof in its own right. It shows Blue Canvas can package OpenClaw implementation as a simple, low-friction offer for businesses that want practical deployment rather than a large consulting programme.

## Public proof around the client

Follow Rabbit publicly presents a strong market position. On its website, the company references:
- BigQuery and broader Google Cloud cost optimisation
- automated optimisation PRs and cost-focused code reviews
- Google Cloud Partner status
- publicly displayed customer logos including Lufthansa Group, Rakuten, Trivago, and Nordstrom
- publicly stated savings figures exceeding $50 million through Rabbit Automation

Those are Follow Rabbit proof points, not Blue Canvas delivery outcomes. But they do make the case study more commercially useful because they show the calibre of company choosing to work with Blue Canvas.

## Current status

This is an implementation-in-progress case study.

The honest version is the strongest version: Blue Canvas is actively setting up OpenClaw for Follow Rabbit, shaping the memory layer, defining initial skills, and building the foundation for repeatable internal workflows.

That is enough to tell a credible story now, and it leaves room to publish a fuller results-based case study later once the rollout has matured.
