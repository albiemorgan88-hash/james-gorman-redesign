Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to make a formal complaint regarding irresponsible lending in relation to my Vanquis credit card account.

**Current position:**
- Credit limit: £6,350
- Current balance: £5,531.48
- Minimum payment: £124.49/month
- Estimated monthly interest: £80.26

**My complaint:**

Under FCA rules (CONC 5.2A and CONC 6.7), you are required to carry out reasonable and proportionate creditworthiness assessments both at the point of initial lending and before any credit limit increase. You must also monitor accounts for signs of financial difficulty.

I believe Vanquis failed in these duties:

1. **The initial credit limit and/or subsequent increases** were approved without adequate affordability checks. At the time the limit was set at or increased to £6,350, my total monthly debt commitments across all creditors were approximately £1,682. A proper assessment of my income against my existing obligations should have identified that this credit was unaffordable.

2. **Persistent debt indicators were ignored.** My account shows a pattern of making minimum payments while interest charges consume the majority of each payment. Under FCA rules (CONC 6.7.27-31), you should have identified this pattern and taken action — including reducing or suspending my credit limit.

3. **My statement shows £80.26 in estimated interest next month** against a minimum payment of £124.49. This means only £44 of my payment reduces the actual debt. At this rate, it would take decades to repay. This is precisely the pattern the FCA's persistent debt rules were designed to prevent.

4. Vanquis has been subject to regulatory action for irresponsible lending practices previously. The FCA has made clear that sub-prime lenders bear a heightened responsibility to assess affordability properly.

**What I am asking for:**

- A refund of all interest and charges applied to this account, to be offset against the outstanding balance
- If the refunded amount exceeds the balance, the difference to be paid to me
- An immediate freeze on further interest charges pending the outcome of this complaint
- Removal of any adverse credit file entries related to this account

**Response timeline:**

Under the FCA's complaint handling rules (DISP), you have 8 weeks to provide a final response. If I do not receive a satisfactory response, I will refer my complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

I look forward to your response.
