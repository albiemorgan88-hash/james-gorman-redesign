Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to make a formal complaint regarding irresponsible lending practices in relation to three personal loans you approved for me.

**The loans in question:**
- Loan 1: £10,000 over 5 years (current balance: £9,354.91, repayments: £246.18/month)
- Loan 2: £7,000 over 5 years (current balance: £8,047.16, repayments: £174.94/month)
- Loan 3: £9,000 over 5 years (current balance: £12,644.22, repayments: £234.15/month)

**Total borrowed from Zopa: £26,000. Total monthly repayments to Zopa alone: £655.27.**

**My complaint:**

Under FCA rules (CONC 5.2A and CONC 5.3), you are required to carry out a reasonable and proportionate assessment of a borrower's creditworthiness before entering into a credit agreement. This assessment must take into account the borrower's ability to make repayments as they fall due, without the borrower experiencing financial difficulty.

I believe you failed in this duty, particularly with Loans 2 and 3:

1. **By the time you approved Loan 2**, you already had visibility of my repayments on Loan 1 (£246/month). A proper affordability check should have shown that adding a further £175/month was creating an unsustainable burden.

2. **By the time you approved Loan 3**, I was already repaying £421/month to you across two loans. Approving a further £234/month — bringing my total Zopa repayments to £655/month — demonstrates a clear failure to properly assess my ability to repay.

3. At the time of these applications, my total debt commitments across all creditors were approximately £1,682/month. A thorough affordability assessment, including checks against credit reference agency data, should have identified that I could not sustainably afford further lending.

4. I was in a position of financial vulnerability when these loans were approved. Responsible lending practices should have identified this.

**What I am asking for:**

In line with FCA guidance and Financial Ombudsman Service precedent on irresponsible lending complaints, I am requesting:

- A refund of all interest and charges applied to Loans 2 and 3 from inception
- The refunded amounts to be applied against the outstanding balances
- If the refunded amounts exceed the outstanding balances, the difference to be paid to me
- Removal of any adverse credit file entries related to these accounts
- An immediate freeze on further interest accrual on all three accounts pending the outcome of this complaint

**Response timeline:**

Under the FCA's complaint handling rules (DISP), you have 8 weeks to provide a final response. If I do not receive a satisfactory response within this period, I will refer my complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

I look forward to your response.
