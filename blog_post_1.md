## Common UK SME AI Implementation Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

### Mistake 1: Starting with Complex Solutions

Many SMEs jump straight to advanced AI like machine learning models or natural language processing. Start simple. A well-configured Zapier automation often delivers more value than a custom AI model.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Data Quality

AI is only as good as your data. If your current systems have inconsistent data entry, duplicate records, or missing information, address this first.

### Mistake 3: Not Training Your Team

The best AI system fails if your team doesn't understand or trust it. Budget 20% of your implementation time for training and change management.

### Mistake 4: Choosing Tools Based on Features, Not Outcomes

Don't get distracted by AI vendors promising 100+ features. Focus on tools that solve your specific problems well.
