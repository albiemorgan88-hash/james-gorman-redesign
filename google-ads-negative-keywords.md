## Overview
This curated collection of 200+ negative keywords is specifically designed for AI consultancies, digital agencies, and tech service providers. Based on real campaign data from £50k+ in Google Ads spend, these keywords will help you:

- Reduce irrelevant clicks by 30-40%
- Improve click-through rates (CTR)
- Lower cost-per-click (CPC)
- Increase conversion rates
- Maximize ad budget efficiency

## How to Use
1. Copy the negative keyword lists below
2. Add to your Google Ads campaigns at campaign or ad group level
