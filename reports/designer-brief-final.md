## About Us

**Blue Canvas** (bluecanvas.ai) is an AI consultancy based in Derry, Northern Ireland, founded by Phil Patterson. We help UK and Irish businesses cut through the AI hype and actually implement it — audits, automation, training, the works.

The site is on **Webflow**. It works, but it looks like a template. We need it to look like a credible, premium consultancy — not Silicon Valley, not corporate. Professional, direct, approachable, with personality.

**Reference site:** singlegrain.com — founder-led story, results front and centre, clean layout, conversion-focused CTAs on every section.

---

## The Problem

- **Generic template aesthetic** — screams "template" not "trusted consultancy"
- **Bland hero** — doesn't grab attention or communicate value quickly
- **Weak conversion flow** — no clear path: land → understand → book a call
- **Duplicate content** — "Accelerating Growth Through Expertise" block repeated on homepage AND /services. Multiple H1 tags on both pages.
- **No team page** — prospects want to know who they're working with
- **Confusing pricing** — monthly retainers and one-off packages mixed together
- **Stock imagery** — generic AI photos that could belong to any site
- **4 font families loading** — only need 2. Slowing the site down.
- **Mobile responsiveness** — needs tightening across breakpoints

## Technical SEO Fixes (Must-Do)

These are existing issues the designer must fix during the rebuild:

1. **H1 tags** — Homepage and /services both have 3 H1 tags. Must be exactly 1 per page. Change extras to H2.
2. **Duplicate sections** — "Accelerating Growth Through Expertise" block appears on homepage AND /services. Remove from one.
3. **Fonts** — 4 families loading (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk, Urbanist). Consolidate to **2 max**. Add `font-display: swap`.
4. **Render-blocking scripts** — Defer non-critical scripts, especially Calendly widget (lazy-load on scroll/click).
5. **Title tag:** `Blue Canvas | AI Consultancy UK | AI Audit, Automation & Training`
6. **Meta description:** `Blue Canvas is a UK-based AI consultancy helping businesses grow with AI audits, automation, and training. Book a free consultation today.`
7. **H1:** `AI Consultancy That Grows Your Business` (hero headline, one per page)
