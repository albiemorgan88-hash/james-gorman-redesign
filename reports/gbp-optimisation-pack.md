## 3. FAQs (20)

### General AI Consultancy

**Q1: What does an AI consultant actually do?**
An AI consultant analyses your business operations, identifies tasks that can be automated or enhanced with artificial intelligence, and helps you implement the right tools. At Blue Canvas, we go further — we build and deploy AI solutions for you, so you're not left figuring it out alone.

**Q2: Is AI only for big companies?**
Not at all. AI tools have become affordable and accessible for businesses of every size. Many of our clients are SMEs with 5–50 staff. The ROI is often even better for smaller businesses because there's less bureaucracy and faster implementation.

**Q3: How much does AI implementation cost?**
It depends on scope. Our AI Audit starts at £750 and gives you a full roadmap. Implementation costs vary based on complexity, but most SME projects fall between £2,000–£10,000. We always start with the highest-ROI tasks first so you see returns quickly.

**Q4: Will AI replace my staff?**
AI replaces tasks, not people. Your team will spend less time on repetitive admin and more time on work that actually needs a human brain. Most businesses we work with redeploy time savings into growth, customer service, or new revenue streams.

**Q5: What AI tools do you typically recommend?**
It depends on the business. We work with a mix of AI agents, large language models, automation platforms, and custom-built solutions. We're tool-agnostic — we recommend what works best for your specific situation, not what pays us a commission.

**Q6: How long does an AI Audit take?**
Typically 1–2 weeks from kickoff to final report. We spend time understanding your operations, mapping workflows, and building a prioritised action plan with clear costings and expected ROI.

**Q7: What industries do you work with?**
Any industry with repeatable processes benefits from AI. We've worked with recruitment, professional services, and trades businesses. Retail, hospitality, healthcare admin, logistics, and construction are all ripe for AI automation.

**Q8: Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?**
Zero. We handle the technical side. Our job is to make AI simple and practical for you and your team. If you can use a smartphone, you can use the tools we deploy.

**Q9: What's the difference between AI automation and traditional software?**
Traditional software follows rigid rules. AI can understand context, learn from data, make decisions, and handle tasks that previously needed human judgement — like drafting emails, summarising documents, or qualifying leads.

**Q10: How quickly will I see ROI from AI?**
Most clients see measurable time savings within the first month of implementation. Financial ROI typically follows within 2–3 months, depending on the complexity of what we deploy.
