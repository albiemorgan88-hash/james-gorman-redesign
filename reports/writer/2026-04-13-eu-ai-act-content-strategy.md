### Detail
Grounding used:
- Official EU sources confirm the AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024.
- Prohibitions, definitions and AI literacy have applied since 2 February 2025.
- Governance rules and GPAI obligations have applied since 2 August 2025.
- Main application date remains 2 August 2026, while the Commission’s 19 November 2025 Digital Omnibus proposal could shift some high-risk timing depending on support measures/standards.
- Scope applies to organisations inside and outside the EU that place AI systems/models on the EU market or use them in the EU.

1. Blue Canvas should own the whole commercial angle:
   - pillar / service page around EU AI Act compliance for UK businesses

   - Best fit is one guide around self-hosted / agentic AI implementation with compliance considerations
   - Use internal links from OCC to Blue Canvas where governance/advisory support is needed
3. New domain is not worth it now.
   - Query space is still immature and trust-sensitive
   - Blue Canvas already has AI audit / policy / governance adjacency

   - Only worth revisiting if Phil builds a dedicated readiness tool/SaaS or benchmark/report product
