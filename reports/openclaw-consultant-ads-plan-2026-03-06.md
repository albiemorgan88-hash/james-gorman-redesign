## 5. Landing Page Assessment

### Current Strengths ✅
- Strong social proof (testimonials, stats)
- Clear pricing (Starter/Growth/Enterprise)
- Good FAQ section addressing objections
- Clear CTA (free strategy call via Calendly)
- Mobile-responsive
- Fast-loading

### Issues to Fix for Quality Score 🔴

| Issue | Impact | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **No dedicated ad landing pages** | High | Create `/google-ads/openclaw-setup` and `/google-ads/ai-agents` pages with keyword-matched H1s |
| **Calendly link goes to bluecanvas.ai** | Medium | Consider OpenClaw-branded Calendly or at least consistent branding |
| **No conversion tracking pixel** | Critical | Install Google Ads conversion tracking on Calendly booking confirmation |
| **No phone number visible** | Medium | Add click-to-call for mobile visitors |
| **Missing trust signals above fold** | Medium | Add "50+ agents deployed" and "£2M saved" stats above fold, not just in testimonials |
| **H1 is generic** | Medium | Match H1 to ad group keyword theme for each landing page variant |
| **No urgency/scarcity** | Low | Add "Limited availability — 3 client slots this month" or similar |
| **Form vs Calendly** | Medium | Consider a simple "Name/Email/Company" form as alternative to Calendly booking — lower friction for some users |

### Landing Page Recommendations

1. **Create 2-3 tailored landing pages:**
   - `/ads/openclaw-setup` — H1: "OpenClaw Setup & Deployment for Your Business"
   - `/ads/ai-agents` — H1: "Custom AI Agents Built for Your Business"
   - `/ads/ai-automation` — H1: "AI Automation That Pays for Itself"

2. **Each page should have:**
   - Keyword-matched H1
   - 3-4 bullet benefits
   - 1 strong testimonial
   - Pricing anchor ("from £1,500")
   - Primary CTA: "Book Free Strategy Call"
   - Secondary CTA: simple form (name + email + "What do you need?")

3. **Install Google Ads conversion tracking** — this is non-negotiable. Without it we're flying blind.
