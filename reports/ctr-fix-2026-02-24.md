## Blog Audit — ALL Posts Missing SEO Fields ⚠️

**Every single blog post (95+ items)** has no dedicated `seo-title` or `seo-description` set in the CMS.

The blog template uses dynamic fields:
- Title: `"Blue Canvas – Scale with AI Consultancy | Blogs - {{name}}"`
- Description: `"{{small-details}}"`

**Issues:**
1. Template title is too long and generic — "Blue Canvas – Scale with AI Consultancy | Blogs - [Post Name]" often exceeds 60 chars
2. Only ~11 of 95+ posts have `small-details` filled in — the rest have **empty meta descriptions**
3. Empty descriptions mean Google auto-generates snippets (unpredictable CTR)

**Recommendation:** Set individual SEO titles/descriptions for at least the top 10 posts by traffic. For the rest, fill in `small-details` so the template fallback works.
