## Beyond Meta Tags: Critical Issues to Address

### 1. 🚨 "Near Me" at #1 Getting Zero Clicks — Possible Causes
- **Google Business Profile (GBP)** may not be optimised — "near me" queries often show a local pack (map) ABOVE organic. If Blue Canvas isn't in the local pack, the organic #1 position is below the fold.
- **Action:** Verify GBP is claimed, complete, and has fresh reviews. Add "AI Consultancy" as primary category.
- **Schema markup:** Add LocalBusiness structured data to the homepage with address, phone, and service area.

### 2. 🔴 Homepage Title Truncation
Current title is 73 characters — Google cuts it at ~60. Whatever Google is showing is probably mangled with "..." which kills CTR.

### 3. 🟡 Brand Name in SERPs
"Blue Canvas" means nothing to cold searchers. It doesn't signal AI, consultancy, or Northern Ireland. The brand needs to lean heavily on descriptive titles rather than brand-first approaches.

### 4. 🟡 Old Year References
The AI tools blog post still references 2025 in its description. Searchers in 2026 will skip content that looks outdated.

### 5. 🟡 Structured Data / Rich Snippets
- Add FAQ schema to pricing page (the FAQ content is already there)
- Add Review/Rating schema if testimonials are available
- Add Article schema to blog posts
- Rich snippets increase SERP real estate = higher CTR
