## Post 1: Case Study — The 20-Hour Discovery
**Pillar:** Case Study | **Publish:** Tuesday 10 March
**Hashtags:** #AI #ProcessAutomation #SmallBusiness #BlueCanvas

We just finished an AI audit for a 30-person business.

They were convinced they didn't need AI. "We're not a tech company."

Then we mapped their workflows.

20 hours a week. That's what their team was spending on tasks AI could handle — data entry, chasing invoices, formatting reports, copying info between systems.

Twenty. Hours. Every. Week.

That's basically a part-time employee doing work a machine could do in seconds.

The kicker? They didn't even realise it. These tasks were so baked into "how we do things" that nobody questioned them.

We're automating 80% of it. No new hires. No massive tech overhaul.

Here's the thing most businesses get wrong about AI: it's not about replacing people. It's about stopping your best people from doing robot work.

## Post 2: Quick Tip — Claude for Meeting Notes
**Pillar:** AI Education | **Publish:** Wednesday 11 March
**Hashtags:** #AITools #Productivity #TechTips #Automation

Stop manually writing meeting notes. Seriously.

Here's a setup that takes 5 minutes and saves hours every week:

1. Record your meetings (Zoom/Teams already do this)
2. Feed the transcript into Claude or ChatGPT
3. Prompt: "Summarise this meeting. List action items with owners and deadlines."

That's it. You get clean notes, clear actions, and a searchable record.

I've seen teams spend 30+ minutes after every meeting writing up notes and emailing round actions. Now it takes 30 seconds.

The real win? Things actually get done because the actions are clear and nobody can claim they "didn't get the memo."

Start with your next meeting. You'll never go back.

## Post 3: Contrarian Take — AI Hype vs Reality
**Pillar:** Thought Leadership | **Publish:** Thursday 12 March
**Hashtags:** #AI #FutureOfWork #AIStrategy #HotTake

Unpopular opinion: 90% of businesses don't need an "AI strategy."

They need to fix their broken processes first.

I've spoken to companies who want to "implement AI" but still run their operations on spreadsheets emailed back and forth, WhatsApp groups as project management, and sticky notes as their CRM.

You don't need a chatbot. You need a system.

AI amplifies what's already there. If your processes are a mess, AI just automates the mess faster.

Before you spend a penny on AI, answer this:
→ Can you describe your core workflow in under 60 seconds?
→ Do you know where time gets wasted each week?
→ Is your data in one place or scattered across 15 tools?

If you can't answer those, AI isn't your problem.

Fix the plumbing. Then turn on the taps.

Agree or disagree?

## Post 4: Behind the Scenes — Running an AI Consultancy
**Pillar:** Behind the Scenes | **Publish:** Friday 13 March
**Hashtags:** #BuildInPublic #AgencyLife #AIAgency #BlueCanvas

The bit nobody tells you about running an AI consultancy:

Most of my day isn't building cool AI stuff.

It's explaining what AI actually is. And more importantly — what it isn't.

Every week I hear:
"Can AI write all our content?" (It can help. It shouldn't do it all.)
"Will this replace my team?" (No. It replaces the boring bits of their job.)
"Is this just ChatGPT?" (That's like asking if a car is just an engine.)

The real work is translating between what AI can do and what a business actually needs.

That translation gap is massive. And it's where the value sits.

The tech is the easy part. Understanding a business well enough to apply it properly — that's the craft.
