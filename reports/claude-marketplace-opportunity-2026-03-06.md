## 1. What Is It?

**Anthropic Marketplace** (officially "Claude Marketplace") — an enterprise software marketplace where Anthropic's corporate customers can purchase third-party Claude-powered tools using their existing Anthropic spend commitment. Announced today (6 March 2026).

**Live URL:** https://claude.com/platform/marketplace  
**Partner Waitlist:** https://claude.com/marketplace-partners  
**Bloomberg coverage:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-06/anthropic-unveils-amazon-inspired-marketplace-for-ai-software

### Key Model Details:
- **Amazon/AWS Marketplace-inspired** — enterprises can redirect part of their annual Anthropic spend to buy third-party partner tools
- **No commission** — Anthropic takes 0% cut of partner purchases
- **Anthropic handles all invoicing** for partner spend
- **Limited preview** currently — not fully open yet
- Enterprise customers talk to their Anthropic account team to activate
- Simplifies procurement — one vendor, one commitment, multiple tools
