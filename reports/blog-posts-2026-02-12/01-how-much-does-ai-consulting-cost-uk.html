What Are You Actually Paying For?

AI consulting isn't one thing. It covers a range of services, and the price depends on which ones you need:

  • AI strategy and roadmapping — Working out where AI fits in your business, what to prioritise, and what to avoid. Usually a one-off engagement.
  • Tool selection and setup — Choosing the right AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, automation platforms) and configuring them for your workflows.
  • Custom automation builds — Building AI-powered workflows using tools like Zapier, Make, or custom code. This is where you start saving real time.
  • Training and upskilling — Getting your team confident with AI tools so they actually use them.
  • Ongoing support and optimisation — Monthly retainers where a consultant keeps improving your AI setup as your business evolves.

Most UK businesses need a combination of these. The mistake is jumping straight to tools without strategy, or paying for strategy without implementation.

What Affects the Price?

A few things drive the cost up or down:

  • Complexity — A simple chatbot setup costs less than rebuilding your entire sales pipeline with AI agents.
  • Custom development — Off-the-shelf tools are cheaper. Custom-built solutions cost more but fit better.
  • Team size — Training 5 people costs less than training 50.
  • Industry — Regulated sectors (healthcare, finance, legal) need more careful implementation, which takes longer.
  • Location — London consultancies charge more. Northern Ireland-based firms like Blue Canvas offer the same quality at more competitive rates, because our overheads are lower.

How to Get Value for Money

Phil Patterson, Blue Canvas founder, puts it simply: "The most expensive AI consulting is the kind that produces a 40-page PDF and no actual change in your business."

Here's how to make sure you're spending wisely:

Start with a clear problem

Don't hire a consultant to "do AI." Hire them to fix something specific — reduce proposal turnaround time, automate follow-up emails, generate leads faster. The clearer you are, the better the outcome.

Ask for measurable outcomes

A good consultant will tell you what success looks like in numbers: hours saved, leads generated, cost reduced. If they can't quantify it, be cautious.

Start small, then scale

You don't need a £20,000 project to get started. A £2,000 strategy session plus a £500/month retainer can transform a small business within three months. We've seen it happen repeatedly with our clients across Northern Ireland and the UK.

Check for implementation, not just advice

Strategy without implementation is just a document. Make sure your consultant actually builds things, trains your team, and sticks around to see results.
