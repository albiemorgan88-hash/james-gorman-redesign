## FAQ

### Will AI-generated content hurt our SEO?
No — when done properly. Google's stance is that quality matters, not whether AI assisted in creation. AI-generated content that's been reviewed, refined, and adds genuine value performs well. Content that's published without human review and adds nothing new may not. We ensure quality in everything we help produce.

### How much time will AI save our marketing team?
Most businesses report saving 10-15 hours per week on content creation, email management, and reporting combined. For smaller teams, that's often enough to eliminate the need for an additional hire.

### Do we still need a marketing team if we use AI?
Yes — but a smaller, more strategic one. AI handles the production grunt work. Humans provide strategy, brand voice, creative direction, and relationship building. The ideal setup is a human strategist + AI execution.

It depends on your needs. Common tools include ChatGPT/Claude for content, Mailchimp/ActiveCampaign for email, Apollo for lead gen, Ahrefs/Semrush for SEO, and various automation platforms. We recommend based on your specific situation and budget.

Content production speed improves immediately. Email automation can be live within 1-2 weeks. SEO improvements typically show within 1-3 months. Lead generation results depend on your market but often materialise within weeks.
