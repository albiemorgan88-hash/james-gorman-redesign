## AI Use Cases for Northern Ireland Businesses

AI isn't sector-specific. Here's how it's already being used by NI businesses:

### Construction & Trades
Northern Ireland has over 11,000 construction businesses, mostly micro-firms. AI is transforming how they bid for work — generating first-draft tenders in minutes instead of days, scanning RFP requirements automatically, and ensuring compliance. A single proposal that used to take 25 hours can now be completed in a fraction of that time with AI assistance.

### Hospitality & Tourism
With 143 hotels and 66 guest houses across NI, standing out on service is essential. AI chatbots handle guest queries 24/7, while AI tools draft personalised responses to reviews — boosting online ratings and saving hours of owner time.

### Legal & Professional Services
Northern Ireland's 600 law firms (employing around 2,300 solicitors) are using AI to summarise lengthy contracts, draft standard legal documents, and manage client communications. Document automation has been shown to cut drafting time by up to 90%.

### Charities & Community Organisations
With over 6,000 charities and community groups in NI, many are turning to AI to write grant applications faster. AI can draft compelling proposals aligned with funder requirements, cutting the typical 30-50 hour writing process dramatically.

### Food & Drink
NI's food and drink sector is worth over £8 billion annually. Artisan producers use AI to generate product descriptions, social media content, and export documentation — handling marketing like a pro without a marketing department.

### Healthcare
From GP practices to dental clinics, AI helps with appointment scheduling, patient communications, clinical note summarisation, and even diagnostic support. The NHS is increasingly piloting AI tools, and private practices in NI are following suit.
