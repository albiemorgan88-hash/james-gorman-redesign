### Why GAA is the Biggest Prize
- ~250+ clubs in NI alone, all amateur, all community-funded
- Lotto/draw culture is **already embedded** — most GAA clubs run paper draws or use Clubforce/Klubfunder
- Many clubs are frustrated with existing platforms (high fees, clunky UX)

- This is where the real revenue is — but also where the competition lives

### Why Cricket is the Best Entry Point
- Phil knows the NWCU scene personally — Ardmore is proof of concept

- Smaller clubs, faster decision-making (no big committee bureaucracy)

**Cricket (82 clubs):** Greenfield. Most don't run draws. The pitch is "start generating £200-500/week you're not getting today." **Easiest wins.**

**Rugby (42 clubs):** Mid-sized clubs with decent supporter bases. Some run draws already. Worth targeting after cricket is established.

**Hockey (70+ clubs):** Smaller memberships, lower pots. Lower priority but still viable.

## 3. Messaging & Pitch

### The Elevator Pitch
> "ClubDraw runs your weekly numbers draw online — no paper tickets, no cash handling, no chasing payments. Members pick their numbers and pay by direct debit. The draw runs automatically every week. Your club keeps 90% of every pot, we take 10%. Zero setup cost, zero risk."

### Key Selling Points (in order of impact)

1. **Zero upfront cost** — No registration fee, no annual fee, no setup charge
2. **Automatic everything** — Draw runs itself, payments collected automatically, winners notified via WhatsApp
3. **No cash handling** — No treasurer counting fivers, no lost tickets, no chasing payments
4. **Reach diaspora** — Members who've moved away can still play (huge for GAA clubs)
5. **Provably fair** — SHA-256 hash published before each draw, mathematically verifiable
6. **Club keeps 90%** — Clean. No hidden fees. We absorb Stripe costs.
7. **White-label** — Your club's brand, your colours, your subdomain (ardmore.clubdraw.co.uk)
8. **WhatsApp notifications** — Results, winners, payment confirmations direct to members' phones
9. **Real-time dashboard** — See members, payments, pot size, draw history in one place
10. **Live in days, not weeks** — We handle all the tech. Club just needs council lottery registration.

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Current Model: 10% Platform Fee
- Club keeps **90% clean** (no Stripe fees deducted from their share)
- ClubDraw absorbs Stripe fees (~2.9% + 20p card / ~1% BACS) from the 10%
- **Net margin to ClubDraw: ~6-7%** after Stripe fees

### Competitive Analysis

| Platform | Effective Total Fee | Market |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| **ClubDraw** | **~10%** | NI/UK |
| Clubforce | ~20-25% all-in | Ireland (GAA dominant) |
| SmartLotto | ~20-28% all-in | Ireland |
| LottoRaiser | 8-15% sliding | Ireland |
| Club Competitions | 20% flat | UK |

### Verdict: 10% is Competitive and Sustainable
- It's the **lowest true cost** in the market when all fees are included
- Undercuts Clubforce (the market leader) by ~50% on total cost
- Still delivers viable margin (~6-7% net)
- Simple to explain: "You keep 90p of every £1"

### Early Adopter Strategy

**First 10 clubs (founding members):**
- **Months 1-3:** 10% standard (no discount needed — it's already cheapest)
- **Referral incentive:** Clubs that refer another club get 1 week fee-free per referral (worth ~£25)
- **Founding club badge:** "Founding Member" badge on their ClubDraw site — social proof, early adopter pride
- **Locked-in rate:** "Your 10% rate is guaranteed for life. We'll never increase it."

**DO NOT go below 10%.** The margin is already thin after Stripe fees. The competitive advantage isn't price — it's simplicity, UX, WhatsApp, and zero hidden fees. Discounting further erodes the only revenue stream.

### Future Pricing Options (12+ months out)
- **Premium tier (£50/month):** Custom domain, advanced analytics, SMS fallback, priority support
- **Enterprise (bespoke):** County boards, multi-club organisations
- **Add-on services:** Membership management, fixture management, sponsorship integration
