### MONDAY — AI Tip #1
**Stop using ChatGPT like a search engine.**

Most business owners type a question and accept whatever comes back. That's like buying a sports car and only driving it to Tesco.

Here's what actually works:

Give it context. Tell ChatGPT who you are, what your business does, and who your audience is. Then ask it to do something specific.

Instead of: "Write me a marketing email"
Try: "You're a marketing strategist for a B2B SaaS company selling to HR directors in the UK. Write a cold email that leads with a stat about employee retention and ends with a soft CTA for a 15-min call."

The difference in output quality is night and day.

I've seen clients go from "AI is rubbish" to "AI just saved us 10 hours a week" simply by learning how to prompt properly.

Your AI is only as good as your instructions.

### SATURDAY — Hot Take / Opinion
**Unpopular opinion: Most "AI strategies" are just expensive to-do lists.**

I've reviewed dozens of AI strategies from other consultancies. Most of them look like this:

📄 30-page PDF
📊 Lots of quadrant diagrams
🗓️ A "phased roadmap" stretching 18 months
💰 Price tag: £15-30K

And what happens? The PDF sits in a shared drive. Nobody reads it. Nothing changes.

Here's what I think an AI strategy should actually be:

→ A 2-page action plan
→ 3 quick wins you can implement this week
→ 1 bigger project for the quarter
→ Clear ownership (who does what)
→ A 60-day check-in to measure results

Strategy without execution is just expensive fiction.

At Blue Canvas, we don't write reports that gather dust. We implement alongside you from day one.
