# Backlink Acquisition Plan — bluecanvas.ai
**Date:** 6 March 2026  
**Current DR:** 19 | **Referring Domains:** 11  
**Target:** 50+ referring domains within 90 days  
**Competitor benchmark:** brainpool.ai DR 42 / 555 RDs

---

## Strategy Overview

Blue Canvas has a severe backlink deficit. With only 11 referring domains vs. brainpool.ai's 555, closing this gap requires an aggressive but systematic approach across 4 channels:

1. **Directory Listings** (quick wins, low effort)
2. **Chamber/Association Memberships** (high authority, local relevance)
3. **Guest Posting** (dofollow links + brand authority)
4. **Press/PR** (high DR links from NI/Ireland tech media)

## 5. Competitor Broken Link Analysis

### brainpool.ai
- Has 555 referring domains — heavily content-marketing driven
- Blog-heavy strategy with AI topic pages (ai-in-marketing, design-architecture etc.)
- **Opportunity:** Create competing content for same topics they rank for, then outreach to sites linking to their blog posts offering a "Derry/NI perspective"

### iwantmore.ai
- Smaller competitor — needs deeper crawl to find broken links
- **Action:** Use Ahrefs broken backlinks report to find dead referring pages

### Recommended approach:
- Run Ahrefs "Broken Backlinks" report for both domains
- Find pages that 404 but still have inbound links
- Create equivalent content on bluecanvas.ai
- Email linking sites offering your live resource as replacement
