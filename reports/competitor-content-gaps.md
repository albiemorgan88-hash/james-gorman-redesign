## Quick Win Recommendations (Ranked by Impact)

### 🥇 1. Launch a blog with 5 cornerstone articles (2-3 weeks)
Write and publish:
- "What Is an AI Audit and Why Your Business Needs One" (target: ai audit uk)
- "AI for Northern Ireland Businesses: A Practical Guide" (target: ai northern ireland)
- "How Much Does AI Implementation Cost for UK SMEs?" (target: ai implementation cost)
- "AI Agents Explained: What They Are and How They Help Your Business" (target: ai agents for business)
- "Is Your Business Ready for AI? 10 Questions to Ask" (target: ai readiness)

**Why:** iwantmore.ai's content volume is likely driving their visibility. Blue Canvas has zero indexable content beyond the homepage. This is the single biggest gap.

### 🥈 2. Create an AI readiness self-assessment tool (1 week)
Simple Typeform/embedded quiz: 10 questions → score → CTA to book consultation.
**Why:** iwantmore, Artellis, and AICC all have one. It's a lead magnet and link-worthy asset. Low effort, high conversion.

### 🥉 3. Publish 2-3 case study pages with specifics (2 weeks)
The Wall Group expansion story has £4M+ in it — that's gold. Write it up properly with problem/solution/result format.
**Why:** iwantmore's video case studies are their strongest trust signal. Blue Canvas has testimonial quotes but no dedicated pages.

### 4. Add industry vertical pages (3-4 weeks)
Start with industries you've actually served: retail, construction, professional services. Each page targeting "[industry] + AI consultancy UK".
**Why:** Brainpool has AI in Construction and AI in Finance pages. These rank well for long-tail commercial intent queries.

### 5. Build a "Northern Ireland AI" resource page (1 week)
Link to AICC, Invest NI resources, local AI events, NI tech ecosystem. Position Blue Canvas as the hub for NI AI info.
**Why:** Nobody owns this niche commercially. AICC is academic. Being the go-to commercial resource for "AI Northern Ireland" reinforces your target keyword cluster.

### 6. Add an AI governance / responsible AI section (1-2 weeks)
Blog post + downloadable AI policy template for SMEs.
**Why:** EU AI Act is driving searches. Artellis charges for ISO training. Giving away a free starter template is a differentiated lead magnet.

## Summary

Blue Canvas's biggest vulnerability is **content poverty** — effectively a single-page site competing against iwantmore.ai's 20+ articles and Brainpool's industry vertical pages. The good news: none of these competitors own the "Northern Ireland" angle commercially, and none provide practical cost/ROI transparency. The blog + readiness tool + case studies trifecta would close the biggest gaps within a month.
