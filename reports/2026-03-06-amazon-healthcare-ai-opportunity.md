## 1. WHAT EXACTLY DID AMAZON ANNOUNCE?

**Product:** Amazon Connect Health
**Launch Date:** 5 March 2026 (literally yesterday)
**What it is:** Purpose-built agentic AI platform for healthcare providers

### Five core capabilities:
1. **Patient Verification** (GA) — AI verifies patient identity via real-time EHR access, replaces manual record lookup
2. **Ambient Documentation** (GA) — AI generates clinical notes from doctor-patient conversations
3. **Appointment Management** (Preview) — Natural language voice scheduling with insurance checks, 24/7
4. **Patient Insights** (Preview) — Summarises patient history, flags conditions since last visit
5. **Medical Coding** (Coming soon) — Auto-generates billing codes with source traceability

### Pricing:
- **$99/month per user** for ambient documentation (up to 600 encounters/month)
- **$0.15 per conversation** for patient verification
- Built on Amazon Connect (their contact centre platform)
- HIPAA-eligible, integrates with EHR systems via SDK

### Key detail: This is AWS's FIRST purpose-built healthcare AI product with regulatory compliance baked in.

### Competitors reacting:
- OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health (Jan 2026)
- Anthropic launched Claude for Healthcare (Jan 2026)
- Microsoft has Nuance/DAX (already dominant in clinical docs)
