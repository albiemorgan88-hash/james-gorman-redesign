FAQ

Will AI-generated content hurt our SEO?

No — when done properly. Google's stance is that quality matters, not whether AI assisted in creation. AI-generated content that's been reviewed, refined, and adds genuine value performs well.

How much time will AI save our marketing team?

Most businesses report saving 10-15 hours per week on content creation, email management, and reporting combined.

Do we still need a marketing team if we use AI?

Yes — but a smaller, more strategic one. AI handles the production grunt work. Humans provide strategy, brand voice, creative direction, and relationship building.

It depends on your needs. Common tools include ChatGPT/Claude for content, Mailchimp/ActiveCampaign for email, Apollo for lead gen, Ahrefs/Semrush for SEO, and various automation platforms.
