## PRIORITY 1: FIX THE BLEEDING (Week 1) — IMMEDIATE IMPACT

### 1A. Fix Blog Title Template — Expected CTR Lift: 200-500%
**The problem:** Current template wastes ~55 chars on brand prefix. Google displays ~55-60 chars. Users see "Blue Canvas AI | AI Consultancy Northern Ireland | ..." and NOTHING of the actual blog title. That's why you have 100+ impressions and ZERO clicks — the titles are meaningless truncated mush.

**The fix:**
- Change template to: `{Post Title} | Blue Canvas` (brand at END, max 8 chars wasted)
- Or better: `{Post Title}` — no brand at all on blog posts (brand shows via favicon in SERPs anyway)
- Keep homepage as: `AI Consultancy Northern Ireland | Blue Canvas`

**Expected impact:** Studies show truncated titles get 30-60% fewer clicks vs. fully visible titles. Going from truncated gibberish to clear, compelling titles could mean 200-500% CTR lift on blog traffic. Even conservatively, going from 0% CTR to 2-3% CTR on 100+ impressions = actual clicks finally.

**Time to implement:** 30 minutes (Webflow global SEO settings)
**Time to see results:** 1-2 weeks (Google re-crawls, re-renders titles)
**Cost:** £0
