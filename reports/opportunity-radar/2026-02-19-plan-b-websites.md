## 🏆 #1: Heat Pump Installer Lead Gen (THE 9.5)

### Why This Is It
The UK Government's **Future Homes Standard 2026** makes heat pumps effectively **mandatory in all new builds from THIS YEAR**. The £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant is driving retrofit demand through the roof. This market is about to explode and the SEO landscape hasn't caught up yet.

| Keyword | Volume/mo | CPC (£) | Competition |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| heat pump installer | 1,300 | 8.39 | 0.53 |
| heat pump installer near me | 210 | 10.56 | 0.28 |
| heat pump cost uk | 1,900 | 2.80 | 0.88 |
| heat pump grants uk | 480 | 4.99 | 0.85 |
| boiler upgrade scheme | 3,600 | 3.16 | 0.64 |

**Competition 0.28-0.53 = very beatable.** CPC £8-10 shows installers pay serious money per click — a qualified lead is worth £50-100+.

### The Play
- **Model:** Rank & Rent OR lead gen sold per-lead
- **Domain:** `heatpumpinstallers.co.uk` or `heatpumpquotes.co.uk` (check availability)
- **Structure:**
  - Homepage: "Find a Heat Pump Installer Near You"
  - City pages: "Heat Pump Installer [Belfast/Manchester/Leeds/etc]" — 50+ pages
  - Info hub: "Heat Pump Cost UK 2026", "Boiler Upgrade Scheme Guide", "Air vs Ground Source Heat Pumps"
  - Lead capture form: property type, current heating, postcode → quote request
- **Content plan:** 30 city pages + 20 info articles = launched in 2-3 weeks
- **Revenue model:**
  - Per-lead: £30-80 per qualified lead sold to installers
  - Rent: £1,000-2,000/month per city to an installer
  - At scale: 10 cities × £1,500 = **£15,000/month**
- **Time to rank:** 3-6 months for city pages (low comp), faster with some links
- **Sellable?** Absolutely. A heat pump lead gen site doing £10k+/mo sells for 30-40x monthly = **£300,000-400,000** on Empire Flippers

### Why It's The 9.5
- Government FORCING demand (legislation, not trends)
- £7,500 grants mean consumers are actively searching
- Competition is still low (0.28 for "near me"!)
- Average installation: £8,000-15,000 = installers will PAY for leads
- Market will only grow for the next 10+ years
- PJ doesn't need to know anything about heat pumps
