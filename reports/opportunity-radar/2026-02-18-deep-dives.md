## 1. 🏆 BOTOX / AESTHETICS — THE BIG PLAY

### The Thesis
Botox (botulinum toxin) is a **prescription-only medicine (POM)** in the UK. Under ASA/CAP rules, you **cannot advertise POMs to the public** — this includes Google Ads, social media ads, and even before/after photos that imply efficacy. Clinics are effectively locked out of paid advertising. **Organic SEO is their ONLY channel.** This makes a ranked site extraordinarily valuable.

### Google Ads Ban — CONFIRMED ✅
- **UK-specific:** Botox is classified as a POM. Google's healthcare policy restricts ads for prescription drugs in the UK. Clinics cannot run Google Ads mentioning "Botox" directly.
- **Workarounds exist** (advertising "anti-wrinkle injections" or "cosmetic fillers" instead), but these are risky — Google regularly disapproves ads that even link to pages mentioning Botox.
- **In practice:** Very few UK aesthetics clinics successfully run Google Ads. SEO = the game.
- **ASA enforcement is active** — they regularly pull ads, social posts, and even website content that promotes Botox as a product.

### Legal/Compliance — IMPORTANT ⚠️

1. **You CANNOT use the word "Botox" in advertising** — it's a brand name for a POM. However:
   - A website can discuss Botox treatments as **editorial/informational content**
   - You CAN use terms like "anti-wrinkle injections", "wrinkle relaxing injections", "cosmetic injectables"
   - Many existing clinics DO mention Botox on their websites — the ASA primarily targets paid advertising and social media promotions
2. **Before/after photos:** ASA considers these efficacy claims for POMs — **not permitted in advertising**. On a website (not paid advertising), this is a grey area. Best practice: use them sparingly with disclaimers.
3. **Medical disclaimers required:** "Results may vary", practitioner qualifications, consultation recommended
4. **No price promotions for Botox** — offering discounts on POMs is prohibited
5. **Website content vs. advertising:** The ASA's remit covers "advertising" broadly (including websites in some interpretations). Safest approach: focus on "anti-wrinkle treatments" as the primary term, mention Botox for SEO but frame it informationally.

### YMYL Challenge — Critical Issue ⚠️

- Google requires demonstrable **E-E-A-T** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- New sites without real authors, FCA credentials, or industry authority will struggle to rank
- Financial advice content needs real expert attribution
- This is NOT like aesthetics where any local site can compete — Google actively suppresses low-authority financial sites
- **Realistic time to rank: 6-12+ months**, and even then may plateau below established competitors
