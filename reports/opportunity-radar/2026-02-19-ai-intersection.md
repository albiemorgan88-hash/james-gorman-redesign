## WHAT THE 9.5 WOULD NEED TO LOOK LIKE

The 9.5/10 unicorn opportunity would need ALL of these simultaneously:

1. **10,000+ monthly UK searches** — enough to build real traffic fast
2. **Competition score under 0.3** — genuinely beatable SERPs
3. **CPC above £3** — proves commercial intent and monetisation potential
4. **AI creating NEW demand** — not just existing demand that AI happens to touch
5. **Tool-based, not just content** — tools create moat, stickiness, and higher multiples when selling
6. **Not YMYL** — avoids Google's strictest quality filters
7. **Clear affiliate/lead gen path** — someone paying £50+ per lead
8. **Growing 30%+ YoY** — riding a wave, not swimming upstream
9. **No dominant incumbent** — no single site owns the space
10. **PJ can build in a weekend** — leverages his exact skillset

**The closest I found:** The AI Text Tools space (#1 above) hits 7 of 10 criteria. It misses on CPC (too low), established competitors (Originality, GPTZero), and the tool quality bar.

**The gap to 9.5:** We need to find a niche where AI is creating FORCED spending (like GDPR did for privacy) with HIGH commercial value per visitor, LOW competition, and where PJ's speed advantage matters. The EU AI Act compliance space has the forced spending but not the volume yet. The redundancy space has the volume but not the CPC.

**My honest take:** The 9.5 might not exist as a pure SEO play right now. The 9.5 might be a **tool play** that happens to rank — something where the tool itself is the product, SEO brings free traffic, and the tool solves a problem people will pay for. The AI text tools space is the closest to this.

**Wild card suggestion:** What if PJ built a **"Will AI Take My Job?" tool** — input your job title, get an AI-powered risk assessment + personalised career pivot plan + CV rewrite + course recommendations? This combines the redundancy cluster (80K searches) with a tool-based approach, avoids pure YMYL content, and monetises through course/CV affiliate. It doesn't exist yet. It's the intersection of a massive current need and AI creating something new.
