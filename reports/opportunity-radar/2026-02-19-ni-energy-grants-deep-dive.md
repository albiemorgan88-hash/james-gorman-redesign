## 2. Current NI Energy Grants Landscape

### Active Schemes

| Scheme | Provider | Target | What's Covered | Income Limit |
|--------|----------|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **Affordable Warmth Scheme** | Dept for Communities / NIHE | Low-income homeowners | Insulation, heating, boilers | <£20,000 gross |
| **NISEP** (NI Sustainable Energy Programme) | Utility Regulator / Energy Saving Trust | All households + businesses | Insulation, heating, LED, draught-proofing | <£38,000 (for priority) |
| **EECG** (Energy Efficiency Capital Grant) | Invest NI | Businesses | Lighting, heating, motors, renewables | Up to £150,000 |

### Closed Schemes
- **Boiler Replacement Scheme** — closed Sept 2023 (budget constraints). Was up to £1,000 for boilers 15+ years old. Consolidated into Affordable Warmth.
- **RHI (Renewable Heat Incentive)** — the infamous "cash for ash" scheme, now closed

### What's Changing
- Warm Healthy Homes Fund (March 2027) will be the **major new scheme** — £30m/year
- Likely to consolidate/expand existing Affordable Warmth + NISEP
- Revised Decent Homes Standard coming for social housing
- Cross-government approach (Communities, Economy, Health)

### NISEP 2025/26 Sub-Schemes (Currently Live)
Delivered by energy companies (Power NI, SSE Airtricity, Firmus Energy, etc.):
- Efficiency Plus Insulation Fully Funded (Phoenix Energy)
- Various cavity wall, loft insulation, LED schemes
- Income thresholds vary by sub-scheme
- Runs April 2025 - March 2026 or until funds exhausted

1. **Small market** — NI has 750K households. Even with 1 in 4 in fuel poverty (187K target households), the search volumes are modest. Compare to England where similar keywords get 10-50x the volume.

2. **Delayed catalyst** — The £150m fund doesn't start until March 2027. The big "moment" is 13 months away. You'd be building content for a year before the real traffic spike.

3. **Government competition** — For YMYL (Your Money Your Life) content about government grants, Google strongly favours .gov domains. You'll struggle to outrank nidirect.gov.uk for head terms.

4. **Revenue ceiling** — Realistic peak is £2-5K/month. This is a solid side project but not a business-changing opportunity.

5. **Lead gen pricing** — NI installers are mostly small local businesses. They may not pay premium lead prices. The insulation market isn't like solar where a single job is worth £5-10K.

### What IS Good

1. **No independent guide exists** — clear gap in the market
2. **Multiple domains available** — exact-match domains still free
3. **Low competition** — many keywords have zero competition
4. **Growing niche** — fuel poverty + net zero = more schemes coming
5. **Genuinely useful** — real people need help navigating this confusing landscape
6. **Low cost to build** — could have a functional site for £500 and a weekend
7. **Portfolio play** — could be one of several similar sites across UK regions

### The Catch
The maths don't support this being a primary revenue focus. At £2-5K/month realistic, it's a nice earner but PJ's time might be better spent on opportunities with larger addressable markets. The **best play** would be to build this as a **template** and replicate across Scotland, Wales, and English regions — each has its own grant schemes. A network of regional energy grant sites would be much more valuable than NI alone.
