## Technical Build Plan

### Recommended Stack
**Option A: Niceboard** ($49-$149/mo) — Fastest to launch. White-label, payment processing built in, SEO-friendly. Used by Superpath ($111K revenue).

**Option B: JBoard** or **Jobboardly** — Similar SaaS solutions.

**Option C: WordPress + WP Job Manager plugin** — More control, lower cost ($0 + hosting), but more setup time.

**Recommendation: Start with Niceboard.** Get to market fast, validate, then consider custom build if scaling.

### Job Seeding Strategy
1. **Scrape/aggregate** from Indeed, Reed, Totaljobs APIs (most have partner feeds)
2. **RSS feeds** from company career pages
3. **Manual curation** of 200-500 jobs at launch
4. **Google Jobs structured data** to appear in Google job search results
