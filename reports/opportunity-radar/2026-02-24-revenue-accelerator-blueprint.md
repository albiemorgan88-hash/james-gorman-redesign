### Legal/Compliance (UK)

- **GDPR:** Must have lawful basis for processing (consent via forms is fine). Privacy policy required. ICO registration (£40/year). Data Processing Agreements with buyers.
- **PECR:** Can't cold call/email leads yourself. But collecting consent via your own forms and passing to a named third party = compliant with proper consent wording.
- **FCA:** Avoid financial services lead gen entirely. Not worth the compliance burden.
- **Claims management:** Heavily regulated since 2019. Avoid personal injury if selling leads to CMCs.
- **Consumer protection:** Don't misrepresent what you're offering. Comparison sites need clear disclaimers.

**Bottom line:** Green energy lead gen is relatively clean from a compliance perspective. Standard GDPR consent, ICO registration, and clear privacy policies cover you.
