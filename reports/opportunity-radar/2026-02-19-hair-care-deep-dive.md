- Avoid going head-to-head with Allure/Cosmo on head terms
- Men's hair + beard care is less competitive and growing

## 7. Risk Assessment

### 🔴 High Risk
- **Google Helpful Content Update:** AI-rewritten content that's thin or unhelpful could get penalised. Mitigation: ensure genuine expertise signals, add personal testing notes, real photos
- **Domain may be penalised already:** Why is an 11-year-old site with 347 articles being sold for $3,500? This is the #1 red flag. **MUST check the domain in Semrush/Ahrefs before buying** — look for traffic cliff, manual actions, toxic backlinks

### 🟡 Medium Risk
- **Amazon commission cuts:** They've slashed rates before (2020: beauty went from 6% to 3%). Mitigate by diversifying to direct affiliate programs
- **Mediavine threshold:** Need 50k sessions/month. Could take 6-12 months to reach. Use Ezoic or Journey in the meantime
- **Competition from AI overviews:** Google increasingly showing AI answers, reducing clicks to content sites

### 🟢 Low Risk
- **Hair care is evergreen:** People always need hair products. Not a fad niche
- **Multiple monetisation paths:** Not dependent on any single revenue stream
- **Low ongoing costs:** Hosting £100-200/year, content creation via Albie = near-zero marginal cost
