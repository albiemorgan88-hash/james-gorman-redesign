## 1. Real Income Reports: What's Actually Making Money NOW

### The Harsh Reality of 2024-2026
The niche site landscape has fundamentally shifted. Key findings:

- **HCU (Helpful Content Update) devastation**: Skipblast's December 2024 report shows 5 out of 7 portfolio sites were either de-indexed or destroyed by Google's March 2024 HCU. Only 1 education site survived, earning **~$165/month** from Ezoic ads. This is a portfolio that previously earned thousands.
- **Fat Stacks Blog** (Jon Dykstra): Once reported $55K/month across 7 niche sites (Forbes, Dec 2019). Has stopped publishing income reports — a telling sign.
- **Increasing.com**: Peaked at $79K/month (Nov 2022) but hasn't published reports since early 2023.
- **Average affiliate marketer**: ~$8,000/month per Whop.com stats, but median is likely **$35-40K/year** (OptinMonster) — pulled up by outliers.

## 3. Portfolio Strategy Recommendation

### The Math: 10 Micro-Sites vs 1 Big Purchase

**Option A: Buy one site for £10-20K**
- Typical: 30-36x monthly revenue multiple
- Gets you: a site earning £300-600/month
- Risk: Single point of failure (one algo update = wiped out, as Skipblast proved)
- Timeline: Immediate income, but HCU risk

**Option B: Build 10 micro-sites from scratch (£0 purchase cost)**
- Cost: ~£100-200 total (domains + hosting)
- Timeline: Build each in a weekend (AI-assisted), 4-8 weeks to start ranking
- Target: £200-500/month each = **£2,000-5,000/month total**
- Risk: Diversified — if 3 fail, 7 still earn
- Exit value: At 30x multiple, portfolio worth **£60K-150K** in 12-18 months

## 4. Buy vs Build: The Verdict

| Factor | BUY (Marketplace) | BUILD (From Scratch) |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Upfront Cost** | £10-50K | £100-200 |
| **Time to Income** | Immediate | 4-8 weeks |

| **Income Ceiling** | Limited by what you bought | Unlimited (add more sites) |
| **Exit Multiple** | Already priced in | Build equity from £0 |
| **AI Advantage** | Minimal (site already built) | MASSIVE (build 10x faster than competitors) |
| **Learning** | Less (just operating) | More (full SEO + content skills) |
| **HCU Risk** | HIGH (existing sites may already be flagged) | LOW (fresh domains, original content) |

### 🏆 RECOMMENDATION: BUILD, DON'T BUY

**Why:**
1. **The HCU changed everything.** Buying existing content sites is now extremely risky. Skipblast lost 5 of 7 sites. Many marketplace listings are sellers dumping HCU-hit sites before the damage shows.
2. **AI is PJ's unfair advantage.** He can produce a 50-article site in a weekend. Buying a site for £15K when you can build 10 better ones for £200 makes no financial sense.
3. **Diversification wins.** 10 small sites earning £200-500 each is more resilient than one site earning £3K.
4. **Fresh domains with original content** are what Google wants post-HCU. Old sites with AI-generated or thin content are being systematically penalised.
5. **Exit value is created, not bought.** Build 10 sites, grow them for 12 months, sell the portfolio for £60-150K. That's a better ROI than any marketplace purchase.

### The Exception
**DO buy if:** You find a site with genuine E-E-A-T (real author, original photos, community engagement) in a boring niche, priced under 24x monthly revenue, with diversified traffic sources. That's rare on marketplaces but worth grabbing.
