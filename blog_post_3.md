## What is OpenClaw (And Why UK Businesses Are Adopting It Rapidly)?

OpenClaw is an open-source AI agent platform that creates personalised AI assistants capable of handling complex business tasks autonomously.

Unlike simple chatbots or basic automation tools, OpenClaw agents can:

- **Read and respond to emails** with context and nuance
- **Manage complex workflows** across multiple systems
- **Make decisions** based on your business rules and preferences
- **Learn from interactions** to improve over time
- **Integrate with existing tools** (CRM, email, calendars, databases)
- **Handle multiple communication channels** (Slack, WhatsApp, Teams, email)

**Why UK Businesses Choose OpenClaw:**

**Customisation:** Complete control over AI behaviour, workflows, and integrations specific to UK business requirements.

**Cost Effectiveness:** No per-seat licensing or usage-based pricing. Scale your AI capability without increasing monthly costs.

**Integration Freedom:** Works with any system that has an API, including UK-specific tools like Xero, Sage, and HMRC systems.
