## Content Guidelines
1. No generic AI thought leadership — ground in Blue Canvas experience
2. Location signals naturally (Derry, Belfast, NI, UK)
3. Internal linking (every post → 2+ other pages)
4. CTA on every post (book free 15-min consultation)
5. Write for humans first — Phil's voice is direct and practical
6. 1,200-2,000 words per post
7. Real data and examples

## What NOT to Write
- "Best AI tools for [year]" — dominated by authority sites
- Generic listicles — zero ranking chance
- Any topic where page 1 is all DR 70+ sites
