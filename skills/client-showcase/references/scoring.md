# SEO Scoring Methodology

Score out of 100. Deduct points for each issue found.

## Scoring Breakdown

| Category | Max Points | Items |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Title tags | 10 | Unique, descriptive, <60 chars, no duplicates/broken |
| Meta descriptions | 10 | Present, compelling, <160 chars, unique per page |
| Schema/structured data | 15 | LocalBusiness, Organization, breadcrumbs, FAQ |
| Technical health | 15 | No 404s, proper redirects, sitemap, robots.txt |
| Content quality | 15 | 300+ words on key pages, unique, keyword-relevant |
| Local SEO | 10 | Location pages, NAP consistency, Google Business |
| Open Graph/social | 5 | OG tags, Twitter cards, social sharing |
| Page speed | 10 | Load time <3s, no render-blocking, optimised images |
| Mobile | 5 | Viewport meta, responsive, tap targets |
| SSL/security | 5 | HTTPS, no mixed content, no exposed versions |

## Score Ranges

- **80-100:** Strong foundation, minor optimisations needed
- **60-79:** Decent but missing key elements
- **40-59:** Significant issues hurting rankings
- **20-39:** Major problems, likely invisible on Google
- **0-19:** Critical — site barely functional for SEO

## Presentation

- Use animated SVG ring that fills to the score
- Red (<40), Orange (40-59), Yellow (60-79), Green (80+)
- Show score climbing in the "after" slide (typically +30-45 points)
